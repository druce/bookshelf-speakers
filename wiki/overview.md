# Overview

**State as of 2026-10-11:** initial build complete. Discover screened **375** models. **141** are in scope (passive, rated 6 or 8 Ω, bookshelf/standmount, sold new in the US). **89** of those have Tier A/B measurements and a speaker page; **52** are in scope but unmeasured. See [candidate-universe](comparisons/candidate-universe.md), with rankings in [ranked-by-price-band](comparisons/ranked-by-price-band.md).

## Thesis so far
1. **Measured accuracy is cheap; amplifier-friendliness is not.** With a subwoofer, the $468 [Ascend Acoustics CBM-170 SE2](speakers/ascend-acoustics-cbm-170-se2.md) predicts within 0.5 preference points of every band leader up to $4,100 ([ranked-by-price-band](comparisons/ranked-by-price-band.md)). Above about $500 a pair, money buys bass extension, output, finish and an easier load, not predicted tonal preference.
2. **"8 Ω" is mostly marketing.** Of the 83 current speakers with a measured minimum, 66 fall below the IEC floor for their rating and 30 dip under 4 Ω ([impedance-honesty](comparisons/impedance-honesty.md)). Among the 21 best-scoring models, only 3 clear the floor: [Elac Debut 2.0 B5.2](speakers/elac-debut-2-0-b5-2.md), [Ascend Acoustics Sierra-1 V2](speakers/ascend-acoustics-sierra-1-v2.md) and [MoFi SourcePoint 8](speakers/mofi-sourcepoint-8.md). Treat nearly every shortlisted speaker as a 4 Ω-class load when choosing an amplifier ([impedance-and-epdr](concepts/impedance-and-epdr.md)).
3. **Sensitivity claims run about 2 dB optimistic.** Across 85 speakers the median measured shortfall is −2.0 dB, and many miss by 3 dB or more ([sensitivity-claims-vs-measured](comparisons/sensitivity-claims-vs-measured.md)). Budget amplifier power from measured figures.
4. **Bass is where bookshelf speakers differ most.** Most Group A models have anechoic f3 of 52–77 Hz. Only [March Audio Sointuva AWG](speakers/march-audio-sointuva-awg.md) (37 Hz) and [Buchardt Audio E50](speakers/buchardt-audio-e50.md) (42 Hz) are "excellent" by Claude.md §7. Plan on a subwoofer for anything with f3 above 60 Hz ([bass-extension-vs-room-gain](concepts/bass-extension-vs-room-gain.md)).

## Open questions (need the human)
- **Buchardt E50** is rated "4/6 Ω" by its maker. It is included as 6 Ω, but should it be in scope?
- **Revel Performa3 succession:** on 2026-10-11 Revel's US site lists a new Performa4 series (M145, M146, marked "New") alongside the Performa3 M105/M106 pages, which show no price. The M105 and M106 are probably being superseded. Confirm before buying, and measure the M145/M146 when data appears.
- **Revel Concerta2/Performa3 status:** Revel lists them, but Audio Advice shows sold-out or "Discontinued" labels. Confirm with a dealer before relying on [Revel M16](speakers/revel-m16.md), [Revel M105](speakers/revel-m105.md) or [Revel M106](speakers/revel-m106.md).
- **"8 Ω compatible" ratings:** Polk, Klipsch, Wharfedale and Paradigm give no explicit nominal. They are scoped in as 8 Ω (rule in Claude.md §2 as applied in ingest). Keep that, or treat them as unrated?
- **Borderline form factors:** [Focal Dome Flax](speakers/focal-dome-flax.md) (satellite), [Human Speakers 81 dk](speakers/human-speakers-81-dk.md) (470 mm tall) and [Role Audio Skiff](speakers/role-audio-skiff.md) (desktop full-range) were kept in. Cambridge Minx Min12 was excluded.
- **Graph-read values:** about a third of the minimum-impedance and THD figures are read from graphs. The [Salk WoW1](speakers/salk-wow1.md) minimum (3.3 Ω graph-read vs ASR's 6.5 Ω label) needs a human look.

## Data gaps
- **Owner reviews:** 12 models now have Crutchfield owner-review tallies (36–337 reviews each, hand-coded with counts and QC notes; see each speaker's User reviews section). Reddit is blocked by the browser extension's safety restrictions. AVS Forum searches returned only classifieds and older-model threads, so direct-sold models (Ascend, March Audio, Buchardt, Neumi) still lack ≥3 independent owner reports.
- **23 in-scope speakers have no preference score.** They are measured only by NRC graphs or Tier B rigs: B&W 705 S3 and 805 D4 lines, Focal Kanta/Sopra/Theva, ATC SCM7/SCM19, Spendor Classic 3/1, Totem Bison/Signature One and others. See the list in [ranked-by-price-band](comparisons/ranked-by-price-band.md).
- **52 in-scope models have no Tier A/B data.** Several are popular: B&W 606 S3 and 607 S3, Elac Debut 3.0 B5.3, Wharfedale Diamond 12.1i, Dali Sonik 1/3, Q Acoustics 3010i/3020c, Fyne F500S and Dynaudio Emit 10/Evoke. Measurements of a predecessor (Diamond 12.1, SS-CS5, Emit M10) are not carried over.
- **Max SPL** is not comparable across sources (ASR draft compression charts vs EAC's 102 dB test ceiling), so it is not used for ranking ([compression-and-max-spl](concepts/compression-and-max-spl.md)).
