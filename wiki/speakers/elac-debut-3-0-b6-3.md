## Summary
The Debut 3.0 DB63 is a 6 Ω, 6.5" two-way with a 1" aluminium dome and a front port, at about $499 per pair. Erin's Audio Corner Klippel data show a minimum impedance of 4.4 Ω (below the IEC floor), low distortion for the class at 86 dB, and mild compression. Frequency-response and directivity metrics could not be computed (no raw Klippel export in the repo), so f3 and sensitivity here are graph-read approximations. Suits small-to-medium rooms; a competent amplifier is advisable.

## Output & distortion
THD at 86 dB is about 0.6% (spike near 155 Hz to about 1.1%) and at 96 dB about 1.9% (graph-read), both under the 3% mark. Compression referenced to 76 dB is about -0.3 dB mid-band at 96 dB and about -1.2 dB near 150-200 Hz at 102 dB, with -1.7 dB near 45 Hz. No stated max SPL. Reasonable for small-to-medium rooms; see [compression-and-max-spl](../concepts/compression-and-max-spl.md).

## Amplifier load
6 Ω nominal, minimum 4.4 Ω above 80 Hz, below the IEC floor of 4.8 Ω, so flagged `below-iec` (not below 4 Ω). Minimum EPDR 2.2 Ω is demanding; use an amplifier comfortable with 4 Ω loads. See [impedance-and-epdr](../concepts/impedance-and-epdr.md).
