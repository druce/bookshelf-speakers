## Summary
The Triangle Borea BR03 is a 2-way, front-ported standmount with a 25 mm silk dome behind a diffuser and a 16 cm paper midwoofer, rated 8 Ω nominal and 90 dB/W/m. On Klippel measurements it is only moderately flat (listening window ±2.3 dB EAC, ±2.1 dB ASR) with port and crossover resonances between 500 Hz and 4 kHz, f3 about 57 Hz and sensitivity about 87 dB, well under the claim. Impedance falls to 4.2 Ω with an EPDR near 2.2 Ω, so it is not a true 8 Ω load. Pro reviewers like its scale and refinement; both measurement reviewers were unconvinced. It suits buyers wanting value and scale who can place it carefully and tolerate some treble forwardness.

## Directivity
Horizontal -6 dB beamwidth about 130° (EAC) or 125° (ASR); vertical about 106°. DI has a minor discontinuity (ERDI residual 1.3 dB EAC / 2.0 dB ASR). The early-reflection dip near 1.5 kHz is 2.2 / 2.1 dB, below the 3 dB flag. SPDI rises from about 5.5 dB at 1 kHz to 9.4 dB at 10 kHz (EAC). Erin notes directivity irregularities near 1.2 kHz (port) and about 4 kHz (crossover); ASR says the front port resonance adds energy below axis and that the tweeter should sit near ear level. On-axis minus listening window above 4 kHz is +0.7 / +0.5 dB, so either aim works, with a small toe-in likely helping; ASR advises carpet against floor bounce. See [directivity-index](../concepts/directivity-index.md), [early-reflections](../concepts/early-reflections.md).

## Output & distortion
EAC measured around 2.2% THD at 86 dB and about 8% at 96 dB (graph-read), mostly second harmonic with strong low-frequency distortion; Erin calls distortion high. Compression stays under 3 dB through 102 dB @1 m, worst about 2.8 dB near 55 Hz, with a compression/distortion feature near 3 kHz. ASR's distortion at 86 dB is lower (about 0.8%) and ASR notes the speaker plays loud without bottoming; the two rigs differ in how they present THD. Practical: fine for small-to-medium rooms at moderate levels; bass limits dominate loud use. See [compression-and-max-spl](../concepts/compression-and-max-spl.md).

## Amplifier load
Rated 8 Ω nominal, but measured minimum 4.2 Ω (both reviewers and the maker), under the IEC 80% floor of 6.4 Ω, so flagged `below-iec`; it is above the 4.0 Ω effectively-4-ohm cutoff. EPDR falls to 2.2 Ω, and one ASR forum member argues the IEC rule would rate it 6 Ω. Choose an amp stable into 4 Ω; poor match for low-power tube amps. See [impedance-and-epdr](../concepts/impedance-and-epdr.md).

## Critic reviews
- [whathifi-triangle-borea-br03](../sources/whathifi-triangle-borea-br03.md) (What Hi-Fi?, 5 stars sound): sophisticated, detailed sound with big scale; slight treble peakiness.
- [toolsandtoys-triangle-borea-br03](../sources/toolsandtoys-triangle-borea-br03.md) (Tools and Toys): natural, clean, bass deep enough to skip a sub; affiliate write-up.
- [asr-triangle-borea-br03](../sources/asr-triangle-borea-br03.md) (ASR, Tier D notes): warm start, grungy mids, bright highs, slightly boomy; not recommended.
- [eac-triangle-borea-br03](../sources/eac-triangle-borea-br03.md) (Erin, Tier D notes): lacks midrange, overpronounced 4-8 kHz; "okay, not good".

## User reviews
About 10 reports ([users-triangle-borea-br03](../sources/users-triangle-borea-br03.md)): 7 positive on sound and value; 3 of 4 ASR-thread listeners found it bright, forward or non-neutral. No QC or reliability problems reported. Maker-site reviews skew positive.

## Conflicts
- Sensitivity: measured 87.0-87.5 dB vs claimed 90 dB (delta about 3 dB).
- Distortion: EAC about 2.2% / 8% (86 / 96 dB) vs ASR about 0.8% / 2.2%; graph-read, different rigs and plots.
- Pro review (What Hi-Fi?) finds treble well-behaved; ASR and EAC find lower-treble and midrange irregularities.
- Pref score 4.0 (EAC) vs 4.3 (ASR): within noise.
- Price: $599 on the maker's site; other US retailers reportedly $399-559 (unverified).
