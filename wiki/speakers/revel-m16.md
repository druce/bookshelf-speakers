## Summary
The Revel Concerta2 M16 is a 6 Ω, two-way, 6.5" ported standmount with a 1" aluminium dome on a waveguide. It measures very well on the Klippel (listening window ±1.4 dB, minor DI discontinuity, good constant-directivity behaviour) with a bass hump near 100 Hz and top-octave roll-off. Bass extension is modest (f3 57 Hz) and impedance dips to about 4 Ω, so it wants a competent amplifier. Suits listeners who want a Harman-style neutral, wide-dispersion speaker at around $700-1,000 per pair. Price/availability need checking: one retailer shows it sold out.

## Directivity
Horizontal -6 dB beamwidth about 122°, vertical about 95°. DI is nearly constant from about 1.8 to 12 kHz (ERDI residual 1.5 dB, minor discontinuity); SPDI rises from 5.5 dB at 1 kHz to 7.5 dB at 10 kHz. Early-reflection dip near 1.5 kHz is 2.2 dB, under the 3 dB flag. ASR notes a vertical dip around crossover at extreme angles. On-axis minus listening window above 4 kHz is +0.6 dB, so either aim works; ASR suggests less toe-in softens a bump near 5 kHz. See [directivity-index](../concepts/directivity-index.md), [early-reflections](../concepts/early-reflections.md).

## Output & distortion
No max-SPL or compression test exists. ASR's Klippel plot (level not stated, so not entered as THD at 86/96 dB) shows THD of about 1.8% near 70-80 Hz, a narrow ~1.95% spike near 320 Hz, and about 1.1% rising toward the crossover near 1.9 kHz; several ASR members criticised this. Audioholics found it composed at high levels, congested only at extreme volume (Tier D). Likely fine for small-to-medium rooms at moderate levels; not for loud listening in large rooms. See [compression-and-max-spl](../concepts/compression-and-max-spl.md).

## Amplifier load
6 Ω nominal. ASR measured about 4 Ω minimum, below the IEC 80% floor of 4.8 Ω, so flagged `below-iec`; Sound & Vision measured 5.05 Ω. Phase swings occur at high impedance (Audioholics). EPDR not computed. Use a stable amp rated for 4 Ω loads; Revel recommends 50-120 W. See [impedance-and-epdr](../concepts/impedance-and-epdr.md).

## Critic reviews
- [audioholics-revel-m16](../sources/audioholics-revel-m16.md) (Audioholics, 4.5/5 performance and value): superlative imaging, balanced natural sound, flat for the price.
- [whathifi-revel-m16](../sources/whathifi-revel-m16.md) (What Hi-Fi?): powerful, tuneful bass and dynamics; edgy if partnered with bright gear.
- [hifireport-revel-m16](../sources/hifireport-revel-m16.md) (HiFiReport): smooth non-metallic treble, wide stage, bass exemplary for price.
- [asr-revel-m16](../sources/asr-revel-m16.md) (ASR, Tier D listening notes): neutral vocals and clear highs, bass somewhat much, max SPL limited; top recommendation.

## User reviews
About 3 owner comments found, so anecdotal ([users-revel-m16](../sources/users-revel-m16.md)): 2 mention less body at low volume or need for clean power; 1 finds it slightly warm/dark. No QC problems reported.

## Conflicts
- Minimum impedance: ~4.1 Ω (ASR, Tier A, graph-read) vs 5.05 Ω (Sound & Vision, Tier B); difference >0.5 Ω. Flag follows the Tier-A value.
- Listening-window flatness: ±1.4 dB (ASR) vs +0.73/-2.33 dB (S&V); different band and averaging, not a true disagreement.
- Preference score: 5.06 here vs 5.5/7.7 posted by a forum member in the ASR thread; used the spinorama.org computed figure.
- Sensitivity: 85.0 (computed) vs 85.5 measured by two Tier-B sources vs 86 claimed; within 1 dB.
- ASR notes top-octave roll-off not seen in Harman's own plot.
- Price/status: Revel US lists it as current; Audio Advice shows sold out with a "Discontinued" label; Crutchfield listing not verifiable live.
