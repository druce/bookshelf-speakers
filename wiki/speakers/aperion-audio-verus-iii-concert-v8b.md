## Summary
The Verus III Concert V8B is a large 6 Ω two-way standmount with an 8" Kevlar woofer and 1.1" silk dome, sold direct for $1,549 per pair. Erin's Klippel data show a listening window of ±2.1 dB with a 100-500 Hz scoop, treble resonances near 4 and 10 kHz, a major DI discontinuity and a low preference score. Bass extends to f3 54 Hz, distortion is low and the load is easy for a 6 Ω speaker. Suits those who want size, wide dispersion and output and accept (or EQ) the tonal flaws; it is at the large end of the bookshelf scope (433 mm tall, 12.7 kg each).

## Directivity
Horizontal -6 dB beamwidth about 122°, vertical about 118° (wide in both planes). DI shows a major discontinuity (ERDI residual 2.3 dB), consistent with Erin's note of imbalance. SPDI rises from 5.8 dB at 1 kHz to 10.6 dB at 10 kHz. Early-reflection dip near 1.5 kHz is 2.5 dB, below the 3 dB flag. On-axis minus listening window above 4 kHz is +0.7 dB, so either aim works; the -3 dB treble jumper can tame brightness. See [directivity-index](../concepts/directivity-index.md), [early-reflections](../concepts/early-reflections.md).

## Output & distortion
Erin's THD is about 1.2% at 86 dB and about 2.2% at 96 dB (graph-read), mostly below 3% across the band; he singles out low bass distortion. Compression at 102 dB @1 m is about 1.1 dB at most, so 102 dB is a lower bound for usable output (no 3 dB compression reached). HomeTheaterHifi measured 0.17% at 1 kHz and 85 dB. Capable of loud listening in medium-to-large rooms. See [compression-and-max-spl](../concepts/compression-and-max-spl.md).

## Amplifier load
6 Ω nominal. Erin shows 5.7 Ω minimum above 80 Hz (above the 4.8 Ω IEC floor) and minimum EPDR 3.0 Ω; impedance is high through the midrange (up to about 20 Ω). Phase is moderate. Maker recommends 20-150 W; any competent amplifier is fine. HomeTheaterHifi's 4.5 Ω minimum is a conflict, see below. See [impedance-and-epdr](../concepts/impedance-and-epdr.md).
