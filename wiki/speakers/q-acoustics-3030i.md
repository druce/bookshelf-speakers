## Summary
The 3030i is the largest 3000i-series standmount: a 6 Ω, 6.5" ported two-way with a 22 mm dome tweeter and 12.5 L cabinet, listed at $499.96 per pair on the maker's US site (What Hi-Fi? says the 3000c series has replaced it). The only measurement is a quasi-anechoic hobbyist spinorama (Tier B) showing neutral listening-window tonality, a resonance near 1.2 kHz and wide directivity to about 6 kHz; no impedance, distortion or Klippel data exist. Graph-read bass extension is about 72 Hz (f3), better than the 3020i but short of the 46 Hz claim. It suits small to medium rooms on a budget.

## Directivity
Author describes wide directivity up to about 6 kHz, narrowing above; flattest horizontal response about 15° off-axis; vertically he advises listening on-axis or not far above the tweeter. Beamwidth numbers, ERDI and SPDI were not computed from the digitised data. Aim about 15° off-axis for the flattest horizontal response. See [directivity-index](../concepts/directivity-index.md), [early-reflections](../concepts/early-reflections.md).

## Output & distortion
No distortion, compression or max-SPL data. What Hi-Fi? says it is composed at high volume for the price (Tier D). See [compression-and-max-spl](../concepts/compression-and-max-spl.md).
