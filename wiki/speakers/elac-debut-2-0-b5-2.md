## Summary
The Debut 2.0 B5.2 is a 6 Ω, 5.25" two-way with a 1" cloth dome on a waveguide and a front port, at about $294 per pair. On Erin's Klippel data it has a listening window of ±2.2 dB with a +3 dB step near 1.5 kHz, modest bass (f3 62 Hz) and only 83.3 dB sensitivity. Compression and midrange distortion rise early. It suits a small room with a subwoofer and ideally EQ; Erin would pick the [[elac-debut-2-0-b6-2]] instead if not using EQ. What Hi-Fi? says the Debut 3.0 DB53 replaces it, but it remains in stock at a US retailer.

## Directivity
Horizontal -6 dB beamwidth about 128°, vertical about 87°. DI is minor-discontinuity (ERDI residual 1.4 dB); SPDI rises from 5.5 dB at 1 kHz to 9.0 dB at 10 kHz. Early-reflection dip near 1.5 kHz is 2.1 dB, under the 3 dB flag. On-axis minus listening window above 4 kHz is +0.9 dB, so either aim works; Erin notes good horizontal directivity lets a single high-shelf fix the 1.5 kHz step. See [[directivity-index]], [[early-reflections]].

## Output & distortion
Erin's tests show THD of about 1.8% at 86 dB and about 4.2% at 96 dB (graph-read), above the 3% mark at 96 dB. Compression referenced to 76 dB reaches about -1.5 dB near 700 Hz at 96 dB and about -2.6 dB at 102 dB, with steep loss below roughly 60 Hz. Erin calls it output-limited. No max SPL stated. Suitable for small rooms at moderate levels; use a sub and high-pass for louder listening. See [[compression-and-max-spl]].

## Amplifier load
6 Ω nominal; minimum 5.2 Ω above 80 Hz, comfortably above the IEC floor of 4.8 Ω, so no flag. Minimum EPDR 2.6 Ω above 80 Hz is moderately demanding with low sensitivity; a competent amp is advisable. Phase stays within about ±45°. See [[impedance-and-epdr]].

## Critic reviews
- [[whathifi-elac-debut-2-0-b5-2]] (What Hi-Fi?, 5/5): detailed, cohesive, well timed; leans analytical rather than warm; less forgiving of partnering gear.
- [[eac-elac-debut-2-0-b5-2]] (Erin's Audio Corner, Tier D notes): uneven sound from the 1.5 kHz step, which he could not pin down by ear; better with EQ.

## User reviews
Only 2 independent owner reports, so anecdotal ([[users-elac-debut-2-0-b5-2]]): bass adequate for size or strong without boom; slight treble grain or clinical tone. No QC problems reported.

## Conflicts
- Sensitivity: 83.3 dB measured versus 86 dB claimed ([[mfr-elac-debut-2-0-b5-2]]), a 2.7 dB shortfall.
- Tone: What Hi-Fi? praises it as balanced and even-handed; Erin's data show a +3 dB step near 1.5 kHz and he found it uneven. Different methods; unresolved.
- Status: What Hi-Fi? says the DB53 replaces it; elac.com still lists it and World Wide Stereo has it in stock, so kept as current.
