## Summary
The PSB Alpha P5 is an 8 Ω-rated, two-way 5.25" ported standmount (290 mm tall) with an inverted layout (tweeter below the woofer), about $549 per pair at US retailers. ASR's Klippel data give a listening-window deviation of ±2.8 dB (on-axis ±3.1 dB), f3 of 56 Hz, a prominent rear-port peak, a major DI discontinuity and high distortion. Stereophile's quasi-anechoic data show a more even woofer-axis response and it won critical praise. Impedance bottoms at about 3.9 Ω, and sensitivity is average-to-low (83.6-85 dB). Suits small rooms and moderate levels.

## Directivity
Horizontal -6 dB beamwidth about 132°, vertical about 106°. DI is classed major-discontinuity (ERDI residual 2.2 dB): ASR says width increases in the crossover region from woofer/tweeter beam mismatch near 3 kHz. SPDI rises from 5.2 dB at 1 kHz to 7.8 dB at 10 kHz. Early-reflection dip near 1.5 kHz is 1.0 dB. Stereophile found a vertical suck-out 10° above and 15° below the woofer axis; ASR advises keeping ears above the (lower) tweeter. On-axis minus listening window above 4 kHz is +0.7 dB, so either aim works. See [directivity-index](../concepts/directivity-index.md), [early-reflections](../concepts/early-reflections.md).

## Output & distortion
ASR's Klippel plot shows about 2.7 % THD near 120-150 Hz even at 86 dB (graph-read), and over 5 % across 80 Hz-1.5 kHz at 96 dB; ASR called it "stunningly bad" and the woofer bottomed on a deep-bass track. ASR's 200 Hz compression sweep shows only about 0.5 dB compression to roughly 105 dB, so the single-tone sweep is benign. Stereophile heard clarity best below about 90 dBC at the listening seat (Tier D). Fit for small rooms at moderate levels. See [compression-and-max-spl](../concepts/compression-and-max-spl.md).

## Amplifier load
8 Ω nominal, 4 Ω minimum. Measured about 3.9 Ω (ASR) and 3.85 Ω (Stereophile) at about 190 Hz, so `effectively-4ohm`; average impedance is near 8 Ω and Stereophile calls it "not particularly difficult" with phase -43° at 105 Hz where magnitude is 8.3 Ω. Maker recommends 10-90 W. EPDR not computed. See [impedance-and-epdr](../concepts/impedance-and-epdr.md).

## Critic reviews
- [stereophile-psb-alpha-p5](../sources/stereophile-psb-alpha-p5.md) (Stereophile, John Atkinson, 2019): outclassed by dearer speakers "not by a wide margin"; voices uncoloured, bass full to 63 Hz.
- [tas-psb-alpha-p5](../sources/tas-psb-alpha-p5.md) (The Absolute Sound, Neil Gader, 2020): natural, non-fatiguing, tuneful bass for size; congestion if pushed.
- [asr-psb-alpha-p5](../sources/asr-psb-alpha-p5.md) (ASR, Tier D listening): warm females, lost male vocals, bottoming woofer; not recommended.

## Conflicts
- Sensitivity: 83.6 dB (ASR computed) vs 85 dB (Stereophile) vs 87 dB claimed; claimed figure is 3.4 dB above Tier A.
- Response quality: Stereophile calls the woofer-axis response "superbly flat and even"; ASR's tweeter-axis Klippel data score ±2.8 dB LW with a port peak. Different reference axis (woofer vs tweeter) and method; both agree on the 3-4 kHz notch.
- Min impedance agrees (3.9 vs 3.85 Ω).
