## Summary
The Monitor XT15 is Polk's smallest Monitor XT model: a 5.25" two-way with a rear port and 1" Terylene dome, now $149 per pair on Polk US. Polk rates it only as compatible with 4- and 8-ohm outputs; it is recorded as 8 Ω compatible, but measured minimum impedance is about 4.0 Ω. Erin's Klippel data show a good listening window (±1.9 dB), minor directivity discontinuity and clean distortion, but low sensitivity (84.4 dB) and limited bass (f3 77 Hz). A useful budget surround or small-room speaker that wants a subwoofer.

## Directivity
Horizontal -6 dB beamwidth about 127°, vertical about 101°. ERDI residual 1.4 dB (minor discontinuity); SPDI rises from 4.3 dB at 1 kHz to 8.8 dB at 10 kHz. Early-reflection dip near 1.5 kHz is 2.1 dB, under the 3 dB flag. On-axis minus listening window above 4 kHz is +0.8 dB, so either aim works. See [directivity-index](../concepts/directivity-index.md), [early-reflections](../concepts/early-reflections.md).

## Output & distortion
THD at 86 dB is about 0.5 % above 100 Hz and at 96 dB about 2.5 % at 150-190 Hz (graph-read). Erin's instant-compression test shows up to about 2.4 dB loss at 102 dB near 70 and 180 Hz, under 1 dB above 700 Hz and no 3 dB point above 50 Hz. With only 84.4 dB sensitivity, reaching 102 dB at 1 m needs roughly 60 W (8 Ω equivalent); suited to small rooms and moderate levels. See [compression-and-max-spl](../concepts/compression-and-max-spl.md).

## Amplifier load
Impedance dips to about 4.0 Ω near 200-300 Hz with peaks near 24 Ω (35 Hz) and 30 Ω (100 Hz); phase stays moderate. Below the IEC 6.4 Ω floor, so `below-iec` (just above the 4 Ω threshold). EPDR not computed. Any competent receiver should cope at modest levels. See [impedance-and-epdr](../concepts/impedance-and-epdr.md).

## Critic reviews
- [tntaudio-polk-audio-xt15](../sources/tntaudio-polk-audio-xt15.md) (TNT-Audio): open, articulate, crisp mid-high with deep, forceful bass for the size; slightly light in lower vocals, narrow soundstage; preferred over the Eltax Monitor 1.
- [eac-polk-audio-xt15](../sources/eac-polk-audio-xt15.md): Erin gave no subjective evaluation in the written page.

## User reviews
Crutchfield ([crutchfield-polk-audio-xt15](../sources/crutchfield-polk-audio-xt15.md); 99 reviews, 89 five-star, hand-tallied): value (about $150/pair) dominates; about 31 of 99 praise clear mids and highs; 9 of 99 say bass is light or use a sub, while 7 of 99 found it better than expected for the size; 4 of 99 say it plays loud without strain. The light-bass reports agree with the measured f3 of 77 Hz.

QC/reliability: no defects or failures in 99 reviews (1 found the angled binding posts awkward).

## Conflicts
- Sensitivity: 84.4 dB measured vs 86 dB claimed (reference unstated, 1 W per Erin); difference of 1.6 dB.
- Bass: f3 77 Hz and f6 64 Hz measured vs 48 Hz claimed FR with no tolerance; TNT-Audio cites a manual ±3 dB figure of 73 Hz, closer to the measured value.
- Impedance: Polk gives 4/8-ohm compatibility only; measured about 4.0 Ω (manual 4.1 Ω per TNT-Audio).
- TNT-Audio praised "deep" bass for the size, which is relative; the measured f3 is 77 Hz.
- Price: Erin quoted about $200/pair in 2022; Polk US now lists $149 (reduced from $179).
