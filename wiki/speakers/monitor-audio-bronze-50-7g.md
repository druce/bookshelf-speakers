## Summary
The Bronze 50 7G is Monitor Audio's entry two-way standmount: a 6" C-CAM woofer, 1" gold C-CAM dome on a UD II waveguide, rear HiVe II port and a 3 kHz crossover, listed at $849 per pair. Its only Tier-B data (Hi-Fi News) show a flat forward response, sensitivity above spec and a tougher-than-rated load (4.18 Ω minimum, EPDR 2.05 Ω). No Klippel/spinorama data exists that I could find, so directivity, f3 and listening-window figures are unrecorded. Press reviews are strongly positive (What Hi-Fi? 5 stars). Needs an amplifier comfortable with a 4 Ω-class load.

## Directivity
No spinorama or beamwidth data. Hi-Fi News reports a flatter response with 15° toe-in and recommends the speakers cross in front of or behind the listener; its listening review notes excellent off-axis tweeter uniformity from the UD II waveguide (Tier D). Take the grille off: it causes a 3.5 dB disruption in the presence band. See [directivity-index](../concepts/directivity-index.md), [early-reflections](../concepts/early-reflections.md).

## Output & distortion
No distortion, compression or max-SPL measurement found. Maker claims 112 dB peak SPL (retailer-reported, claimed only). What Hi-Fi? says it plays happily at higher volumes (Tier D). See [compression-and-max-spl](../concepts/compression-and-max-spl.md).

## Amplifier load
Rated 8 Ω, but Hi-Fi News measured 4.18 Ω minimum at 200 Hz and EPDR down to 2.05 Ω at 125 Hz with ±45° phase swings in the bass, so flagged `below-iec` (above the 4 Ω effectively-4-ohm line). HFN suggests a capable budget integrated amplifier; What Hi-Fi? found no compatibility problems with its amps. See [impedance-and-epdr](../concepts/impedance-and-epdr.md).
