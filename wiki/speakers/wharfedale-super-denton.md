## Summary
The Wharfedale Super Denton is a compact 6 Ω three-way standmount (6.5" Kevlar woofer, 2" dome mid, 1" dome tweeter, dual rear ports) with an asymmetric, mirror-imaged baffle. Erin's Audio Corner reports a preference score of 5.9 despite dips around 200-500 Hz and 3 kHz and a resonance near 660 Hz. Bass is modest (about 62 Hz f3, graph-read), and impedance falls to 3.0 Ω, so it is effectively a 4 Ω load. Critics like its smooth, natural tonality and value; it suits small to medium rooms with a capable amplifier.

## Directivity
Beamwidth and DI figures were not computed (no raw data). From EAC's plots, DI rises smoothly from about 50 dB offset at 100 Hz to about 62 dB at 20 kHz with a small step near 2-3 kHz, and the early-reflection response tracks the listening window with a gentle slope. The mid and tweeter are offset on the baffle (use as mirrored pair); StereoNET suggests midrange domes inward. On-axis runs above the listening window in the top octaves (about 1-2 dB, graph-read), suggesting a little toe-in is fine. See [[directivity-index]], [[early-reflections]].

## Output & distortion
EAC graph-read THD about 0.7% at 86 dB and 2.2% at 96 dB (worst case across 100 Hz-10 kHz), so clean by [[compression-and-max-spl]] standards at moderate level, with bass distortion rising at 96 dB. Compression sweep: up to about 0.9 dB at 96 dB; at 102 dB compression exceeds 3 dB below about 110 Hz and near 200 Hz. Erin notes higher midrange multitone distortion. Good for small-to-medium rooms; use a subwoofer or high-pass for loud, bass-heavy material. Maker claims 103 dB peak (unverified).

## Amplifier load
6 Ω nominal. EAC measured a 3.0 Ω minimum (above 80 Hz) and a 1.4 Ω EPDR minimum near 110 Hz, with phase swinging between about -60° and +48°; flag `effectively-4ohm`. Choose an amplifier stable into low-impedance loads; eCoustics suggests 100-200 W class A/B brings out the best. See [[impedance-and-epdr]].

## Critic reviews
- [[ecoustics-wharfedale-super-denton]] (eCoustics, 10/10): rich tonal weight, natural vocals, bass not full range, scales with amplification.
- [[stereonet-wharfedale-super-denton]] (StereoNET, 8.5/10): balanced and smooth, more bass weight than expected, less air than pricier models.
- [[eac-wharfedale-super-denton]] (Erin's Audio Corner, Tier D listening): good but slightly reduced mids and upper mids; prefers the Linton; bass decent not deep.

## User reviews
Two reports, anecdotal ([[users-wharfedale-super-denton]]): both positive. No QC issues reported.

## Conflicts
- Sensitivity: ~86.5 dB graph-read vs 87 dB claimed (within 1 dB).
- Bass: claimed 52 Hz (±3 dB) and 40 Hz (-6 dB) vs graph-read f3 about 62 Hz, f6 about 52 Hz.
- Minimum impedance: claimed 3.4 Ω vs measured 3.0 Ω.
- Critic praise for smoothness vs measured 660 Hz resonance and 200-500 Hz / 3 kHz dips (Erin heard slightly reduced mids).
- Price: $1,499 maker-direct; $1,399 was the original price per eCoustics.
