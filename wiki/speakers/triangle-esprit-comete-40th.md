## Summary
The Esprit Comete 40th is an 8 Ω-rated, two-way, front-ported standmount with a 6.5" cellulose midwoofer and a 1" magnesium-dome horn tweeter, made in France. NRC anechoic data show a moderately even midrange with a dip near 4 kHz, high sensitivity (88.0 dB) and a steep bass roll-off, plus an impedance that dips to about 3.6 Ω. It suits listeners who value a forward, smooth midrange and efficiency, ideally with a sub and a capable amplifier. Currently on sale at $1,900 per pair (regular $2,750).

## Directivity
No beamwidth, DI or spinorama data. NRC horizontal curves show 15 and 30 deg tracking on-axis closely; 45-75 deg curves fall progressively above 1 kHz (graph only, not quantified). The reviewer used about 15 deg toe-in and found less toe-in reduced image size and specificity. See [directivity-index](../concepts/directivity-index.md), [early-reflections](../concepts/early-reflections.md).

## Output & distortion
NRC measured THD+N at 90 dB (2 m): about 1.7-2% across the 1.4-2 kHz region and about 5-6% at 50 Hz (graph-read). Level is not 86 or 96 dB, so those fields are null. No compression or max-SPL test; the reviewer played cleanly to 95-100 dB peaks (Tier D). Likely adequate for small-to-medium rooms; bass limits headroom. See [compression-and-max-spl](../concepts/compression-and-max-spl.md).

## Amplifier load
Rated 8 Ω, but the NRC curve dips to about 3.6 Ω near 200 Hz (flagged `effectively-4ohm`; maker claims 4.2 Ω), with peaks near 20 Ω and phase swings (phase graph not analysed). EPDR not computed. Use an amplifier comfortable at 4 Ω; the reviewer's 300 W amp never exceeded about 30 W. See [impedance-and-epdr](../concepts/impedance-and-epdr.md).

## Conflicts
- Sensitivity: 88.0 dB measured (NRC) vs 90 dB claimed (maker, now) vs 92 dB claimed per the 2021 review; the measured value is 2 dB below the current claim.
- Minimum impedance: about 3.6 Ω (NRC, graph-read) vs 4.2 Ω claimed; difference over 0.5 Ω.
- Crossover: 3.8 kHz (reviewer) vs unclear "2500 Hz - 350 Hz" (maker).
- Bass: maker claims 49 Hz; NRC shows -3 dB near 72 Hz, while the in-room -3 dB was 38 Hz (room gain).
- Measured sample is the 2021 launch model; current production may differ.
