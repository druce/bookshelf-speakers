## Summary
The Sierra-LX is Ascend's $1,598 flagship passive standmount: a 6" SEAS aluminium-cone woofer, 26 mm dome and a bamboo cabinet, with tunable bass plugs. Erin's Klippel data shows an excellent listening window (±1.3 dB), very wide dispersion and good bass extension for the size (f3 46 Hz, f6 42 Hz). Sensitivity is low at 80.6 dB, so it wants a powerful amplifier. Impedance, distortion and compression have not been ingested (Erin's review is video-only), so output limits are unverified; the maker claims 350 W power handling.

## Directivity
Horizontal -6 dB beamwidth about 144°, vertical about 91°. DI has a minor discontinuity (ERDI residual 1.2 dB); SPDI rises from 5.6 dB at 1 kHz to 8.2 dB at 10 kHz. Early-reflection dip near 1.5 kHz is 1.4 dB, under the 3 dB flag. On-axis minus listening window above 4 kHz is +0.8 dB, so either aim works; ASR forum members note a 2-5 kHz energy increase at wide angles and that a very slight toe-in is used at the maker's demos. See [directivity-index](../concepts/directivity-index.md), [early-reflections](../concepts/early-reflections.md).

## Output & distortion
No distortion or compression data ingested. Aggregators mention Erin's compression data and a mid-range distortion comment, but these were not verified from a primary page. The maker claims 350 W continuous / 500 W peak and "massive dynamics" (Tier C, unverified). With 80.6 dB sensitivity, expect to need substantial power for loud levels. See [compression-and-max-spl](../concepts/compression-and-max-spl.md).

## Amplifier load
Rated 8 Ω; no measured impedance ingested, so no flag can be set. Low sensitivity means ample amplifier power is advisable; maker suggests 33-180 W minimum by listening distance. See [impedance-and-epdr](../concepts/impedance-and-epdr.md).

## User reviews
Anecdotal ([users-ascend-acoustics-sierra-lx](../sources/users-ascend-acoustics-sierra-lx.md), 3 owner reports from 2 people): wide, large soundstage and strong bass; 2 of 2 independent owners found the top end slightly bright, and one returned his pair for it. No QC problems reported.

## Conflicts
- Sensitivity: 80.6 dB measured vs 83 dB maker anechoic (2.4 dB higher) vs 87 dB maker in-room.
- Bass: maker anechoic -6 dB at 36 Hz vs computed f6 42 Hz (6 Hz difference).
- Preference score: 6.04 here; aggregators quote other values (6.63, 5.28) from other computations; used the packet value.
- Treble: ASR forum members disagree whether the measured response explains the reported brightness.
