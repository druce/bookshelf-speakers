## Summary
The Persona B is Paradigm's flagship standmount: a 7" beryllium mid/woofer and 1" beryllium dome in a curved, ported cabinet, at $5,249.99 each. Paradigm says only "compatible with 8 ohms", but both labs measured about 3.4-3.8 Ω minimum, so it behaves like a 4 Ω speaker. Measurements show a somewhat uneven response (treble lift, presence dip) and good sensitivity; there is no spinorama, so directivity and preference score are unavailable. A rave subjective review (2017) contrasts with the lab data. Price is far above the rest of the field.

## Directivity
Not measured in CTA-2034 form; NRC plotted on-axis and off-axis responses to 75 degrees (graphs not read). No beamwidth, DI or preference score. HFN advises a slight toe-in off-axis to tame the treble lift. See [directivity-index](../concepts/directivity-index.md), [early-reflections](../concepts/early-reflections.md).

## Output & distortion
No compression or max-SPL test. HFN found THD of 0.8% or less at 90 dB/1 m, 100 Hz-10 kHz (clean). NRC's 90 dB THD plot shows distortion rising to roughly 3-5% below ~70 Hz (graph-read, approximate). Sensitivity near 87 dB with clean mid distortion suggests adequate output for medium-to-large rooms, but the single 7" woofer limits deep bass at volume. See [compression-and-max-spl](../concepts/compression-and-max-spl.md).

## Amplifier load
Marketed as "compatible with 8 ohms", but measured minimum is about 3.4 Ω (NRC) / 3.8 Ω (HFN), flagged `effectively-4ohm`. Impedance peaks to about 23 Ω near 90 Hz; phase stays within +/-50 degrees (HFN), so the load is tough but not extreme. Use an amplifier stable into 4 Ω. EPDR not computed. See [impedance-and-epdr](../concepts/impedance-and-epdr.md).

## Critic reviews
- [soundstage-paradigm-persona-b](../sources/soundstage-paradigm-persona-b.md) (SoundStage!, Reviewers' Choice): fast controlled bass, neutral and effortless mids and highs, precise imaging.
- [hifinews-paradigm-persona-b](../sources/hifinews-paradigm-persona-b.md) (Hi-Fi News lab report, measurements only): lively treble, presence suckout, bass extension generous for size, load tough but manageable.

## Conflicts
- Subjective rave ([soundstage-paradigm-persona-b](../sources/soundstage-paradigm-persona-b.md)) vs measured +/-4.6 to 4.8 dB response errors ([hifinews-paradigm-persona-b](../sources/hifinews-paradigm-persona-b.md)) and a treble lift in NRC graphs; unresolved (different listeners and metrics).
- Sensitivity: 86.9 dB (NRC LW mean) vs 90.5 dB at 1 kHz (HFN; 87.2 dB band mean) vs 89 dB claimed; the NRC and HFN band means agree within 0.3 dB.
- Impedance: nominal "8 ohm compatible" vs measured minima 3.4-3.8 Ω; NRC and HFN differ by about 0.4 Ω (graph-read vs stated).
- LF extension: 42 Hz (HFN, -6 dB, nearfield) vs NRC graph roll-off near 75-80 Hz (gated 2 m); methods differ.
- Price/status: Paradigm lists it current at $5,249.99 each; the original review price was $7,000/pair; no dealer stock verified.
