## Summary
The Totem Signature One is a 2-way, rear-ported 6.5" standmount with a chambered 1" aluminium dome, rated 8 Ω and 87.5 dB, at $3,800 per pair. Measured sensitivity is only 83.0-83.5 dB, about 4 dB under the claim, so it needs a reasonably powerful amp. The impedance is an easy 7.4-7.7 Ω minimum. Tier A/B data show strong cabinet and port resonances near 490-700 Hz and a crossover-region suckout above the tweeter axis, but wide even horizontal dispersion. Suits listeners who value refined treble and imaging in small to medium rooms. No spinorama exists, so no preference score.

## Directivity
Stereophile reports wide, even horizontal dispersion to 6 kHz, narrowing above as expected for a 1" metal dome, which it links to stable imaging; vertically a sharp suckout develops just above the tweeter axis near the crossover, so the smoothest treble lies at or just below the tweeter axis (sit or aim accordingly). NRC provides off-axis curves to 75 degrees but no numeric beamwidth, DI or toe-in metrics; none computed. See [directivity-index](../concepts/directivity-index.md), [early-reflections](../concepts/early-reflections.md).

## Output & distortion
NRC's THD+N at 90 dB (measured at 2 m) shows about 2.5% near 100 Hz, 2-3.5% around 200-300 Hz (graph-read), falling under about 1% above 500 Hz; the level is at 2 m, so not entered as thd_86/96. No compression or max-SPL test. With only 83 dB sensitivity, loud listening in larger rooms will demand substantial amplifier power. See [compression-and-max-spl](../concepts/compression-and-max-spl.md).

## Amplifier load
Easy load: 7.4-7.7 Ω minimum near 200 Hz, high impedance elsewhere (above 50 Ω at about 85 Hz), phase occasionally extreme but at high magnitude (Stereophile). No flag. EPDR not computed. Low sensitivity argues for an amp with real power rather than a low-power tube amp. See [impedance-and-epdr](../concepts/impedance-and-epdr.md).

## Conflicts
- Sensitivity: 83.0 dB (NRC) and 83.5 dB (Stereophile) vs 87.5 dB claimed ([mfr-totem-acoustic-signature-one](../sources/mfr-totem-acoustic-signature-one.md)), delta about 4 dB; Stereophile quotes the spec as 87 dB.
- Crossover: maker 2.5 kHz (2nd order), Stereophile review text 2.7 kHz, measured acoustic 3.5 kHz.
- Bass: claimed 45 Hz (-3 dB) vs NRC listening-window rolling through -3 dB near 85 Hz (graph-read; chamber low-frequency limits); SoundStage's listener found bass deeper than 45 Hz in room.
- Min impedance 7.4 vs 7.7 Ω: within 0.5 Ω.
- Both Tier A/B measurements date from the 2017-18 launch; Totem's page still lists the same specs.
