## Summary
The Emit 20 is a 6 Ω, rear-ported two-way with an 18 cm MSP woofer and a 28 mm Cerotar soft dome, large for a bookshelf (370 mm tall, 10.3 kg). On Erin's Klippel it shows a listening window of ±2.6 dB, good bass (f3 52 Hz) with low distortion, wide directivity, a woofer resonance near 700 Hz, and sensitivity of only 83.2 dB against a claimed 86 dB. It suits listeners wanting scale and bass in a standmount in a mid-sized room, with an amplifier of moderate-to-good power. List price is $1,049/pair; street prices near $800 have been reported but only the list price was confirmed in stock.

## Directivity
Horizontal -6 dB beamwidth about 135°, vertical about 122°: wide and consistent through about 13 kHz per Erin. DI shows a minor discontinuity (ERDI residual 1.5 dB); SPDI rises from 5.1 dB at 1 kHz to 8.0 dB at 10 kHz. Early-reflection dip near 1.5 kHz is 2.6 dB, just under the 3 dB flag. On-axis minus listening window above 4 kHz is +0.5 dB, so either aim works on that metric, but Erin found the top end bright on-axis and preferred 10-15° toe-out from the listener (off-axis); What Hi-Fi? found a little toe-in helped imaging, so the sources differ. See [directivity-index](../concepts/directivity-index.md), [early-reflections](../concepts/early-reflections.md).

## Output & distortion
Erin's THD is about 0.8% at 86 dB and about 2.4% at 96 dB at worst, near 350 Hz (graph-read), with low distortion down to about 40 Hz. Compression at 102 dB @1 m is worst at the 700 Hz woofer resonance (about -2.2 dB), under about 1 dB elsewhere; no compression limit of 3 dB was reached, so `max_spl_db` is not entered. Suited to moderate-to-loud listening in small and medium rooms; the low sensitivity needs a decent amplifier. See [compression-and-max-spl](../concepts/compression-and-max-spl.md).

## Amplifier load
6 Ω nominal; measured minimum 5.5 Ω above 80 Hz, above the IEC floor of 4.8 Ω for a 6 Ω rating, so no flag. EPDR minimum of 2.9 Ω is moderately demanding; phase swings are modest (roughly ±45°). A competent amplifier stable into 4 Ω is a good match; one reviewer suggests at least 70 W (secondary report). See [impedance-and-epdr](../concepts/impedance-and-epdr.md).

## Critic reviews
- [whathifi-dynaudio-emit-20](../sources/whathifi-dynaudio-emit-20.md) (What Hi-Fi?, Sound 4): impressive scale and refined treble; dynamics and cohesion trail the KEF LS50 Meta.
- [audiograde-dynaudio-emit-20](../sources/audiograde-dynaudio-emit-20.md) (Audiograde, Best Value): sweet spot of the range; wide, deep stage and seamless integration.
- [hifireport-dynaudio-emit-20](../sources/hifireport-dynaudio-emit-20.md) (HiFiReport): bass beyond expectations, detailed non-harsh character, high value.
- [eac-dynaudio-emit-20](../sources/eac-dynaudio-emit-20.md) (Erin's Audio Corner, Tier D notes): warm easy listening with great bass, but muffled mids and a 4 kHz peak.

## User reviews
Only 1 owner report found (AV Forums), so anecdotal: balanced and non-harsh but less rhythmically driven than a Triangle Titus EZ ([users-dynaudio-emit-20-2026-10-11](../sources/users-dynaudio-emit-20-2026-10-11.md)). No QC problems reported.

## Conflicts
- Sensitivity: claimed 86 dB vs computed 83.2 dB (Erin Klippel): 2.8 dB below claim.
- Low-frequency limit: maker -6 dB at 42 Hz, HiFi Report quotes 39 Hz, Erin computed f6 39 Hz; f3 computed 52 Hz vs Erin's text 54 Hz (within tolerance).
- Aim: Erin prefers off-axis aim; What Hi-Fi? found toe-in improved focus.
- Erin's page body is dated 2023-11-12; dataset lists 2023-11-22.
