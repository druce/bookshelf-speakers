## Summary
The Revel Performa3 M106 is an 8 Ω nominal, 6.5" two-way ported standmount with a 1" aluminium dome on an acoustic-lens waveguide in a curved cabinet. Three independent rigs agree it is flat and very clean: ASR's Klippel shows listening window ±1.7 dB with minor DI discontinuity and distortion near zero above 1 kHz. Bass extension is good for a bookshelf (f3 59 Hz) and compression onset is high, but measured sensitivity (about 85.5 dB) is lower than the 87 dB claim and impedance dips to about 4.3-4.6 Ω. A vertical crossover suckout (Stereophile) favours tall stands. Suits listeners wanting a neutral reference-style standmount with clean output at higher levels. Price is about $2,200 per pair.

## Directivity
Horizontal -6 dB beamwidth about 132 degrees (ASR's text says about 100), vertical about 110 degrees. DI has a minor discontinuity (ERDI residual 1.1 dB); SPDI rises from 5.3 dB at 1 kHz to 8.0 dB at 10 kHz. Early-reflection dip near 1.5 kHz is 1.2 dB, under the 3 dB flag. ASR notes woofer beaming against a wider tweeter beam causes an off-axis dip near crossover; Stereophile found a deep vertical suckout more than 5 degrees above and 15 degrees below the tweeter axis, so tall stands are better than low ones. On-axis minus listening window above 4 kHz is +0.7 dB, so either aim works. See [directivity-index](../concepts/directivity-index.md), [early-reflections](../concepts/early-reflections.md).

## Output & distortion
ASR shows THD near zero above 500-1,000 Hz at both 86 dB and 96 dB @1 m, with LF distortion near 100 Hz of about 2.5% at 86 dB and about 6% at 96 dB. NRC's THD+N at 90 dB agrees in shape (~2.8% at 100 Hz). ASR's draft compression test shows about 110 dB at 80 Hz. SoundStage's listener found no strain at high volume. Practical implication: clean for medium rooms at high levels; deep or loud bass is limited by the 6.5" woofer and port. See [compression-and-max-spl](../concepts/compression-and-max-spl.md).

## Amplifier load
Rated 8 Ω; ASR measured 4.6 Ω and NRC about 4.3 Ω near 190 Hz, below the IEC floor of 6.4 Ω (flag `below-iec`), but above 4 Ω. Stereophile found 5.76 Ω with -34° phase at 138 Hz and 2.4 kHz and suggested an amplifier rated for 4 Ω; phase reaches -48° at 29 Hz where impedance is high. EPDR not computed. Revel recommends 50-150 W. See [impedance-and-epdr](../concepts/impedance-and-epdr.md).

## Critic reviews
- [stereophile-revel-m106](../sources/stereophile-revel-m106.md) (Stereophile, 2014): extraordinary; unsurpassed upper-bass clarity and transient articulation; beat Epos Elan 10 and Dynaudio Excite X12.
- [soundstage-revel-m106](../sources/soundstage-revel-m106.md) (SoundStage!, 2015): clean, neutral, transparent midrange; bass tight but a little lightweight until placed near the front wall; highly recommended.
- [asr-revel-m106](../sources/asr-revel-m106.md) (ASR, Tier D listening notes): smooth, detailed, plays loud without strain; strongest recommendation.

## User reviews
Two ASR owner mentions only ([users-revel-m106](../sources/users-revel-m106.md)), so anecdotal: one drops the subwoofer after finding in-room bass to about 40 Hz and sufficient level. No QC reports.

## Conflicts
- Sensitivity: 85.4 dB (ASR computed), 85.6 dB (NRC), 86.4 dB (Stereophile) versus 87 dB claimed; spread of 1.0 dB, all below the claim.
- Minimum impedance: 4.6 Ω (ASR) vs ~4.3 Ω (NRC), both graph-read, 0.3 Ω apart; Stereophile reports 5.76 Ω at two frequencies but no minimum, so it does not contradict them.
- Beamwidth: computed 132 degrees versus ASR's text of about 100 degrees; different definitions.
- ASR's sample was pre-owned; NRC and Stereophile used review samples of the original 2014-2015 production.
