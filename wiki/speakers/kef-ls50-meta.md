## Summary
The KEF LS50 Meta is an 8 Ω-rated, two-way ported standmount with a 1" vented aluminium dome and 5.25" cone in a Uni-Q coaxial array, priced at $1,599.99 per pair at KEF US. It measures very smoothly (listening window ±1.7 dB, smooth directivity) but its low bass is modest (f3 about 64 Hz), its impedance averages near 4 Ω, and its small woofer distorts heavily below ~150 Hz. Suits near-field or small-room listeners who value imaging and midrange clarity and have a robust amplifier, ideally with a subwoofer.

## Directivity
Horizontal -6 dB beamwidth about 123° (ASR data 113°; ASR says roughly ±50°), vertical about 118°. DI is smooth (ERDI residual 0.8 dB) with SPDI rising from 4.5 dB at 1 kHz to 8.1 dB at 10 kHz. The early-reflection dip near 1.5 kHz is 1.8 dB, below the 3 dB flag. On-axis minus listening window above 4 kHz is +0.8 dB (ASR +0.7), so either aim works; Erin found the best balance about 15° off-axis and ASR suggests pointing them at the listener. Stereophile saw very uniform dispersion in both planes. See [directivity-index](../concepts/directivity-index.md), [early-reflections](../concepts/early-reflections.md).

## Output & distortion
Erin's THD at 86 dB reaches about 2.2% near 100 Hz and rises steeply below it; at 96 dB it is about 6.7% near 100 Hz (graph-read), while above 1 kHz it stays around 0.5% or less. Compression at 102 dB exceeds 3 dB below ~90 Hz. ASR reported near-100% THD in the lowest bass at 86 dB and audible bass distortion and crackle when pushed. No max-SPL figure is stated, so `max_spl_db` is null. Fine for near-field and small rooms at moderate levels; pair with a sub and high-pass around 80-120 Hz for loud listening. See [compression-and-max-spl](../concepts/compression-and-max-spl.md).

## Amplifier load
8 Ω rated, but measured minimum 3.7 Ω (flag `effectively-4ohm`) with minimum EPDR of 1.7-1.8 Ω in 135-140 Hz and 660-725 Hz regions. Impedance peaks 26 Ω near 90 Hz and 23 Ω near 2.6 kHz. Combined with low sensitivity, it wants a high-current amplifier stable into 4 Ω; KEF suggests 40-100 W. See [impedance-and-epdr](../concepts/impedance-and-epdr.md).

## Critic reviews
- [stereophile-kef-ls50-meta](../sources/stereophile-kef-ls50-meta.md) (Stereophile, John Atkinson, highly recommended): bass like the original, more treble energy, more transparent, palpable vocals.
- [whathifi-kef-ls50-meta](../sources/whathifi-kef-ls50-meta.md) (What Hi-Fi?, 5/5): smooth, balanced, precise imaging; bass punchy but limited depth.
- [asr-kef-ls50-meta](../sources/asr-kef-ls50-meta.md) (ASR, Tier D listening notes): sound "right"; recommended, pair with a subwoofer for loud bass.
- [eac-kef-ls50-meta](../sources/eac-kef-ls50-meta.md) (Erin, Tier D notes): precise imaging, some lack of upper-midrange attack, distortion audible at high levels.

## User reviews
Crutchfield ([crutchfield-kef-ls50-meta](../sources/crutchfield-kef-ls50-meta.md); 143 reviews, 130 five-star, hand-tallied): imaging (~54) and clarity (~70) lead the praise. 15 of 143 say bass is limited or recommend a sub (9 of 143 found bass better than expected); 6 of 143 note limited loudness; 7 of 143 say it needs a capable amplifier; 6 of 143 call treble bright or forward, 7 of 143 explicitly not harsh. The amplifier comments agree with the measured 82.7 dB sensitivity and 3.7 Ω minimum. Forum posts ([users-kef-ls50-meta](../sources/users-kef-ls50-meta.md), about 5) were similar: 4 of 5 mention bass distortion or a sub, 2 of 5 a strong amp.

QC/reliability: product defects 2 of 143 (poor-condition first pair, replaced; chipping paint); no driver or electrical failures.

## Conflicts
- Sensitivity: 82.7 dB (EAC computed), 83.4 (ASR computed), 84.5 (Stereophile) versus 85 claimed; delta up to 2.3 dB below the claim.
- Min impedance 3.7 Ω (Tier A) versus 8 Ω nominal: speaker is effectively a 4 Ω load.
- f3: 64 Hz (EAC) vs 68 Hz (ASR), within 5 Hz of threshold; port plug state may differ (audioXpress measured with the port plugged).
- Pref score: 5.7 (EAC), 5.4 (ASR), 5.0 (audioXpress digitised); spread over 0.5, indicating rig/sample sensitivity.
- Price/status: KEF US page shows $1,599.99 but an unclear purchase flow; Erin notes sales near $1,000; no separate retailer check made.
