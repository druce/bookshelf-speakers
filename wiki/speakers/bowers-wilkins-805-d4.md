## Summary
The 805 D4 is the 800 Series Diamond standmount: an 8 Ω-rated, two-way, front-ported cabinet with a 1" diamond dome in a tube above a 6.5" Continuum woofer, 440 mm tall and 15.6 kg each. NRC anechoic data show a dished listening window with a broad treble peak near 9-10 kHz and sensitivity of 84.4 dB; Hi-Fi News agrees on the shape and finds good bass extension (f6 39 Hz) with low distortion. Impedance bottoms near 4.3-4.7 Ω, so it is not a true 8 Ω load. It suits small-to-medium rooms and listeners who value resolution and imaging and can tolerate a bright-leaning balance. No Klippel spinorama is available.

## Directivity
No beamwidth, DI or early-reflection data are published in the ingested sources (NRC supplied on-axis and off-axis curves only as images). The treble rise means on-axis is brighter than the listening window; moderate toe-in is the usual advice for B&W tweeter-on-top designs, but that is not derived from data here. See [directivity-index](../concepts/directivity-index.md), [early-reflections](../concepts/early-reflections.md).

## Output & distortion
Distortion at 90 dB is low (HFN: 0.1-0.45% harmonic; NRC graph shows about 2% THD+N at 100 Hz falling below 1% above 300 Hz). No compression or max-SPL test. Sensible for small-to-medium rooms at normal levels; see [compression-and-max-spl](../concepts/compression-and-max-spl.md).

## Amplifier load
8 Ω nominal, 4.3-4.65 Ω minimum with phase of about ±57° in the bass; EPDR was not stated for this model (the Signature measures about 2.0-2.1 Ω, so expect something similar). Use a current-capable amplifier; B&W recommends 50-120 W. See [impedance-and-epdr](../concepts/impedance-and-epdr.md).

## Critic reviews
- [whathifi-bowers-wilkins-805-d4](../sources/whathifi-bowers-wilkins-805-d4.md) (What Hi-Fi?): class-leading clarity and imaging; lifeless at low volume; deeper-bass rivals exist.
- [soundstageultra-bowers-wilkins-805-d4](../sources/soundstageultra-bowers-wilkins-805-d4.md) (SoundStage! Ultra): "detail monster" with exemplary bass control; treble emphasis can fatigue at high volume.

## User reviews
Only 3 reports, so anecdotal ([users-bowers-wilkins-805-d4](../sources/users-bowers-wilkins-805-d4.md)): 2 describe treble-forward or fatiguing sound (1 owner, 1 demo listener), 1 secondhand report finds it open without edge. No QC problems found.

## Conflicts
- Sensitivity: 84.4 dB (NRC, 300 Hz-3 kHz) vs 86.2 dB (HFN, 500 Hz-8 kHz) vs claimed 88 dB; band and averaging differ, not a true disagreement, but 88 dB is not supported.
- Minimum impedance: ~4.3 Ω (NRC, graph-read) vs 4.65 Ω (HFN); 0.35 Ω difference, within graph-reading error.
- Price: B&W US and World Wide Stereo $10,000; earlier reviews cite $8,000-9,500. B&W US showed no stock (notification form) while World Wide Stereo listed it available; Audio Advice showed the pair sold out.
