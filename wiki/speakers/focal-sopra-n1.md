## Summary
The Sopra N°1 is Focal's top-line standmount (below the Utopia range): a 6.5" "W" sandwich-cone woofer, beryllium inverted-dome tweeter in a separate horn-loaded module, rear slot port, and a very heavy 19 kg cabinet. Both Tier-A sets (Audioholics outdoor gated, NRC anechoic) show a flat, neutral response, sensitivity 86.8-87.8 dB and a benign impedance, with low-frequency extension closer to 50 Hz (-6 dB) than Focal's 41 Hz claim. It is very expensive (listed at $12,998/pair, up from the $9,990 of 2020) and suits listeners paying for build and finish; on measured performance alone it is not clearly ahead of far cheaper Harman-style monitors.

## Directivity
No numeric beamwidth or DI export exists. Audioholics reports very mild tweeter beaming above 10 kHz (visible mainly in the early-reflections curve) and a slight directivity error where the tweeter is a little wider than the woofer near crossover, with little effect to 30 degrees off axis. On-axis has a small top-treble lift, so a slight off-axis aim may tame it. See [directivity-index](../concepts/directivity-index.md), [early-reflections](../concepts/early-reflections.md).

## Output & distortion
No compression or max-SPL test. NRC's THD+N at 90 dB/2 m (about 96 dB at 1 m) is roughly 1.3% at 100 Hz and under about 0.6% above 200 Hz (graph-read), clean for the level. Audioholics heard no compression or distortion at loud listening in a 24x13 ft room (Tier D). Likely ample for medium rooms; the bass limits very loud, bass-heavy use. See [compression-and-max-spl](../concepts/compression-and-max-spl.md).

## Amplifier load
8 Ω nominal, Focal claims a 3.9 Ω minimum. Audioholics found about 5 Ω at lowest (flagged `below-iec`, under 6.4 Ω); NRC's curve reads near 3.8 Ω around 190 Hz, which would be `effectively-4ohm`. Port tuning about 55 Hz. EPDR and phase not read. A stable amplifier rated for 4 Ω is prudent. See [impedance-and-epdr](../concepts/impedance-and-epdr.md).
