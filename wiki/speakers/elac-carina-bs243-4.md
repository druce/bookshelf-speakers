## Summary
The Carina BS243.4 is a 6 Ω, two-way, ported 5.25" standmount with a JET folded-ribbon (AMT) tweeter and a downward-firing port on an integral metal plinth. On the Klippel it has very wide, smooth directivity and a listening-window deviation of ±2.8 dB, held back by a response hole near 2.8 kHz at the crossover. Sensitivity is low (82.2 dB measured against 85 dB claimed) and the impedance falls to 4.6 Ω. It suits listeners who want a wide-dispersion, refined-sounding speaker, have a capable amplifier, and can apply a little EQ. Price is $1,199 per pair (ASR cited $999 at review time).

## Directivity
Horizontal -6 dB beamwidth about 153° and vertical about 119°, with a smooth DI (ERDI residual 0.9 dB). SPDI rises from 5.1 dB at 1 kHz to 7.7 dB at 10 kHz. The early-reflection dip near 1.5 kHz is 3.0 dB, right at the 3 dB flag. On-axis minus listening window above 4 kHz is +0.4 dB, so toe-in is not critical; Elac and Stereophile both say tone barely changes with toe-in, which acts mostly as image focus. Stereophile found a vertical crossover suckout beyond 5° above or 10° below the woofer axis and ASR notes the usual two-way vertical issue, so aim the tweeter at ear height; Stereophile also notes it turns directional above 7.6 kHz. See [directivity-index](../concepts/directivity-index.md), [early-reflections](../concepts/early-reflections.md).

## Output & distortion
No max-SPL or compression test. ASR's THD graph at 86 dB peaks near 2.3% around 100-110 Hz (graph-read) and shows a narrow resonance near 1.9 kHz (about 1.65%). At 96 dB the plot exceeds 5% around 100-120 Hz, so thd_96db_pct is left null, and the 1.9 kHz spike reaches about 2.9%. ASR heard distorted sub-bass when pushed. Fine for small-to-medium rooms at moderate levels; low sensitivity means it takes real amplifier power. TAS found it dynamic at higher levels (Tier D). See [compression-and-max-spl](../concepts/compression-and-max-spl.md).

## Amplifier load
6 Ω nominal, 4.8 Ω minimum claimed. Both ASR and Stereophile measured 4.6 Ω (about 190-197 Hz), under the IEC 80% floor of 4.8 Ω, so flagged `below-iec`. Stereophile judged it not a difficult load because the phase peak sits where impedance is 12.65 Ω and treble impedance averages near 8 Ω. EPDR not computed. With 82 dB sensitivity, use an amplifier comfortable at 4 Ω with ample power (Elac suggests 30-150 W). See [impedance-and-epdr](../concepts/impedance-and-epdr.md).

## Critic reviews
- [stereophile-elac-carina-bs243-4](../sources/stereophile-elac-carina-bs243-4.md) (Stereophile, Reichert): more refined than any similarly priced speaker he knows, supersmooth balance; full tonal balance only about 12 in from the wall.
- [tas-elac-carina-bs243-4](../sources/tas-elac-carina-bs243-4.md) (The Absolute Sound, Gader): rich, slightly dark midrange, fatigue-free JET treble, unexpected bass weight; thick bass in a small room; very high for musicality and value.
- [asr-elac-carina-bs243-4](../sources/asr-elac-carina-bs243-4.md) (ASR, Tier D listening notes): wide halo, great clarity, slightly bright; preferred after EQ; recommended.

## User reviews
Anecdotal, about 5 reports ([users-elac-carina-bs243-4](../sources/users-elac-carina-bs243-4.md)): 2 liked the clarity, 1 found imaging and bass weak, 2 raised price/performance. Two sources were captured from search summaries only. No QC or reliability problems reported.

## Conflicts
- Sensitivity: claimed 85 dB vs 82.2 dB (ASR computed) and ~82.5 dB (Stereophile); about 2.5-3 dB below spec. ASR text says ~83 dB.
- Minimum impedance: claimed 4.8 Ω vs 4.6 Ω measured by both ASR (graph-read) and Stereophile; the maker's own minimum is at the IEC floor.
- Bass character: ASR/Klippel shows f3 54 Hz and distorted sub-bass; TAS reports solid extension into the 50-60 Hz range and some bass thickness in-room.
- Dimensions: maker's mm and inch lines are ordered inconsistently with Stereophile's HxWxD; height 318-319 mm agrees, depth is uncertain.
