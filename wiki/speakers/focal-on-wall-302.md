## Summary
The On Wall 302 is Focal's larger slim on-wall speaker: 1.25 m tall, 90 mm deep, four 4" flax mid-bass drivers around a 1" aluminium-magnesium inverted-dome tweeter, rated 8 Ω nominal. Focal pitches it for both home cinema and stereo, wall-mounted or on an optional stand. The only independent measurement ingested is an Audioholics in-room 1 m sensitivity (88.0 dB, 3 dB under the claim) and impedance curve (about 4.9 Ω minimum), which is not a spinorama, so flatness, directivity and bass extension remain unmeasured here. It suits buyers who want a premium LCR beside a large screen and will add a subwoofer.

## Directivity
Not measured. Focal does not publish dispersion data. [soundstage-focal-on-wall-302](../sources/soundstage-focal-on-wall-302.md) reported consistent tonal balance off the sweet spot (Tier D impression). On a wall there is no toe-in, so early side-wall reflections matter; see [directivity-index](../concepts/directivity-index.md) and [early-reflections](../concepts/early-reflections.md).

## Output & distortion
No distortion or compression measurements found. Audioholics said it played loud without strain with a 150 W/channel receiver and a subwoofer; SoundStage! said it played "pretty darn loud" from modest amplifiers (Tier D). Claimed sensitivity of 91 dB is not supported by the 88 dB in-room figure; plan on average-to-high sensitivity. See [compression-and-max-spl](../concepts/compression-and-max-spl.md).

## Amplifier load
Nominal 8 Ω, claimed minimum 5 Ω; the graph in [audioholics-focal-on-wall-302](../sources/audioholics-focal-on-wall-302.md) reads about 4.9 Ω near 200 Hz and with two large impedance peaks at port tuning. Audioholics suggested a 6 Ω equivalent rating but judged any modest AVR adequate. EPDR not computed (see [impedance-and-epdr](../concepts/impedance-and-epdr.md)). Phase is benign per the reviewer.

## Critic reviews
- [audioholics-focal-on-wall-302](../sources/audioholics-focal-on-wall-302.md) (Audioholics, 4/5 performance and 4/5 value stars, Gene DellaSala): called it excellent for music and home theater, capable of rivaling bookshelf designs, needs a subwoofer for deep bass, expensive; recommended.
- [soundstage-focal-on-wall-302](../sources/soundstage-focal-on-wall-302.md) (SoundStage!, Doug Schneider, 2022): praised super-clear midrange and clean spirited highs, reasonable bass with little below 50 Hz; "great choice" for discreet wall-mount use, though floorstanders give more bass for the money.
