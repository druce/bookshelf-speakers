## Summary
The Totem Bison Monitor is an 8 Ω-rated, rear-ported two-way with a 5.25" long-throw woofer and 1.3" textile dome, crossed at 2.5 kHz first order, priced $2,300 per pair. The only measurement found is Hi-Fi News' lab report (Tier B): a 5.4 Ω minimum, a roughly 3 dB bump around 800 Hz-1.3 kHz, bass to 41 Hz (-6 dB re 200 Hz) and low distortion at 90 dB. No spinorama or directivity data exists. Critics praise imaging and mid/treble clarity with tuneful but not weighty bass.

## Directivity
Not measured; no beamwidth or DI data. Hi-Fi News' listener noted tonal balance held well off-axis horizontally and vertically (Tier D). The lab saw the grille cut output by about 3 dB near 5 kHz, so listening without the grille is likely preferable. See [directivity-index](../concepts/directivity-index.md), [early-reflections](../concepts/early-reflections.md).

## Output & distortion
THD at 90 dB SPL/1 m is under 0.7% from 100 Hz to 10 kHz (0.65% at 100 Hz), which is clean for a box this size. No compression or max-SPL test exists. Port is small (about 30 mm) tuned near 35 Hz. Expect fine performance in small-to-medium rooms; loud listening in large rooms is untested. See [compression-and-max-spl](../concepts/compression-and-max-spl.md).

## Amplifier load
Rated 8 Ω; measured minimum 5.4 Ω at 228 Hz is below the IEC floor of 6.4 Ω (flag `below-iec`), though Hi-Fi News called it a comfortable 6 Ω-class load. Phase stays within about ±45 degrees and minimum EPDR is 2.8 Ω at 52 Hz, so a stable amplifier with good current is sensible. Maker recommends 30-150 W. See [impedance-and-epdr](../concepts/impedance-and-epdr.md).

## Conflicts
- Sensitivity: claimed 87 dB vs 84.8 dB (mean band) / 83.0 dB (IEC) at Hi-Fi News; 87.8 dB only at the 1 kHz bump.
- Impedance: rated 8 Ω vs 5.4 Ω minimum.
- Claimed 40 Hz (±3 dB) vs 41 Hz at -6 dB (re 200 Hz): the claim looks optimistic by several dB.
- Weight: maker lists 13.49 kg; Hi-Fi News 5 kg each; left null. Hi-Fi News table height 850 mm is inconsistent with maker's 333 mm.
