# Audio Science Review (ASR)

**Who:** Amir Majidimehr's forum, audiosciencereview.com. Speaker reviews since late 2019.

**Rig:** Klippel Near-Field Scanner (NFS). A robotic arm scans the speaker in an ordinary room, and holographic field separation removes room reflections to produce an anechoic-equivalent far field from about 20 Hz to 20 kHz. Grille off, referenced to the tweeter axis, typically 20 points/octave output. Tier A per Claude.md §3.

**Published per review:**
- Full CTA-2034 [spinorama](../concepts/spinorama.md), horizontal and vertical directivity contour plots, predicted in-room response.
- Impedance and phase, sometimes with EPDR in later reviews ([impedance-and-epdr](../concepts/impedance-and-epdr.md)).
- Harmonic distortion at 86 dB and 96 dB SPL @ 1 m (relative %, by harmonic), from about 2020 onward ([compression-and-max-spl](../concepts/compression-and-max-spl.md)).
- Subjective listening impressions from Amir, usually after EQ, and a recommendation ("panther" scale: headless/golfing/happy/…).
- Raw Klippel text exports are posted to the thread and mirrored by [spinorama-org](../sources/spinorama-org.md).

**Known biases and limits:**
- One sample per model, often the reviewer's own purchase or a loaner.
- The listening tests are sighted and by one listener, so treat them as Tier D, not Tier A.
- Max SPL and compression are not standardised. Sensitivity has been stated inconsistently in older reviews.
- The ASR community is strongly measurement-first. Forum replies under a review are Tier E and often include owners' reports.
- Amir disclosed that his company Madrona Digital is a Harman dealer (e.g. in the Revel M16 review).
