# Audioholics

**Who:** Gene DellaSala's site. Measurements are mostly by James Larson.

**Rig:** Outdoor ground-plane and quasi-anechoic (gated) measurements, merged with ground-plane or near-field bass. Since about 2019, full horizontal and vertical polar sets are used to build CTA-2034 [spinorama](../concepts/spinorama.md) curves. Claude.md §3 lists Audioholics as Tier A when a full spinorama is published. Older reviews without polars are Tier B.

**Published per review:**
- Spinorama, or horizontal/vertical off-axis response.
- Impedance and phase. Audioholics often comments explicitly on whether a "nominal 8 Ω" claim holds up ([impedance-and-epdr](../concepts/impedance-and-epdr.md)).
- For some speakers, CEA-2010-style burst output and long-term output-compression tests ([compression-and-max-spl](../concepts/compression-and-max-spl.md)).
- Extensive subjective listening, which is Tier D.

**Known biases and limits:**
- The gate limits resolution in the low-mid band, where accuracy depends on the merge.
- Fewer bookshelf reviews than ASR or EAC.
- Measured data is mirrored by spinorama.org as `misc-audioholics`.
