# Stereophile

**Who:** A US print and web magazine. Measurements are by John Atkinson (retired as technical editor in 2024, but still measuring) and colleagues. Subjective reviews come from a staff of writers.

**Rig:** Quasi-anechoic, gated MLS/DRA measurements at 50" on the tweeter axis, spliced to nearfield woofer and port responses below about 300 Hz. Lateral and vertical off-axis response families are published, but not a full CTA-2034 spinorama. Tier B per Claude.md §3, with lower confidence below about 300 Hz.

**Published per review ("Measurements" sidebar):**
- Estimated sensitivity, B-weighted at 2.83 V / 1 m ([sensitivity-normalization](../concepts/sensitivity-normalization.md)).
- Impedance magnitude and phase, plus explicit commentary on the minimum value and on **EPDR** ([impedance-and-epdr](../concepts/impedance-and-epdr.md)).
- Cabinet-wall resonances (accelerometer).
- Listening-axis response averaged across a 30° horizontal window.
- Off-axis families, step response and cumulative spectral decay.

**Known biases and limits:**
- Subjective conclusions often diverge from the measurements, and Stereophile prints both.
- Review samples are loaners. Coverage skews to the $1,000+ high end.
- "Recommended Components" listings are Tier D.
