# Erin's Audio Corner

**Who:** Erin Hardison, erinsaudiocorner.com and a YouTube channel. Speaker measurements since about 2020.

**Rig:** Klippel Near-Field Scanner (NFS), the same system as [[asr]]. Anechoic-equivalent spinorama from about 20 Hz. Some reviews include alternate configurations, such as sealed vs ported plugs, grille on/off, or a 10–15° reference axis; spinorama.org stores these as separate measurement keys. Tier A per Claude.md §3.

**Published per review:**
- CTA-2034 [[spinorama]], polar and contour plots, estimated in-room response.
- Impedance and phase.
- Harmonic distortion at 86 and 96 dB @ 1 m.
- **Compression sweeps** at stepped levels (e.g. 76→102 dB @ 1 m) and a **max-SPL / dynamic range** test using multitone. This is the most systematic output-limit data among the Tier A sources ([[compression-and-max-spl]]).
- Subjective notes from his listening room. These are sighted, so Tier D.

**Known biases and limits:**
- One sample, usually a manufacturer loaner, which is disclosed in the foreword.
- Patreon-supported.
- The "maxed out" and compression results depend on the test signal used; compare within EAC only.
