# Princeton 3D3A Lab

**Who:** The 3D Audio and Applied Acoustics lab at Princeton University (Edgar Choueiri). In 2015 it published an anechoic directivity database of loudspeakers.

**Rig:** Anechoic chamber with full horizontal and vertical polar measurements. Spinorama.org derives CTA-2034 curves from it under the key `princeton`. Treated as Tier A for directivity. Low-frequency accuracy depends on chamber size; the speaker page notes it where relevant. The dataset dates from 2015, so most of the speakers are now discontinued.
