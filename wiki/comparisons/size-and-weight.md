## Notes
Filed 2026-10-11 (query: range of dimensions and standouts). All figures are computed by `scripts/size_table.py`.
- **Smallest:** [Role Audio Skiff](../speakers/role-audio-skiff.md) (152 mm tall, 2.0 L, 1.6 kg) and [Focal Dome Flax](../speakers/focal-dome-flax.md) (3.6 L) are desktop/satellite-class, with f3 of 126–143 Hz, so they need a sub. Smallest that also measures well: [Neumi Silk 4](../speakers/neumi-silk-4.md) (6.6 L, pref w/sub 7.1, $190) and [Polk Audio Monitor XT15](../speakers/polk-audio-xt15.md) (8.1 L, 7.3, $149).
- **Best measured under 15 L:** [Polk Audio Reserve R100](../speakers/polk-audio-reserve-r100.md) (14.0 L, pref w/sub 7.9, LW ±1.0 dB). Mid-size Group A models sit at 16–18 L: [Ascend Acoustics CBM-170 SE2](../speakers/ascend-acoustics-cbm-170-se2.md), [KEF LS50 Meta](../speakers/kef-ls50-meta.md), [Revel M105](../speakers/revel-m105.md), [Ascend Acoustics Sierra-1 V2](../speakers/ascend-acoustics-sierra-1-v2.md).
- **Largest:** [Human Speakers 81 dk](../speakers/human-speakers-81-dk.md) (470 mm tall, 50.5 L, slightly over the ~18 in guideline), [Focal Sopra N°1](../speakers/focal-sopra-n1.md) (47 L, heaviest at 19 kg) and [MoFi SourcePoint 8](../speakers/mofi-sourcepoint-8.md) (44 L, 290 mm wide).
- **Deepest cabinets:** [Q Acoustics Concept 300](../speakers/q-acoustics-concept-300.md) (400 mm), [Focal Sopra N°1](../speakers/focal-sopra-n1.md) (396 mm) and [Polk Audio Reserve R200](../speakers/polk-audio-reserve-r200.md) (354 mm). Check shelf depth. Shallowest: [Avantone Pro CLA-10](../speakers/avantone-pro-cla-10.md) (198 mm) and [March Audio Sointuva AWG](../speakers/march-audio-sointuva-awg.md) (220 mm).
- **Deep bass for the size:** [March Audio Sointuva AWG](../speakers/march-audio-sointuva-awg.md) (f3 37 Hz from 23.8 L), [Buchardt Audio E50](../speakers/buchardt-audio-e50.md) (42 Hz, 21.3 L), [Ascend Acoustics Sierra-LX](../speakers/ascend-acoustics-sierra-lx.md) (46 Hz, 18.5 L) and, for its size, [PSB Alpha P3](../speakers/psb-alpha-p3.md) (62 Hz from 5.0 L). [SVS Ultra Evolution Bookshelf](../speakers/svs-ultra-evolution-bookshelf.md) (40 Hz) is Stereophile's nearfield-spliced figure (Tier B), not comparable with the Klippel-derived values.
- **Shallow bass for the size:** [Avantone Pro CLA-10](../speakers/avantone-pro-cla-10.md) (131 Hz) and [Vandersteen VLR](../speakers/vandersteen-vlr.md) (111 Hz) are real. [Q Acoustics 5020](../speakers/q-acoustics-5020.md) (103 Hz) and [Revel M126Be](../speakers/revel-m126be.md) (93 Hz) are f3-definition artifacts of a shallow LW shelf. Their f6 is about 50–60 Hz; see the speaker pages.
- **Box size explains bass only loosely:** f3 falls with volume (log–log slope −0.26, r = −0.59), so doubling the box lowers f3 by only about 17% on average. Tuning and design matter as much as size.
