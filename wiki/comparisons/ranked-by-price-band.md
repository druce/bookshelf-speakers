# Ranked by price band

Current, in-scope speakers with a spinorama-derived Olive preference score, sorted by **score with an ideal subwoofer** within each price band. Small bookshelf speakers are compared fairly on that score because bass extension dominates the no-sub score ([preference-score](../concepts/preference-score.md)). Per Claude.md §7, differences under 0.5 are not meaningful. Scores from Tier B hobbyist rigs are marked (B) and are less comparable. **Group A** = within 0.5 of the band leader; **Group B** = within 1.0. Rank inside a group is noise. The score ignores distortion, output and amplifier load, so the table also carries LW deviation ([listening-window](../concepts/listening-window.md)), f3 ([bass-extension-vs-room-gain](../concepts/bass-extension-vs-room-gain.md)), sensitivity, measured minimum impedance with its flag ([impedance-and-epdr](../concepts/impedance-and-epdr.md)) and THD at 96 dB. Labels follow Claude.md §7. Generated by `scripts/rank.py` from `data/speakers.csv`.

**Cross-band observations (computed from the table, Tier A only):**
- [Ascend Acoustics CBM-170 SE2](../speakers/ascend-acoustics-cbm-170-se2.md) ($468, pref w/sub 8.0) is within 0.5 of **every** band leader above it: [Ascend Acoustics Sierra-1 V2](../speakers/ascend-acoustics-sierra-1-v2.md) 8.4, [Revel M105](../speakers/revel-m105.md) 8.2, [March Audio Sointuva AWG](../speakers/march-audio-sointuva-awg.md) 8.45. With a subwoofer, spending more than about $500 a pair buys bass extension, output, load and finish rather than predicted tonal preference, at least as far as the Olive model can see.
- Of the 21 Group A/B models, only 3 have a measured minimum that clears the IEC floor ([Elac Debut 2.0 B5.2](../speakers/elac-debut-2-0-b5-2.md), [Ascend Acoustics Sierra-1 V2](../speakers/ascend-acoustics-sierra-1-v2.md), [MoFi SourcePoint 8](../speakers/mofi-sourcepoint-8.md)). Three have no measured minimum ([Ascend Acoustics Sierra-LX](../speakers/ascend-acoustics-sierra-lx.md), [Buchardt Audio E50](../speakers/buchardt-audio-e50.md), [Q Acoustics 3030i](../speakers/q-acoustics-3030i.md)), and the other 15 are flagged. In practice, the best-measuring passive bookshelf speakers are mostly 4 Ω-class loads. See [impedance-honesty](impedance-honesty.md).
- f3 values of 93–103 Hz for [Revel M126Be](../speakers/revel-m126be.md) and [Q Acoustics 5020](../speakers/q-acoustics-5020.md) come from a shallow LW shelf crossing −3 dB well above the real roll-off (f6 ≈ 50 Hz for the M126Be); see each page's Conflicts and [bass-extension-vs-room-gain](../concepts/bass-extension-vs-room-gain.md).
- `max_spl_db` is **not** used for ranking: ASR draft compression charts, EAC's 102 dB test ceiling and stated limits are not comparable.
