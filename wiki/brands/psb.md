# PSB

## Overview
PSB designs with the NRC anechoic chamber ([eac-psb-imagine-b50](../sources/eac-psb-imagine-b50.md), [soundstage-nrc-psb-imagine-b50](../sources/soundstage-nrc-psb-imagine-b50.md)). Alpha models (2019 relaunch) use inverted drivers with the tweeter below the woofer and L4 acoustic crossovers ([mfr-psb-alpha-p3](../sources/mfr-psb-alpha-p3.md)); the Synchrony B600 is the flagship standmount, rated 6 Ω ([mfr-psb-synchrony-b600](../sources/mfr-psb-synchrony-b600.md)). Imagine B50 and Alpha P5 each have Klippel or NRC data ([eac-psb-imagine-b50](../sources/eac-psb-imagine-b50.md), [asr-psb-alpha-p5](../sources/asr-psb-alpha-p5.md)).

## Patterns
Minimums are 3.3 to 4.1 Ω: four of four are flagged (one below-iec, three effectively-4ohm). Measured sensitivity is below claim in all four, by 1.1, 3.4, 0.3 and 4.7 dB; the maker's Alpha claims run 3-5 dB above Klippel values ([asr-psb-alpha-p3](../sources/asr-psb-alpha-p3.md), [asr-psb-alpha-p5](../sources/asr-psb-alpha-p5.md)). Preference scores run 3.1, 4.0 and 4.6 without sub. ASR was negative on both Alphas while Stereophile and TAS praised the P5 ([stereophile-psb-alpha-p5](../sources/stereophile-psb-alpha-p5.md), [tas-psb-alpha-p5](../sources/tas-psb-alpha-p5.md)).
