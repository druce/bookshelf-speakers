# Salk

## Overview
Salk Sound (Seattle) builds to order, factory-direct; the WoW1 is its only mini-monitor, with a Hiquphon OW1 tweeter, Seas Excel W12 woofer and front port ([mfr-salk-wow1](../sources/mfr-salk-wow1.md)). Its price rose from $1,295 (2020) to $2,595. The only data is ASR's Klippel review ([asr-salk-wow1](../sources/asr-salk-wow1.md)).
