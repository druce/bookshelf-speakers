# ATC

## Overview
ATC builds its drivers in-house (SL mid/bass, SH25-76 tweeter) in sealed cabinets and gives its entry series low sensitivity of 84-85 dB claimed ([avmentor-atc-scm19](../sources/avmentor-atc-scm19.md), [stereophile-atc-scm7](../sources/stereophile-atc-scm7.md)). Both paged models are premium priced and have Tier B or critic-level data only; neither has a spinorama or listening-window figure. US supply is via dealers ([avmentor-atc-scm19](../sources/avmentor-atc-scm19.md)).

## Patterns
Both models are rated 8 Ω and both measure below the IEC 6.4 Ω floor (5.6 and 5.3 Ω), flagged below-iec. Both measure 2.0 dB under claimed sensitivity (83.0 vs 85.0; 82.0 vs 84.0; [stereophile-atc-scm7](../sources/stereophile-atc-scm7.md)). Both critic pages call for a capable amp. The SCM19 user page records 4 reports wanting a capable amp, 3 positive on bass and dynamics, and no QC problems ([users-atc-scm19-2026-10-11](../sources/users-atc-scm19-2026-10-11.md)); no user page for the SCM7.
