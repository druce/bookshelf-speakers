# Wharfedale

## Overview
Wharfedale's paged models differ in character: the Super Denton is a Heritage-series compact three-way with an offset mid/tweeter mirror pair, and the EVO 4.1 is the two-way with an AMT tweeter in the EVO4 line ([eac-wharfedale-super-denton](../sources/eac-wharfedale-super-denton.md), [asr-wharfedale-evo-4-1](../sources/asr-wharfedale-evo-4-1.md)). Both are low-impedance designs; most of the brand's other screened speakers fail on height or lack independent measurements.

## Patterns
- Impedance: 2 of 2 are flagged effectively-4ohm (Super Denton 3.0 Ω against a 6 Ω rating, EVO 4.1 3.2 Ω against 8 Ω).
- Sensitivity: 86.5 and 85.9 dB, within 1.1 dB of the 87.0 dB claims.
- Of 8 other screened models, 3 are out of scope on height and 5 have no Tier A/B data.
- ASR did not recommend the EVO 4.1 (3.8 kHz tweeter resonance); the Super Denton drew uniformly positive critics ([ecoustics-wharfedale-super-denton](../sources/ecoustics-wharfedale-super-denton.md), [stereonet-wharfedale-super-denton](../sources/stereonet-wharfedale-super-denton.md)).
- Users: [users-wharfedale-super-denton](../sources/users-wharfedale-super-denton.md) 2 reports, [users-wharfedale-evo-4-1](../sources/users-wharfedale-evo-4-1.md) 6 reports (incentivised and snippet-based); all anecdotal, no QC problems recorded.
