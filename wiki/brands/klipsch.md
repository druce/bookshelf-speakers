# Klipsch

## Overview
Klipsch's Reference Premiere standmounts use a horn-loaded 90x90 Tractrix design with narrow-ish dispersion, and Klipsch rates sensitivity in-room, half-space ([[mfr-klipsch-rp-500m-ii]], [[eac-klipsch-rp-500m-ii]]). Specs say '8 ohms compatible' rather than a nominal. Both paged models have Erin's Audio Corner Klippel data and score 5.2 and 5.6 without sub, high in this wiki ([[eac-klipsch-rp-500m-ii]], [[eac-klipsch-rp-600m-ii]]), at $400 and $700.

## Patterns
Both measure 3.3 and 3.5 Ω minimum, flagged effectively-4ohm. Measured sensitivity is 84.8 and 85.4 dB, 7.2 and 9.1 dB under the half-space claims, so the claims are not comparable to 2.83 V/1 m. Both have a 1.9 dB listening-window deviation and f3 of 66-68 Hz. Critics call the line lively and bright and note amp matching matters ([[hifitrends-klipsch-rp-600m-ii]], [[stereonet-klipsch-rp-600m-ii]]). User pages hold fewer than 5 reports each; one unspecified build complaint was noted for the RP-600M II ([[users-klipsch-rp-600m-ii]], [[users-klipsch-rp-500m-ii]]).
