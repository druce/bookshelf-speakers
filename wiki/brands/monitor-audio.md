# Monitor Audio

## Overview
Monitor Audio's Bronze 7G is its current entry series, with a 6 in C-CAM woofer, gold dome tweeter and 3 kHz crossover ([[mfr-monitor-audio-bronze-50-7g]]). The Apex A10 is a lifestyle or home-theatre compact in an aluminium cabinet, sold singly ([[mfr-monitor-audio-apex-a10]]). Evidence differs: Hi-Fi News lab data for the Bronze ([[hifinews-monitor-audio-bronze-50-7g]]) and ASR for the Apex ([[asr-monitor-audio-apex-a10]]).

## Patterns
Both models are flagged for impedance (4.2 Ω below-iec; 3.0 Ω effectively-4ohm) despite an 8 Ω rating. Measured sensitivity is 86.7 and 86.3 dB, close to claims (86.0 and 87.0). ASR called the Apex A10 bright and 'showroom tuned' and did not recommend it ([[asr-monitor-audio-apex-a10]]). Apex user page has 3 owner reports, 2 of 3 noting bright treble ([[users-monitor-audio-apex-a10]]); no Bronze user page.
