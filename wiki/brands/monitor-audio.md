# Monitor Audio

## Overview
Monitor Audio's Bronze 7G is its current entry series, with a 6 in C-CAM woofer, gold dome tweeter and 3 kHz crossover ([mfr-monitor-audio-bronze-50-7g](../sources/mfr-monitor-audio-bronze-50-7g.md)). The Apex A10 is a lifestyle or home-theatre compact in an aluminium cabinet, sold singly ([mfr-monitor-audio-apex-a10](../sources/mfr-monitor-audio-apex-a10.md)). Evidence differs: Hi-Fi News lab data for the Bronze ([hifinews-monitor-audio-bronze-50-7g](../sources/hifinews-monitor-audio-bronze-50-7g.md)) and ASR for the Apex ([asr-monitor-audio-apex-a10](../sources/asr-monitor-audio-apex-a10.md)).

## Patterns
Both models are flagged for impedance (4.2 Ω below-iec; 3.0 Ω effectively-4ohm) despite an 8 Ω rating. Measured sensitivity is 86.7 and 86.3 dB, close to claims (86.0 and 87.0). ASR called the Apex A10 bright and 'showroom tuned' and did not recommend it ([asr-monitor-audio-apex-a10](../sources/asr-monitor-audio-apex-a10.md)). Apex user page has 3 owner reports, 2 of 3 noting bright treble ([users-monitor-audio-apex-a10](../sources/users-monitor-audio-apex-a10.md)); no Bronze user page.
