# Fluance

## Overview
Fluance is an online-direct budget brand whose passive Elite, Reference and Signature lines all sell for roughly $150 to $200 per pair and are rated "8 ohm compatible" ([mfr-fluance-hfs-signature](../sources/mfr-fluance-hfs-signature.md)). Both paged models carry impedance flags. ASR measured the HFS Signature at ±4.5 dB listening window and f3 of 74 Hz ([asr-fluance-hfs-signature](../sources/asr-fluance-hfs-signature.md)); the Reference XL8S was measured by Archimago, not Klippel, so LW and f3 are blank ([archimago-fluance-reference-xl8s](../sources/archimago-fluance-reference-xl8s.md)).

## Patterns
- Impedance: 2 of 2 fall below 6.4 Ω (XL8S 6.0 Ω below-iec; HFS 3.7 Ω effectively-4ohm).
- Sensitivity: 2 of 2 measure at or above the 85.0 dB claim (85.0 and 85.8 dB).
- Preference scores are low: 3.5 and 2.9 (5.6 and 5.5 with sub).
- Critics: HomeTheaterHiFi ([hometheaterhifi-fluance-hfs-signature](../sources/hometheaterhifi-fluance-hfs-signature.md), [hometheaterhifi-fluance-reference-xl8s](../sources/hometheaterhifi-fluance-reference-xl8s.md)) praised value; ASR does not recommend the HFS; Archimago found a rough 1.5–8 kHz response on the XL8S.
- Users: [users-fluance-hfs-signature](../sources/users-fluance-hfs-signature.md) records 4 reports, split 2 positive and 2 negative, anecdotal. Stock gaps noted on the maker site.
