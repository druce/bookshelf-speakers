# Chesky Audio

## Overview
Chesky Audio sells direct, with 3D-printed cabinets hand-built to order and a quoted 4-6 week lead time ([[mfr-chesky-audio-lc1]]). The LC1 is the only paged model; its impedance never drops below 8.4 Ω, unusually benign, but it measures 2.8 dB under claimed sensitivity ([[eac-chesky-audio-lc1]]). Enthusiast outlets praised it ([[futureaudiophile-chesky-audio-lc1]], [[enjoythemusic-chesky-audio-lc1]]) while Erin found recessed mids and early bass roll-off.
