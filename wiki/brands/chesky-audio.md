# Chesky Audio

## Overview
Chesky Audio sells direct, with 3D-printed cabinets hand-built to order and a quoted 4-6 week lead time ([mfr-chesky-audio-lc1](../sources/mfr-chesky-audio-lc1.md)). The LC1 is the only paged model; its impedance never drops below 8.4 Ω, unusually benign, but it measures 2.8 dB under claimed sensitivity ([eac-chesky-audio-lc1](../sources/eac-chesky-audio-lc1.md)). Enthusiast outlets praised it ([futureaudiophile-chesky-audio-lc1](../sources/futureaudiophile-chesky-audio-lc1.md), [enjoythemusic-chesky-audio-lc1](../sources/enjoythemusic-chesky-audio-lc1.md)) while Erin found recessed mids and early bass roll-off.
