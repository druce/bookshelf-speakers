# Amphion

## Overview
Amphion is a Finnish maker of handmade speakers; its Argon1 uses a waveguide tweeter and a low (1.6 kHz) crossover and is rated 8 Ω and 86 dB ([mfr-amphion-argon1](../sources/mfr-amphion-argon1.md)). US pricing runs from $1,919 direct to $2,498 at retail ([mfr-amphion-argon1](../sources/mfr-amphion-argon1.md)). The only in-scope model here has no impedance, sensitivity or f3 measurement in the wiki; its evidence is a spinorama.org preference score ([spinorama-org](../sources/spinorama-org.md)) plus critic reviews ([napilopez-amphion-argon1](../sources/napilopez-amphion-argon1.md), [audiophilia-amphion-argon1](../sources/audiophilia-amphion-argon1.md), [hifireport-amphion-argon1](../sources/hifireport-amphion-argon1.md)).

## Patterns
Single model. Preference score 4.8 without and 7.0 with sub ([spinorama-org](../sources/spinorama-org.md)). Critics praise wide dispersion, imaging and tonal uniformity, with some on-axis treble brightness and limited deep bass. Sensitivity is claimed only (86.0 dB), not measured.
