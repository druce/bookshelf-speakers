# Dynaudio

## Overview
Dynaudio rates its Special Forty and Emit 20 at 6 Ω, not 8 Ω; the Emit series is its entry range, sharing a 28 mm Cerotar tweeter with the Hexis ([mfr-dynaudio-emit-20](../sources/mfr-dynaudio-emit-20.md)). Both paged models have Tier A data ([eac-dynaudio-special-forty](../sources/eac-dynaudio-special-forty.md), [soundstage-nrc-dynaudio-special-forty](../sources/soundstage-nrc-dynaudio-special-forty.md), [eac-dynaudio-emit-20](../sources/eac-dynaudio-emit-20.md)) and measure similarly: f3 of 52 Hz and 83.2 dB. The Special Forty is a 2017 anniversary model now in the Editions series ([mfr-dynaudio-special-forty](../sources/mfr-dynaudio-special-forty.md)).

## Patterns
Both models measure 2.8 dB under the 86 dB claim ([eac-dynaudio-special-forty](../sources/eac-dynaudio-special-forty.md), [eac-dynaudio-emit-20](../sources/eac-dynaudio-emit-20.md)). Minimum impedance is 4.9 and 5.5 Ω against a 6 Ω rating, so neither is flagged. Both have listening-window deviation in the 'good' band (2.2 and 2.6 dB). Four of the six current models screened have no Tier A/B data. User pages are anecdotal: 3 comments on the Special Forty ([users-dynaudio-special-forty](../sources/users-dynaudio-special-forty.md)) and 1 on the Emit 20; no QC failures reported in either ([users-dynaudio-emit-20-2026-10-11](../sources/users-dynaudio-emit-20-2026-10-11.md)).
