# Acoustic Energy

## Overview
Acoustic Energy's only paged model is the AE100 Mk2, the entry standmount of the 100 Series Mk2 with HDF cabinets and a WDT waveguide tweeter ([mfr-acoustic-energy-ae100-mk2](../sources/mfr-acoustic-energy-ae100-mk2.md)). It is rated 6 Ω rather than 8 Ω and sells at about $499 per pair. Erin's Audio Corner measured a listening-window deviation of ±2.0 dB, f3 of 64 Hz and a preference score of 5.0 (7.2 with sub) ([eac-acoustic-energy-ae100-mk2](../sources/eac-acoustic-energy-ae100-mk2.md), Tier A).

## Patterns
One measured model only, so no brand-level pattern can be stated. For that model, measured sensitivity (83.6 dB) falls 3.4 dB short of the 87.0 dB claim ([eac-acoustic-energy-ae100-mk2](../sources/eac-acoustic-energy-ae100-mk2.md)), and the 4.7 Ω minimum is below the IEC floor for a 6 Ω rating. Critics ([stereonet-acoustic-energy-ae100-mk2](../sources/stereonet-acoustic-energy-ae100-mk2.md), [ecoustics-acoustic-energy-ae100-mk2](../sources/ecoustics-acoustic-energy-ae100-mk2.md), [hifireport-acoustic-energy-ae100-mk2](../sources/hifireport-acoustic-energy-ae100-mk2.md)) praise soundstage and value but flag bright lower treble and amplifier sensitivity. No user-report page ingested.
