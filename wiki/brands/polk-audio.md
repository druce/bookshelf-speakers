# Polk Audio

## Overview
Polk's paged models are Monitor XT, Signature Elite, Reserve and T-series budget standmounts, with Terylene dome tweeters and Dynamic Balance cones; the Reserve series adds a Pinnacle ring radiator and Turbine Cone ([mfr-polk-audio-reserve-r100](../sources/mfr-polk-audio-reserve-r100.md), [tntaudio-polk-audio-xt15](../sources/tntaudio-polk-audio-xt15.md)). Polk states 4- and 8-ohm compatibility rather than a nominal for the ES, XT and Reserve lines; only the T15 is rated 8 Ω, so the table's Rated column is per this wiki's recording, not a Polk nominal.

## Patterns
- 6 of 6 measure a minimum of 3.6-4.1 Ω: 3 effectively-4ohm (R100, R200, ES20) and 3 below-iec.
- 5 of 6 fall 0.3-1.6 dB under claimed sensitivity; ES20 measures 1.2 dB above (87.2 vs 86.0).
- Listening-window deviation is 1.0-1.9 dB on five models (R100 flattest); the T15 is the outlier at 7.1 dB with preference 0.3.
- Users: ES20, R100, R200, T15 pages each hold fewer than 5 anecdotal reports and no QC problems ([users-polk-audio-reserve-r100](../sources/users-polk-audio-reserve-r100.md), [users-polk-audio-reserve-r200](../sources/users-polk-audio-reserve-r200.md), [users-polk-audio-signature-elite-es20](../sources/users-polk-audio-signature-elite-es20.md), [users-polk-audio-t15](../sources/users-polk-audio-t15.md)).
