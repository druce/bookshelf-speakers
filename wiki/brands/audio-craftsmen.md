# Audio Craftsmen

## Overview
Per its manufacturer page, Audio Craftsmen is a Toronto-based direct-sale brand (Pahmer Audio) that debuted at AXPONA 2023, using SEAS drivers in double-walled "split and angled" cabinets ([mfr-audio-craftsmen-victoria](../sources/mfr-audio-craftsmen-victoria.md)). The Victoria's price fell from $5,999 at launch to $3,990 on the same page. Erin's Klippel data places it at the edge of the "good" window with moderate bass extension ([eac-audio-craftsmen-victoria](../sources/eac-audio-craftsmen-victoria.md)).
