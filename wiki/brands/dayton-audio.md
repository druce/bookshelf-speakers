# Dayton Audio

## Overview
Dayton Audio is the Parts Express house brand; the OPAL1 is its first upscale finished hi-fi bookshelf, with an MMAG woofer and dual passive radiators ([[mfr-dayton-audio-opal1]]). It was clearanced to $499 against a listed $1,038.99 MSRP. Erin's Audio Corner measured f3 of 51 Hz, ±2.6 dB listening window and a 75.4 dB sensitivity ([[eac-dayton-audio-opal1]], Tier A).
