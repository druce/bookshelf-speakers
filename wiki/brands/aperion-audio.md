# Aperion Audio

## Overview
Aperion Audio sells direct in the US with a 30-day home audition; its Verus III Concert V8B is a 6 Ω two-way with an 8" woofer, bi-amp terminals and a treble jumper ([mfr-aperion-audio-verus-iii-concert-v8b](../sources/mfr-aperion-audio-verus-iii-concert-v8b.md)). Erin's Klippel measurement is the only Tier A data and records a mid-range scoop and treble resonances ([eac-aperion-audio-verus-iii-concert-v8b](../sources/eac-aperion-audio-verus-iii-concert-v8b.md)); HomeTheaterHifi, by contrast, calls it a winner ([hometheaterhifi-aperion-audio-verus-iii-concert-v8b](../sources/hometheaterhifi-aperion-audio-verus-iii-concert-v8b.md)).
