# Ascend Acoustics

## Overview
Ascend Acoustics is a US direct-sale maker that bought a Klippel NFS in 2021 and tunes its V2 generation designs on it ([[mfr-ascend-acoustics-cbm-170-se2]]). The three measured standmounts (CBM-170 SE2, Sierra-1 V2, Sierra-LX) span $468 to $1,598 per pair and all show a listening-window deviation of ±1.8 dB or better. The Sierra-LX is described as the flagship standmount with a 6 in aluminium-cone SEAS woofer ([[mfr-ascend-acoustics-sierra-lx]]).

## Patterns
- Sensitivity: 2 of 3 models measure 5.9 to 6.4 dB below the 87.0 dB claim (Sierra-1 V2 at 81.1, Sierra-LX at 80.6); the CBM-170 SE2 is within 0.5 dB. The maker's in-room sensitivity claims run 4–6 dB above anechoic measurements ([[mfr-ascend-acoustics-cbm-170-se2]]).
- Impedance: 1 of 3 (CBM-170 SE2, 3.4 Ω) is flagged effectively-4ohm; Sierra-1 V2 holds 7.0 Ω; Sierra-LX has no measured minimum.
- LW deviation: 3 of 3 within ±1.8 dB, all rated good or better by the §7 scale.
- Evidence: Erin's Audio Corner measured two models ([[eac-ascend-acoustics-sierra-1-v2]], [[eac-ascend-acoustics-sierra-lx]]) and ASR the CBM-170 SE2 and Sierra-1 V2 ([[asr-ascend-acoustics-cbm-170-se2]], [[asr-ascend-acoustics-sierra-1-v2]]).
- User reports: one page ([[users-ascend-acoustics-sierra-lx]]), under five reports, anecdotal; no QC problems recorded.
