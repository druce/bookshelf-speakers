# Vandersteen

## Overview
Vandersteen (Hanford, CA) follows a first-order, time-coherent philosophy; the VLR is its compact standmount ([mfr-vandersteen-vlr](../sources/mfr-vandersteen-vlr.md)). ASR called it the second most broken speaker it had measured, with a 3.4 dB window, 111 Hz f3 and 2.6 preference score ([asr-vandersteen-vlr](../sources/asr-vandersteen-vlr.md)); Tone Audio in 2015 praised its coherence and ease of drive ([toneaudio-vandersteen-vlr](../sources/toneaudio-vandersteen-vlr.md)).
