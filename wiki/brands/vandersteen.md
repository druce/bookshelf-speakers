# Vandersteen

## Overview
Vandersteen (Hanford, CA) follows a first-order, time-coherent philosophy; the VLR is its compact standmount ([[mfr-vandersteen-vlr]]). ASR called it the second most broken speaker it had measured, with a 3.4 dB window, 111 Hz f3 and 2.6 preference score ([[asr-vandersteen-vlr]]); Tone Audio in 2015 praised its coherence and ease of drive ([[toneaudio-vandersteen-vlr]]).
