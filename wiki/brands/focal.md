# Focal

## Overview
Focal is a French maker (Theva is made in France) whose paged models span the entry Theva, mid-range Kanta, top-below-Utopia Sopra, and the Dome Flax home-theatre satellite ([[mfr-focal-theva-n1]], [[mfr-focal-kanta-n1]], [[mfr-focal-sopra-n1]], [[mfr-focal-dome-flax]]). The Kanta and Sopra use beryllium tweeters and have NRC data ([[soundstage-nrc-focal-kanta-n1]], [[soundstage-nrc-focal-sopra-n1]]). US prices for the Kanta and Sopra rose over time (to $8,598 and $12,998 at one retailer).

## Patterns
All four are rated 8 Ω; minimums are 4.5, 3.8, 5.0 and 3.3 Ω, so all four carry an impedance flag (two effectively-4ohm). Measured sensitivity is below claim in all four: by 0.5, 1.5, 1.2 and 5.0 dB. ASR rejected the Dome Flax for uneven response and distortion ([[asr-focal-dome-flax]]). The Sopra notes record claimed 41 Hz vs about 50 Hz measured ([[audioholics-focal-sopra-n1]]). No owner reports exist for the Dome Flax ([[users-focal-dome-flax]]); no other Focal user pages ingested.
