# Neumi

## Overview
Neumi (Neumitech) sells through Amazon at budget prices; the BS5 is a 5" 6 Ω front-ported model and the Silk 4 a 4" rear-ported model priced at $189.99 on the maker page ([mfr-neumi-bs5](../sources/mfr-neumi-bs5.md), [mfr-neumi-silk-4](../sources/mfr-neumi-silk-4.md)). Erin measured both with port-related midrange resonances ([eac-neumi-bs5](../sources/eac-neumi-bs5.md), [eac-neumi-silk-4](../sources/eac-neumi-silk-4.md)).

## Patterns
- 2 of 2 measure well under claimed sensitivity: BS5 82.5 vs 87.3 dB (4.8), Silk 4 82.2 vs 85.0 dB (2.8).
- 2 of 2 have minimum impedance below the IEC floor (4.1 Ω on a 6 Ω rating; 5.7 Ω on an 8 Ω rating).
- Listening-window deviation 3.5 and 2.4 dB; f3 68 and 73 Hz.
- Users: Silk 4 has only 23 Amazon ratings, anecdotal; no failure reports for either ([users-neumi-silk-4](../sources/users-neumi-silk-4.md), [users-neumi-bs5](../sources/users-neumi-bs5.md)). BS5 first-party Amazon listing was unavailable on 2026-10-11.
