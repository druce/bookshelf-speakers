# Bowers & Wilkins

## Overview
Bowers & Wilkins is represented by the 700 S3 and 800 D4 standmounts, priced $3,800 to $14,000 per pair, all using a tweeter-on-top design with carbon or diamond domes per the maker pages ([mfr-bowers-wilkins-705-s3](../sources/mfr-bowers-wilkins-705-s3.md), [mfr-bowers-wilkins-805-d4](../sources/mfr-bowers-wilkins-805-d4.md)). Data comes from Stereophile, SoundStage/NRC and Hi-Fi News (Tier A/B), not full spinorama, so listening-window and f3 are blank. Critics consistently describe detail and imaging with a raised treble ([whathifi-bowers-wilkins-705-s3](../sources/whathifi-bowers-wilkins-705-s3.md), [hifinews-bowers-wilkins-805-d4](../sources/hifinews-bowers-wilkins-805-d4.md), [soundstage-bowers-wilkins-805-d4-signature](../sources/soundstage-bowers-wilkins-805-d4-signature.md)).

## Patterns
- 4 of 4 models measure a minimum impedance below the IEC floor of 6.4 Ω (3.9-4.7 Ω) despite an 8 Ω rating; 3 of 4 are flagged below-iec and the 705 S3 effectively-4ohm.
- 3 of 4 fall 2.0-3.9 dB short of the claimed 88 dB sensitivity (705 S3 Signature, 805 D4, 805 D4 Signature); the 705 S3 measures 88.9 dB ([soundstage-bowers-wilkins-805-d4-signature](../sources/soundstage-bowers-wilkins-805-d4-signature.md), [hifinews-bowers-wilkins-705-s3-signature](../sources/hifinews-bowers-wilkins-705-s3-signature.md), [stereophile-bowers-wilkins-705-s3](../sources/stereophile-bowers-wilkins-705-s3.md)).
- Stereophile heard hardening with a current-hungry amp on the 805 D4 Signature ([stereophile-bowers-wilkins-805-d4-signature](../sources/stereophile-bowers-wilkins-805-d4-signature.md)).
- User reports: 3 for the 805 D4, anecdotal, no QC problems ([users-bowers-wilkins-805-d4](../sources/users-bowers-wilkins-805-d4.md)).
