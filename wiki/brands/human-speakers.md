# Human Speakers

## Overview
HUMAN Speakers is a one-person New Hampshire builder that makes its own drivers; all systems are specified at nominally 6 Ω ([[mfr-human-speakers-81-dk]]). The 81 dk is 18.5 in tall, marginally over the guideline, and is kept with a note. ASR found crossover and driver-resonance problems ([[asr-human-speakers-81-dk]]).
