# PS Audio

## Overview
PS Audio's Aspen line comprises the FR30, FR20 and FR10 floorstanders and the FR5, the only two-way standmount; all share a 2.5 in planar-magnetic tweeter. The line is sold direct with a 30-day trial and a 3-year warranty ([[stereophile-ps-audio-aspen-fr5]]). Erin's Audio Corner measured ±2.7 dB listening window and f3 of 51 Hz ([[eac-ps-audio-aspen-fr5]]).
