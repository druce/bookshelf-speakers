# Buchardt Audio

## Overview
Buchardt Audio sells direct to consumers and prices in EUR with shipping and taxes included; there is no dealer network ([[stevehuff-buchardt-audio-e50]]). The E50 is the flagship standmount with a Purifi woofer and passive radiators; the maker page says "4/6 ohms" and it is treated as 6 Ω ([[mfr-buchardt-audio-e50]]). Erin's Audio Corner measured f3 of 42 Hz and ±1.9 dB listening-window deviation ([[eac-buchardt-audio-e50]], Tier A).
