# Totem Acoustic

## Overview
Totem builds its speakers in Canada (Montreal); the Signature One replaced the Model 1 Signature in 2017, and its crossovers are hand-built with a long break-in recommended ([[mfr-totem-acoustic-signature-one]]). The paged models run from the $599 Kin One entry compact to the $2,300 Bison Monitor and $3,800 Signature One. Only the Kin One has Erin's Audio Corner spinorama data ([[eac-totem-acoustic-kin-one]]); the other two rest on lab measurements from Hi-Fi News, Stereophile and NRC.

## Patterns
- Sensitivity: 3 of 3 measure below claim: Kin One 83.1 vs 89.0 (5.9 dB), Signature One 83.0 vs 87.5 (4.5 dB), Bison Monitor 84.8 vs 87.0 (2.2 dB) ([[nrc-totem-acoustic-signature-one]], [[hifinews-totem-acoustic-bison-monitor]]).
- Impedance: 2 of 3 below the IEC floor (Kin One 4.4 Ω, Bison 5.4 Ω); Signature One holds 7.7 Ω.
- Ratings: the Kin One is rated 6 Ω; the others 8 Ω.
- Only the Kin One has LW (±2.3 dB), f3 (87 Hz) and preference (3.7; 6.6 with sub).
- Critics praise imaging and rhythm across all three; Stereophile noted a narrow 500-600 Hz disturbance on the Signature One.
- No user-report pages ingested.
