# Triangle

## Overview
Triangle is a French brand; Borea models are made in China and the 40th Anniversary models in France, with horn-loaded tweeters and cellulose paper cones ([[soundstage-triangle-esprit-comete-40th]]). US sales are direct with deep sale discounts ([[mfr-triangle-esprit-comete-40th]]). Both paged models have NRC or Klippel data ([[soundstage-nrc-triangle-esprit-comete-40th]], [[eac-triangle-borea-br03]]).

## Patterns
Minimums are 3.6 Ω (effectively-4ohm) and 4.2 Ω (below-iec) against 8 Ω ratings. Measured sensitivity is 88.0 and 87.0 dB against 90.0 claimed (2.0 and 3.0 dB short). Five of the seven models screened have no Tier A/B data. Measurement reviewers (ASR, Erin) did not recommend the BR03 while others praised value ([[asr-triangle-borea-br03]], [[whathifi-triangle-borea-br03]]). About 10 BR03 user reports: 7 positive, 3 of 4 ASR listeners found it bright; no QC problems ([[users-triangle-borea-br03]]).
