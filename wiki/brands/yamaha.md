# Yamaha

## Overview
Yamaha's NS line piano-gloss finish and build quality are praised ([audioholics-yamaha-ns-800a](../sources/audioholics-yamaha-ns-800a.md)). The NS-800A is the two-way sibling of the NS-2000A, rated 6 Ω with a 3.8 Ω minimum claimed by the maker ([mfr-yamaha-ns-800a](../sources/mfr-yamaha-ns-800a.md)). Erin's Audio Corner measured ±2.2 dB listening window and f3 of 57 Hz ([eac-yamaha-ns-800a](../sources/eac-yamaha-ns-800a.md), Tier A).
