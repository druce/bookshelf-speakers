# SVS

## Overview
SVS, built on subwoofer heritage, sells direct and through dealers, manufactures in China and gives a 5-year warranty ([mfr-svs-prime-bookshelf](../sources/mfr-svs-prime-bookshelf.md)). The Prime Bookshelf has spinorama data from Erin ([eac-svs-prime-bookshelf](../sources/eac-svs-prime-bookshelf.md)); the Ultra Evolution Bookshelf, with time-aligned sloped cabinet and diamond-coated tweeter, was measured by Stereophile and is notable for 40 Hz f3 ([stereophile-svs-ultra-evolution-bookshelf](../sources/stereophile-svs-ultra-evolution-bookshelf.md)).

## Patterns
- 2 of 2 are effectively-4ohm: 3.9 Ω on an 8 Ω label (Prime) and 3.7 Ω on a 6 Ω rating (Ultra Evolution).
- Sensitivity is within 0.4 dB of the 87 dB claim on both (86.6; 87.0).
- Prime's claimed 48 Hz compares with a measured 70 Hz f3 ([eac-svs-prime-bookshelf](../sources/eac-svs-prime-bookshelf.md)).
- Prime users: no QC problems mentioned ([users-svs-prime-bookshelf](../sources/users-svs-prime-bookshelf.md)).
