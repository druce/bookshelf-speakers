# Avantone Pro

## Overview
Avantone Pro is a pro-audio brand; the CLA-10 is a Chris Lord-Alge-endorsed passive sealed monitor in the NS-10M mould at $699 per pair ([[mfr-avantone-pro-cla-10]]). ASR measured a ±2.8 dB listening window, f3 of 131 Hz and a preference score of 2.2 (5.9 with sub) ([[asr-avantone-pro-cla-10]], Tier A). The maker page was not retrievable, so specs come from retailers.

## Patterns
One model only. Measured sensitivity (90.2 dB) matches the 90.0 dB claim, and minimum impedance is 6.8 Ω, above the IEC floor. Critics ([[musictech-avantone-pro-cla-10]], [[soundonsound-avantone-pro-cla-10]]) describe an NS-10-like character that is brighter than the original and tiring. User reports ([[users-avantone-pro-cla-10]]) record no QC or reliability problems; push-on terminals were noted as less robust by Sound on Sound.
