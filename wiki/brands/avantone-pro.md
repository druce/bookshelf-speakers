# Avantone Pro

## Overview
Avantone Pro is a pro-audio brand; the CLA-10 is a Chris Lord-Alge-endorsed passive sealed monitor in the NS-10M mould at $699 per pair ([mfr-avantone-pro-cla-10](../sources/mfr-avantone-pro-cla-10.md)). ASR measured a ±2.8 dB listening window, f3 of 131 Hz and a preference score of 2.2 (5.9 with sub) ([asr-avantone-pro-cla-10](../sources/asr-avantone-pro-cla-10.md), Tier A). The maker page was not retrievable, so specs come from retailers.

## Patterns
One model only. Measured sensitivity (90.2 dB) matches the 90.0 dB claim, and minimum impedance is 6.8 Ω, above the IEC floor. Critics ([musictech-avantone-pro-cla-10](../sources/musictech-avantone-pro-cla-10.md), [soundonsound-avantone-pro-cla-10](../sources/soundonsound-avantone-pro-cla-10.md)) describe an NS-10-like character that is brighter than the original and tiring. User reports ([users-avantone-pro-cla-10](../sources/users-avantone-pro-cla-10.md)) record no QC or reliability problems; push-on terminals were noted as less robust by Sound on Sound.
