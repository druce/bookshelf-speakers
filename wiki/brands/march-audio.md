# March Audio

## Overview
March Audio is an Australian made-to-order maker selling direct, using Purifi and SB Acoustics drivers, a waveguide tweeter and passive-radiator bass, with a 5-year warranty ([[mfr-march-audio-sointuva-awg]]). The Sointuva AWG has the strongest measured numbers in this group: 1.5 dB window, 37 Hz f3 and preference 6.95 (8.45 with sub) ([[eac-march-audio-sointuva-awg]]).
