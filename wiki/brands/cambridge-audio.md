# Cambridge Audio

## Overview
The SX series is Cambridge Audio's entry speaker range, with the SX-50 at $229 and the SX-60 at $329 on the maker's US page ([mfr-cambridge-audio-sx-60](../sources/mfr-cambridge-audio-sx-60.md)). The SX-60 is maker-rated 8 Ω, though Hi-Fi World measured about 6 Ω ([mfr-cambridge-audio-sx-60](../sources/mfr-cambridge-audio-sx-60.md)). Evidence is critic-level ([hifiworld-cambridge-audio-sx-60](../sources/hifiworld-cambridge-audio-sx-60.md), [whathifi-cambridge-audio-sx-60](../sources/whathifi-cambridge-audio-sx-60.md), [audioappraisal-cambridge-audio-sx-60](../sources/audioappraisal-cambridge-audio-sx-60.md)); there is no Tier A data.

## Patterns
Single paged model.
