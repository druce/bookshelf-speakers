# Cambridge Audio

## Overview
The SX series is Cambridge Audio's entry speaker range, with the SX-50 at $229 and the SX-60 at $329 on the maker's US page ([[mfr-cambridge-audio-sx-60]]). The SX-60 is maker-rated 8 Ω, though Hi-Fi World measured about 6 Ω ([[mfr-cambridge-audio-sx-60]]). Evidence is critic-level ([[hifiworld-cambridge-audio-sx-60]], [[whathifi-cambridge-audio-sx-60]], [[audioappraisal-cambridge-audio-sx-60]]); there is no Tier A data.
