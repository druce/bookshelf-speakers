# Sony

## Overview
The SS-CS5M2 (2025) replaces the SS-CS5 of 2019 and is a three-way with a 17 kHz super-tweeter, rated 6 Ω, at roughly $250 to $280 per pair ([[mfr-sony-ss-cs5m2]]). Archimago measured a 5.7 Ω minimum and reports it as about 1.2 dB louder than its predecessor with less brightness above 8 kHz ([[archimago-sony-ss-cs5m2]]).
