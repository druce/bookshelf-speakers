# Hsu Research

## Overview
Hsu Research is a factory-direct US maker best known for subwoofers, selling the CCB-8 and MFL-6 as its current speakers ([mfr-hsu-research-ccb-8](../sources/mfr-hsu-research-ccb-8.md)). Both use Don Keele constant-directivity horns, but ASR's Klippel results show rough response and non-constant directivity ([asr-hsu-research-ccb-8](../sources/asr-hsu-research-ccb-8.md), [asr-hsu-research-mfl-6](../sources/asr-hsu-research-mfl-6.md)). The pair costs $558 (MFL-6) and $778 (CCB-8).

## Patterns
- Impedance: 2 of 2 reach about 4 Ω (4.0 and 3.8 Ω), flagged below-iec and effectively-4ohm.
- Response: 2 of 2 exceed ±3.5 dB listening-window deviation (coloured band of the §7 scale).
- Preference: 3.4 and 1.6 (5.6 and 4.0 with sub).
- Sensitivity: CCB-8 measures 88.8 dB against an 88.0 dB claim. Maker figures are quoted in half-space, about 6 dB above full-space ([mfr-hsu-research-ccb-8](../sources/mfr-hsu-research-ccb-8.md)).
- Critics split: Sound & Vision, Audioholics and AV Nirvana liked them; ASR rejected both.
- Users: [users-hsu-research-ccb-8](../sources/users-hsu-research-ccb-8.md) shows 5 of 5 positive owner comments, all from the maker's store; [users-hsu-research-mfl-6](../sources/users-hsu-research-mfl-6.md) has 4 positive comments, also maker-site. Self-selected, anecdotal; no QC issues recorded.
