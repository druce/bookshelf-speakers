# Paradigm

## Overview
Paradigm makes the Founder line in Toronto and follows NRC-style directivity practice but voices treble hot according to ASR ([[mfr-paradigm-founder-40b]], [[asr-paradigm-monitor-se-atom]]). The paged models span the entry Monitor SE Atom, mid-tier Founder 40B and flagship Persona B with beryllium drivers ([[mfr-paradigm-persona-b]]). Paradigm's spec pages state 'compatible with 8 ohms' without a nominal rating.

## Patterns
Minimums are 4.5, 3.4 and 3.5 Ω: one below-iec, two effectively-4ohm. All three measure below claim: by 1.4, 2.1 and 1.8 dB. The Founder 40B scores 4.9 (7.1 with sub) and the Monitor SE Atom 4.1 (6.5). Critics praise imaging and value; ASR and SoundStage's lab note bright or lively treble ([[asr-paradigm-monitor-se-atom]], [[soundstage-nrc-paradigm-persona-b]]). The SE Atom user page has 6 Audio Advice reviews, all 5/5, and no QC reports ([[users-paradigm-monitor-se-atom]]).
