# XSA Labs

## Overview
XSA Labs (formerly Vera-Fi) sells direct only, made to order; the Vanguard is an LS3/5a-inspired sealed two-way with an inverted Harsch crossover ([mfr-xsa-labs-vanguard](../sources/mfr-xsa-labs-vanguard.md)). ASR's Klippel data show a coloured midrange hill, weak bass and low sensitivity ([asr-xsa-labs-vanguard](../sources/asr-xsa-labs-vanguard.md)), while Stereo Times and TNT-Audio praised it ([stereotimes-xsa-labs-vanguard](../sources/stereotimes-xsa-labs-vanguard.md), [tntaudio-xsa-labs-vanguard](../sources/tntaudio-xsa-labs-vanguard.md)).
