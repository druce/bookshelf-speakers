# XSA Labs

## Overview
XSA Labs (formerly Vera-Fi) sells direct only, made to order; the Vanguard is an LS3/5a-inspired sealed two-way with an inverted Harsch crossover ([[mfr-xsa-labs-vanguard]]). ASR's Klippel data show a coloured midrange hill, weak bass and low sensitivity ([[asr-xsa-labs-vanguard]]), while Stereo Times and TNT-Audio praised it ([[stereotimes-xsa-labs-vanguard]], [[tntaudio-xsa-labs-vanguard]]).
