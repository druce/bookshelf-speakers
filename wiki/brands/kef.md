# KEF

## Overview
KEF's in-scope entry is the LS50 Meta, a 5.25 in Uni-Q coaxial with MAT at $1,600 per pair ([[mfr-kef-ls50-meta]]). Erin's Audio Corner and ASR cite smooth coaxial directivity and measured ±1.7 dB listening window with f3 of 64 Hz ([[eac-kef-ls50-meta]], [[asr-kef-ls50-meta]]). Most other KEF models screened are rated 4 Ω and so fall out of scope.

## Patterns
Of 6 KEF models screened, 4 are 4 Ω rated and out of scope, 1 is discontinued and 1 is paged. The paged LS50 Meta measures 3.7 Ω minimum (effectively-4ohm) and 82.7 dB sensitivity against an 85.0 dB claim, i.e. 2.3 dB short; Stereophile also measured it ([[stereophile-kef-ls50-meta-measurements]]). Critics ([[stereophile-kef-ls50-meta]], [[whathifi-kef-ls50-meta]], [[audioxpress-kef-ls50-meta]]) praise transparency and imaging, with limited bass depth. [[users-kef-ls50-meta]] records no QC or reliability reports.
