# Revel

## Overview
Revel is Harman-owned and sold through dealers, with no direct price on its site ([mfr-revel-m16](../sources/mfr-revel-m16.md)). The measured models use waveguide tweeters; the M126Be adds a beryllium tweeter ([mfr-revel-m126be](../sources/mfr-revel-m126be.md)). They span Concerta2 (M16) through Performa3 (M105, M106) to PerformaBe (M126Be), and all four have a listening-window deviation of ±1.7 dB or better. Several have retailer-side availability questions (M16, M105, M126Be show sold-out or discontinued labels at Audio Advice while still listed on Revel US).

## Patterns
- Impedance: 4 of 4 measure below the IEC floor for their rating (M16 4.1 Ω, M106 4.6 Ω, M126Be 4.9 Ω flagged below-iec; M105 3.8 Ω effectively-4ohm).
- Sensitivity: all four measure 1.0 to 1.6 dB under claim (85.0 to 85.7 dB).
- LW deviation: 4 of 4 within ±1.7 dB.
- Preference scores: 5.1 to 5.8 (7.3 to 8.2 with sub), M105 highest.
- Bass: f3 spans 57 to 60 Hz on three models, 93 Hz on the M126Be.
- Measured by Erin, ASR and NRC; three rigs agreed on the M106 ([asr-revel-m106](../sources/asr-revel-m106.md), [soundstage-nrc-revel-m106](../sources/soundstage-nrc-revel-m106.md), [stereophile-revel-m106](../sources/stereophile-revel-m106.md)).
- Users: [users-revel-m106](../sources/users-revel-m106.md), [users-revel-m105](../sources/users-revel-m105.md), [users-revel-m16](../sources/users-revel-m16.md), [users-revel-m126be](../sources/users-revel-m126be.md) together hold roughly a handful of reports, all anecdotal; no QC problems recorded.
