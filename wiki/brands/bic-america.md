# BIC America

## Overview
BIC America is a budget US brand; the Venturi DV62si lists at $275 MSRP with street prices around $160-230 and a 7-year parts warranty ([mfr-bic-america-venturi-dv62si](../sources/mfr-bic-america-venturi-dv62si.md), [users-bic-america-venturi-dv62si](../sources/users-bic-america-venturi-dv62si.md)). ASR criticised its directivity and treble ([asr-bic-america-venturi-dv62si](../sources/asr-bic-america-venturi-dv62si.md)). It is the lowest-priced speaker among this group of brands.
