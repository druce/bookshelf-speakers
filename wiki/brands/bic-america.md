# BIC America

## Overview
BIC America is a budget US brand; the Venturi DV62si lists at $275 MSRP with street prices around $160-230 and a 7-year parts warranty ([[mfr-bic-america-venturi-dv62si]], [[users-bic-america-venturi-dv62si]]). ASR criticised its directivity and treble ([[asr-bic-america-venturi-dv62si]]). It is the lowest-priced speaker among this group of brands.
