# Zu Audio

## Overview
Zu Audio (Utah) is known for high-efficiency full-range-driver speakers; the Method is its first coaxial 8 in standmount, front-ported, sold direct and priced per speaker with a claimed 600-hour burn-in ([mfr-zu-audio-method](../sources/mfr-zu-audio-method.md)). Erin's Audio Corner Klippel data show a coloured response and crossover-region problems ([eac-zu-audio-method](../sources/eac-zu-audio-method.md)); Positive Feedback found it lively with wide imaging ([positivefeedback-zu-audio-method](../sources/positivefeedback-zu-audio-method.md)).
