# SoundStage!: Focal On Wall 302 (2022-08-01)

## Summary
Subjective review, no measurements. Schneider used the 302s on the optional stands in two rooms (TV system and a music-only stereo room), 15 inches from the wall in the latter, so he says they lacked wall reinforcement. He concluded the 302 is a "great choice" for people who want movie and music sound with a discreet wall-mounted form.

## Key claims (Tier D, attributed)
- Clear midrange, clean and spirited but not too bright highs.
- Not much output below about 50 Hz; bass-heads would want a subwoofer. Reasonable extension with good impact and detail otherwise.
- Consistent tonal balance away from the sweet spot; wide dispersion impression.
- Played loud even from a modest receiver (Emotiva BasX TA1) and NAD C 399.
- Launch pricing quoted: US$1,499 each (301: US$999 each); stands US$499/pair.
- Reservation: floorstanders at similar money give deeper bass; Focal Chora 806 suggested for music-only buyers.

## Method notes
Listening only, stand-mounted rather than wall-mounted; no measurements, no wall-voicing statement.

## Reliability notes
Single reviewer, loaner or review sample; values quoted from maker. Price is now US$1,749 each.
