# HiFi Knights: Buchardt E50

## Summary
Dawid Grzyb praises exemplary build, great sonics and strong value. His sound notes (from page 3, saved as `raw/reviews-critic/hifiknights-buchardt-audio-e50-p3.md`, model-extracted) describe a relaxed, warm, dynamic speaker with deep controlled bass and a big power reserve.

## Key claims
- Bass deep, controlled and composed without wall coupling; voicing relaxed, juicy and warm.
- Treble integrates well with the waveguide tweeter; calmer and warmer than his lean, bright reference monitors.
- Dynamics, scale and force are the strongest assets; soundstage bold but not as deep as a point-source speaker.
- A high-output-impedance tube-hybrid amp gave limited bass control; he suggests a stronger class-D amp for a leaner, faster sound.
- Verdict: punches well above its roughly EUR 3,950 price (as paraphrased).

## Method notes
Tube-leaning reference system (Trilogy amp, LampizatOr DAC). No measurements. The raw page-1 file holds only the introduction, so sonic claims rely on the page-3 extract.
