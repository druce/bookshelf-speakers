# SoundStage!/NRC: Paradigm Persona B measurements (2017-12-01)

## Summary
Anechoic-chamber measurements at Canada's NRC (grille on). Page text gives only one number, a listening-window sensitivity of 86.9 dB; the rest is in graphs, read visually here. The listening window is fairly uneven with a broad lift around 8-10 kHz and a dip near 2-3 kHz; impedance dips to about 3.4 Ω.

| min_impedance_ohm | ~3.4 Ω @ ~45 Hz and ~170 Hz | graph-read (approx.) |

## Method notes
Mic between tweeter and mid-woofer; measured at 2 m, plotted at 1 m; listening window = on-axis, +/-15 degrees H and V. Only response curves, no CTA-2034 spinorama or beamwidth data, so no directivity metrics or preference score.

## Reliability notes
Tier A anechoic (NRC) but graph-only, single sample pair from the review. Page forbids reproduction, so only paraphrased graph readings are recorded.
