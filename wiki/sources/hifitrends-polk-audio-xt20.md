# HiFi Trends: Polk Monitor XT20 (2022-04-07)

## Summary
Purchased pair auditioned on an Audiolab 6000A Play, 10 ft listening distance, 3.5 ft from the rear wall, toed in. Scores 7/10 for build, features and sound.

## Key claims
- Tonal balance: laid-back, slightly warm but not boomy; sweet treble, mids the strongest area; some low-frequency coloration and slight boxiness.
- Dynamics: a little soft, with constrained punch on fast complex passages.
- Imaging/stage: good imaging, average width; large sweet spot for the class.
- Build: solid for the price; rap test quiet.
- Verdict: enjoyable, balanced budget hi-fi speaker at $299.

## Method notes
Listening only; no measurements.

## Reliability notes
Single reviewer, bought the sample; the page carries an affiliate Amazon link.
