# EAC: Chesky Audio LC1 review (2025-07-08)

## Summary
Klippel NFS review of a loaned pair (bought direct by a viewer). Erin found a recessed 300-700 Hz region, a bass roll-off with f3 about 71 Hz, severe vertical lobing below the tweeter axis, and audibly mismatched samples; he does not recommend it without EQ and a subwoofer. Impedance is high and easy to drive. Page lists review date 2025-04-19 in the body; published metadata 2025-07-08.

## Method notes
Klippel Near-Field Scanner, reference point at the tweeter, no grille, broken-in. Two samples measured because imaging sounded unstable; spinorama shown for both, primary data is sample 1. Distortion plots are in dB relative to fundamental; percentages converted by the wiki. Erin's subjective notes (Tier D): dull, hollow, thin vocals, little bass without wall reinforcement; best aimed straight at the listener close to a wall. Passive radiators ineffective below about 80 Hz in-room per Erin. Crossover judged first-order with a Zobel network.

## Reliability notes
Loaned units, not paid. Sample-to-sample mismatch (midwoofer 1-3 kHz, dip about 1.2 kHz and peak about 3 kHz in sample 2) means a single pair may not represent production. Summary text on the page is AI-generated; numbers cross-checked with graphs where possible.
