# What Hi-Fi?: Focal Theva No.1 (2025-09-11)

## Summary
Four-star review (Sound 4, Build 4, Compatibility 4). Praised for scale, authority and midrange; held back by strong rivals (Acoustic Energy AE300 Mk2, KEF Q3 Meta).

## Key claims
- Big, authoritative, not forward; full-bodied, smooth tonal balance; focused, layered imaging.
- Bass rich and full with good articulation; good weight and punch.
- Treble slightly exposed and a little thin or tizzy with sibilant or electronic sounds.
- Lacks some verve and rhythmic precision versus class leaders; loses full-bodied balance at low volume.
- Compatibility: 8 Ω nominal, dips to 4.6 Ω (per maker); benefits from an amp with some grunt. At least 40 cm from rear wall; slight toe-in.
- Listed spec: rear-ported, 39 x 21 x 26 cm, 7 kg, 89 dB/W/m.

## Reliability notes
Subjective listening only; no measurements. Rated against competitors, which colours the verdict.
