# Hi-Fi Choice: Focal Kanta N°1

## Summary
A positive UK review at £4,500 per pair: clean, open, agile, but limited at very high volume by the single 165 mm driver. Rating and date are not on the saved page.

## Key claims (attributed, paraphrased)
- General "cleanliness" and a translucent, open sound; imaging precise and soundstage deep and wide.
- Beryllium tweeter fast and propulsive, good for beat-driven music; bass synth rendered dry but supple with crisp note starts and stops.
- On loud, bass-heavy rock the mid/bass driver struggles and the sound turns "a little dynamically flat".
- Works best over 0.5 m from the rear wall; modest toe-in; port bung supplied.
- Called it "without doubt a fine standmount", but at a price where it must be a serious performer.
