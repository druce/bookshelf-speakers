# What Hi-Fi?: Q Acoustics 3020i

## Summary
Favourable review of the 3020i as a larger, more detailed step up from the 3010i and an improvement over the original 3020. Verdict: great first speakers.

## Key claims (attributed)
- Describes a rich, warm sound with full bass, precise treble and sweeping dynamics; treble more refined than the older 3020.
- Says bass has solidity without help from a back wall; best tonal balance with some room behind, though near-wall placement only costs a little openness.
- Rival Dali Spektor 2 judged a touch clearer in the midrange and more rhythmically precise.
- Cons: deep cabinet (just shy of 28 cm), which complicates stand placement.

## Method notes
Listening in What Hi-Fi?'s test rooms; no measurements. Star rating not captured in the saved text.

## Reliability notes
House reviewer; the "warm" description sits against ASR's finding of high-frequency emphasis ([asr-q-acoustics-3020i](asr-q-acoustics-3020i.md)).
