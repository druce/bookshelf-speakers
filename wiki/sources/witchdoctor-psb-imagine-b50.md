# Witchdoctor: PSB Imagine B50 (2025-08-20)

## Summary
Gary Steel awards a Gold Badge, calling the B50 beautifully coherent with nimble, tuneful bass; priced NZ$1,499.

## Key claims
- Bass sounded present and tonally correct, even on electronic material he expected to need a subwoofer (Steel).
- Sweet, non-fatiguing treble on bright recordings.
- Not ideal for rock and loudness seekers; less girth and power than floorstanders (Steel).
- Needs sturdy stands and space around the rear slot port; lists 87 dB sensitivity (room figure from maker).
- Reviewed with a JAVA Single Shot integrated amp.

## Method notes
Listening only, NZ market, no measurements.

## Reliability notes
Single reviewer; enthusiastic tone; "practically flawless" claim is subjective.
