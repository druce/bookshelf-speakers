# SoundStage! Hi-Fi: PSB Alpha P3 (2019-02-01)

## Summary
Schneider finds the P3 remarkably clean and detailed above the low bass for its launch price ($199/pair), with extended, pristine highs, but audibly limited bass.

## Key claims
- Bass: heard nothing below about 60 Hz; piano's lowest notes missing; still punchy at upper bass when played loud (Schneider).
- Treble: highs reached with clarity he would expect from $1,000/pair speakers.
- Midrange: voice as clear as Q Acoustics 3020i and Paradigm Atom (both larger, 6.5" woofers).
- Verdict: for budget-minded audiophiles who value quality over quantity, a gem.

## Reliability notes
Subjective Tier D at launch price. Review predates ASR's measurements showing resonances. No measurements.
