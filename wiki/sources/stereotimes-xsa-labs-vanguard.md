# Stereo Times: XSA Labs Vanguard (Greg Voth)

## Summary
Enthusiastic review with extensive designer background (Viet Nguyen); the author wants to buy a pair for near-field use. Publication date not shown (written around the 2022-23 launch).

## Key claims
- Reviewer described imaging as wide and deep, with speakers disappearing; best with no toe-in.
- Reported surprising bass depth for the size, setup dependent.
- Designer says the woofer plays up to ~5.5 kHz for coherence; inverted Harsch crossover chosen for a LS3/5a-like step response.
- Grilles removed for critical listening for a more energetic top end.
- Launch price 799/pair (first 25 pairs).

## Reliability notes
Subjective only, no measurements; contradicts ASR's measured tonal balance and bass. Single sample.
