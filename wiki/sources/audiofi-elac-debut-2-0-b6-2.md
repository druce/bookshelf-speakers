# audioFi.net: ELAC Debut 2.0 B6.2 (Performance 9/10, Value 9/10)

## Summary
Described as honest, musical and easy to drive and place, a clear step up from the original B6 in mids and highs.

## Key claims
- Bass needed about 150 hours of run-in to open up; still less impactful than the original B6 (reviewer suspects the front port).
- Detailed, uncoloured mids and treble.
- Poor recordings are not flattered.

## Reliability notes
Tier D; independent Malaysian site, one reviewer, modest equipment chain.
