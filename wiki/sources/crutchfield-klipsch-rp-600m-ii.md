# Crutchfield customer reviews — Klipsch Reference Premiere RP-600M II

## Summary
217 Crutchfield reviews: 182 five-star, 22 four-star, 10 three-star, 3 two-star. Treble is the divisive point (11 call it bright or fatiguing, 13 explicitly not); bass is praised for the size (~29) and 7 note it is easy to drive. No driver or electrical failures.
