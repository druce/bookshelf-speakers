# The Absolute Sound: Revel M126Be (page dated 2019-01-29)

## Summary
Gader finds the M126Be a refined, more resolving evolution of the M106, forwardly voiced with a slightly cool timbre, and one of the most rewarding small speakers he has reviewed. Page date as shown on the site; review price $4,000/pair.

## Key claims (attributed)
- Tonally neutral like the M106 but with more low-level resolution, timbral realism and top-end air and speed.
- Forwardly voiced, slightly cooler cast; unforgiving of poor recordings.
- Beryllium treble airy, open, not brittle; tweeter integrates with woofer almost like a point source.
- Near-imperturbable midrange dynamics, though traces of compression appeared at high output.
- Bass quick and defined with little port artifact; perceivable output near 50 Hz, bottom octave out of reach; paired well with a GoldenEar SuperSub X.
- Soundstage scale and image specificity are its most impressive virtues; suggests experimenting with wall distance.

Subjective listening only; no measurements.
