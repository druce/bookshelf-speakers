# Richer Sounds blog: Bronze 50 (7G)

## Summary
A retailer-employed reviewer likes the 7G Bronze 50: wide, airy soundstage, precise imaging, clarity-leaning tonal balance, bass limited by size but punchy and tight.

## Key claims (attributed)
- Highs crisp but not sharp; open, expressive mids; warm, intimate vocals; "less bright than they used to be".
- Bass tight and musical, a sub helps in larger rooms; can be pushed to their limits.
- Scales with better amplification.

## Reliability notes
Retailer with a commercial interest; treat as weak Tier D.
