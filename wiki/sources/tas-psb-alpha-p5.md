# TAS: PSB Alpha P5 (Neil Gader, 2020-07-27)

## Summary
Gader praises the P5 as a naturalistic, non-fatiguing budget compact with a smooth tweeter and well-weighted midrange; it is ideal for a small den, limited in extension and scale. Price quoted $399/pair at the time.

## Key claims
- Tonal balance: commendably neutral, nicely weighted mids, non-fatiguing treble, little evidence of peaks and bumps.
- Bass: tuneful for the size but little below about 50 Hz; solid 60-70 Hz; port quick with minimal coloration.
- Imaging: fairly precise; drivers integrate; soundstage small-scale.
- Dynamics: plays loud enough in small rooms; congestion when pushed.
- Verdict: "does nothing wrong-headed"; a prime candidate for a later subwoofer.

## Reliability notes
Subjective Tier D; contrasts with ASR's measurement-based criticism.
