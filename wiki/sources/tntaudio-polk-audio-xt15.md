# TNT-Audio: Polk Monitor XT15 listening test (Feb 2026)

## Summary
Listening comparison against budget rivals (Eltax Monitor 1, Lonpoo LP42MX, Wiibo Karino 200) and Indiana Line Tesi 2. Cadeddu preferred the XT15 over the Eltax, and finds it open and articulate with surprisingly deep, powerful bass for its size and no flattering voicing.

## Key claims
- Tonal balance: crisp, clear mid-high, powerful and extended bass; slightly light in lower vocals and lacking harmonic richness at the top.
- Dynamics: very good, handles demanding tracks and fills medium rooms; port chuffing acceptable.
- Imaging: soundstage not wide or deep, but outlines are well defined.
- Build: internally careful, shielded drivers, basic exterior; 5-year warranty.
- Easy load: reports a 4.1 Ω minimum from the manual (maker claim) and 4.385 kg actual weight per speaker.
- Verdict: honest and fairly accurate; good for a second system or first system.

## Method notes
Listening only, no measurements in the article. Speakers about 50 cm from the rear wall, 3 m listening distance.

## Reliability notes
Single reviewer, independent magazine with no advertising; price quoted in euros (about 209 EUR/pair).
