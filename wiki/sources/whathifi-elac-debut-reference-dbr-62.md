# What Hi-Fi?: Elac DBR62

## Summary
Scores 4/5 sound, 4/5 compatibility, 5/5 build. Praised for transparent midrange and driver integration; criticised for subdued drive and a need for capable partnering.

## Key claims
- Midrange clean, clear and delicate, not forward or etched; vocals nuanced.
- Seamless driver integration; tweeter waveguide gives refined, insightful highs.
- Bass blends well and gives solidity, but rhythmic drive and dynamic punch are subdued on harder music.
- Congested close to a rear wall; start about 50 cm out. Revealing: suggests Rega Brio / Cambridge CXA61 class amplifier.
- Verdict: cerebral, "admire rather than love" for some.

## Method notes
Listening only; specs repeated from maker (6 ohm, 86 dB, 44 Hz-35 kHz).

## Reliability notes
Price at review time GBP 499 / USD 600; now USD 799.
