# EAC: Klipsch RP-500M II review (2022-06-10)

## Summary
Klippel NFS measurement of a loaned (Audio Advice) pair, grille off. The speaker has a lifted top octave with a 3-4 dB class peak around 10-15 kHz, modest bass (f3 about 66 Hz) and an impedance that falls to about 3.3 Ω above 80 Hz. Erin judges it clearly better than the first-generation RP-500M in the midrange, notes Klipsch's 92 dB rating is closer to 85 dB anechoic, and suggests toeing out about 30° to tame treble. Best at moderate levels and long distances.

## Method notes
Klippel Near-Field Scanner, reference plane at the tweeter, grille off for main data (grille-on set also published). Distortion at 86 and 96 dB @1 m; instantaneous compression sweep referenced to 76 dB with 86/96/102 dB steps. Impedance at 0.10 V RMS. The page's graph file names say "RP-500M I" but titles and text are RP-500M II (the page compares against "v1"), so this is a labelling slip, not a different model. Erin's listing of "$599 per pair" is the list price at the time.

## Reliability notes
Single loaned sample. No payment; affiliate link to Audio Advice disclosed. THD, compression, impedance figures here are read from graphs. Subjective notes (Tier D): mid-range clarity improved over v1; 10-15 kHz peak can make synths strained; suits moderate volumes at distance; toe out about 30° for smoother treble; buy RP-600M if more output is needed.
