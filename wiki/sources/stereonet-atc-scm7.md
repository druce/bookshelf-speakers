# Stereonet: ATC SIA2-100 / SCM7 system review

## Summary
UK system review (SCM7 £875). The reviewer likes the SCM7 as a small-speaker with vital presence; I only read the first part of the page.

## Key claims
- Classic British small-speaker sound: rhythmic agility, tonal purity, vital and sharp guitars and horns.
- Bass "practically indestructible" with large woofer excursion, but cannot match larger speakers for punch.
- Needs a muscular solid-state amp; low sensitivity noted (claim 84 dB).

## Reliability notes
Undated, author not shown, paired with ATC amp; Tier D.
