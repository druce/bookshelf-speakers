# Erin's Audio Corner: March Audio Sointuva AWG (2023-11-25)

## Summary
Klippel NFS measurement of a manufacturer-loaned pair, measured with the reference axis 7° above the tweeter at the designer's request. Erin calls the sound excellent and the bass remarkably deep for a standmount; he flags low sensitivity and wide-then-narrowing directivity (about ±70° up to 4 kHz). The page's pasted manufacturer specs are Tier C and kept separate below.

## Method notes
Klippel Near-Field Scanner, CTA-2034 spinorama, reference point 7° above tweeter per maker; speaker broken in. Impedance at 0.10 V. Multitone and compression tests included (plots not read). Erin notes listening at tweeter height dips 2-4 kHz and sounds bright. Erin used 100 W in an 18x14x9 ft room and found it mostly adequate, wanting more for daily levels.

## Reliability notes
Single manufacturer-loaned pair; Erin states no compensation. Computed sensitivity (81.2 dB) is below the 83.0 dB claim; the 7° reference axis may shift level slightly.
