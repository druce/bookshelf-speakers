# Future Audiophile: Chesky Audio LC1 (updated September 2026)

## Summary
Enthusiastic review of the 3D-printed LC1 at about $1,000 per pair; no measurements.

## Key claims
- Describes the sound as well balanced, bordering on neutral, with wide soundstage and accurate imaging (Forst).
- Bass called clean, competent and never boomy for the size; on one dense rock track it sounded flat until paired with a REL T5 subwoofer, which opened it up.
- Silk dome treble described as accurate and smooth; no grilles supplied.
- Downsides listed: one-year warranty, "low" sensitivity, no grille option. Text says 86 dB while the spec table says 84 dB at 2.83 V/1 m (internal inconsistency).
- Cabinet described as airtight with minimal resonance even at high volume; Lucca Chesky is the 18-year-old founder.

## Method notes
Home listening in several rooms and placements; no amplifier model stated in the extracted text. Publication date not shown (update note September 2026).

## Reliability notes
Site uses affiliate links; the review frames the product as a pick for its best-of-2026 list. Claims conflict with Erin's Klippel findings on bass and tonal balance ([[eac-chesky-audio-lc1]]).
