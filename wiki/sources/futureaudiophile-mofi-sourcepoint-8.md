# Future Audiophile review: MoFi SourcePoint 8 (Steven Stone)

## Summary
Stone used the SourcePoint 8 as a nearfield reference for about a month and liked it a lot, recommending it for small rooms or midfield, ideally with a subwoofer. No numeric rating.

## Key claims (attributed)
- Warm, easy tonal balance with excellent dynamic contrast; seductive, detailed midrange.
- Bass does not go lower than expected for the size with two rear ports, but kicks hard; about 80 dB average at the listening position was enough for punch.
- Three-dimensional imaging and ability to disappear rated highly; handled intentionally distorted bass on one track without added strain.
- Considers the larger SourcePoint 10 a better fit for mid or large rooms (lower bass, a few dB more sensitive).
