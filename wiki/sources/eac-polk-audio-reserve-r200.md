# EAC: Polk Reserve R200 review (2023-06)

## Summary
Klippel NFS measurement of a brand-new loaned pair. Erin found it relatively neutral with good tonality, reasonably tight imaging, but limited output and mid-range distortion; some glare near 800 Hz-1 kHz and a resonance around 1 kHz. He recommends aiming it directly at the listener and a 4 Ω-capable amp.

## Method notes
Klippel NFS, grille off (assumed default), reference tweeter plane. Compression sweeps 86/96/102 dB @1 m vs 76 dB, plus multitone distortion (higher distortion at 800 Hz-1 kHz at high output, per text; graph not read). Page date reads Jun 10, 2023 although the header lists 18 Jun.

## Reliability notes
Single loaned new pair. Erin Tier D notes: in-room bass good to ~50 Hz but lacks mid-bass thump; neutral mids with slight glare 800 Hz-1 kHz; artificial top-octave above 10 kHz; compress/distort at higher volume, crossing to a sub at 80-100 Hz helps; ring tweeter narrows at HF so aim at listener without toe-out. Retail cited $750/pair.
