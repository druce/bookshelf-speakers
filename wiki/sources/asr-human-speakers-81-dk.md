# ASR: Human Speakers 81 dk review (2024-01-04)

## Summary
Klippel measurement of a member-loaned $1,270 pair. ASR found a peak before the crossover, a dip at it and a strong resonance after, a woofer resonance forcing its way out and weak tweeter output above about 5 kHz, wide dispersion, high distortion at 96 dB and impedance with several resonances. Not recommended; no listening test.

## Reliability notes
