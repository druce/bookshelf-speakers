# Stereophile: 805 D4 Signature measurements

## Summary
John Atkinson's MLSSA measurements show a textbook woofer alignment, a quiet enclosure and well-controlled lateral dispersion, but a "tailored" high-frequency response with the tweeter balanced up to about 5 dB high and a peak between 9 and 10 kHz. The load is current-hungry, with EPDR falling to about 2 Ω.

## Method notes
Quasi-anechoic farfield (MLSSA, DPA 4006) at 50 inches with nearfield summing below 300 Hz; impedance via Dayton DATS V2; in-room spatial average. Footnoted EPDR method.

## Reliability notes
Single pair. Quasi-anechoic, so lower confidence below about 300 Hz. Reviewed speaker is the Signature version only.
