# ecoustics: AE100 MK2 review (2024-04-20)

## Summary
Ian White found the MK2 balanced, punchy and engaging with excellent soundstage, but harder to drive than the specs suggest and sensitive to amplifier match, toe-in and placement. Lists $499/pair at Amazon.

## Key claims
- A 40 W NAD ran out of gas at higher levels; bass lean and defined, so he moved to a stronger amplifier.
- Sounds more balanced than the predecessor; very punchy midrange; detailed, open tweeter that can be slightly hard with the wrong amp.
- Midrange and lower treble on the brighter side with bite on vocals.
- Preferred little or zero toe-in, neutral-to-warm amplification and placement near a wall for bass reinforcement.
- Imaging precise, soundstage width excellent for the price.

## Method notes
Listening only. Review states the series is sold through selected authorized North American dealers.
