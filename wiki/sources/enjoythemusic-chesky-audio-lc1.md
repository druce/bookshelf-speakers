# Enjoy the Music: Chesky Audio LC1 (July 2025)

## Summary
Very positive listening review, no measurements. Raw file is a model-extracted summary (page body did not scrape).

## Key claims
- Nagle found usable bass to about 40 Hz, better than the 50 Hz claim, but "a bit slower".
- Transparent midrange with well-integrated drivers; treble accurate to 20 kHz.
- Very wide center soundstage; about 25 degrees toe-in and tweeters at ear level improved center depth.
- Recommends powerful solid-state amplification; setup took experimentation.
- Concludes no other sub-$1,000 small monitor he knows matches it.

## Method notes
Listening only; speaker amplifier not named in the extracted text.

## Reliability notes
Paraphrased by a model, not verbatim. Bass-extension claim is subjective and contradicts Erin's measured f3 about 70 Hz ([[eac-chesky-audio-lc1]]).
