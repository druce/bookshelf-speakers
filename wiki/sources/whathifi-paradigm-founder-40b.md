# What Hi-Fi? / Australian Hi-Fi: Paradigm Founder 40B (2022-03-17)

## Summary
Reprint of an Australian Hi-Fi review with an in-depth Newport Test Labs report. The reviewer was highly enthusiastic about imaging and unexpectedly deep bass; the lab report found a flat in-room response, 88.5 dB sensitivity and an impedance dip near 3.1 Ω.

## Key claims (Tier D)
- Reviewer described uncoloured, monitor-like sound with a holographic soundstage and superb imaging.
- Bass depth and quality described as surprising for the size; verdict: the reviewer would buy them. Listed cons: acronym-heavy marketing, lowish impedance.

## Method notes
Newport Test Labs: in-room pink noise, anechoic HF with and without grille, near-field LF with port separated.

## Reliability notes
Lab is the magazine's contractor, method differs from Klippel NFS; LF is in-room/near-field. Review was written for Australian Hi-Fi; the page's reviewer byline is not captured.
