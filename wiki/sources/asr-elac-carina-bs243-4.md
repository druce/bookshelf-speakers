# ASR: Elac Carina BS243.4 review (2022-05-28)

## Summary
Klippel NFS review of a loaned pair (member loan, stated street price US $999 at the time). Amir finds wide, even directivity and good build, but a response hole near 2.8 kHz at the AMT/woofer crossover and low sensitivity (about 83 dB, roughly 4 dB below average). He recommends it, noting the dip is easily corrected with EQ. Distortion shows a narrowband resonance near 1.9 kHz.

## Method notes
Klippel NFS, reference axis at tweeter centre, no grille mentioned, 17 deg C. Listening notes (Tier D, attributed): wide halo, excellent clarity, slightly bright; with two EQ filters preferred; sub-bass attempts were audibly distorted; could be a nice centre speaker for its dispersion. Vertical performance has the usual two-way non-coaxial problem, so aim the tweeter at ear height.

## Reliability notes
Single loaned sample. The ASR thread (page 2) debates whether the 2.8 kHz hole is woofer cone breakup rather than the tweeter; unresolved. No max-SPL/compression test.
