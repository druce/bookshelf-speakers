# Audiophilia: Amphion Argon1 (Star Component Award)

## Summary
Charles Brown awards the Argon1 a Star Component Award at US$1,400/pair, praising expansive imaging, tonal uniformity and off-axis performance.

## Key claims
- Wide horizontal field and impressive imaging; clear, fast bass for the size.
- Tonal uniformity and off-axis capability "almost impossible to match" in the class.
- No audible coherence problems in the midrange despite a small metal-cone woofer; low 1.6 kHz crossover credited.
- Specs quoted: 5.25" aluminium woofer (133 mm), 1" titanium tweeter, 315 x 160 x 265 mm, 7 kg.

## Method notes
Listening on Tri-Art class D integrated and a Mytek DAC.

## Reliability notes
Single reviewer, strongly positive; US price from 2021 is stale.
