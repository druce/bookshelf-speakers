# Sound & Vision: Hsu Chimera/SC and MFL-6 (2025-10-31)

## Summary
Subwoofer-led review that added five MFL-6 speakers as a 5.1 set. Wilson began sceptical of horn tweeters but found the MFL-6 smooth, detailed and easy to listen to, able to keep up with the loud subwoofer. Placement needs care because of the angled panel.

## Key claims
- Called the speakers "incredibly smooth and precise"; no harsh or "cupped hands" horn sound.
- Played loud without audible distortion; quoted a 92 dB sensitivity rating (conditions unstated).
- Aiming discipline needed: the 45° canted front panel and down-firing port affect placement; used a wedge under the centre.
- Included a mounting template; build and satin paint were praised.

## Method notes
Listening only; no measurements. Review is mainly about the subwoofers.

## Reliability notes
Single reviewer who received samples from Hsu; strongly disagrees with [[asr-hsu-research-mfl-6]] on treble character. Not a standalone MFL-6 review.
