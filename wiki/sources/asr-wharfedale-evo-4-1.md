# ASR: Wharfedale EVO 4.1 review (2021-11-21)

## Summary
Klippel NFS review of a purchased sample ($799). Amir finds a stepped response with a wide resonance near 3.8 kHz traced to the AMT tweeter in the crossover region, strong upper-treble beaming (large AMT), and distortion bumps at 2-4 kHz. He says the speaker sounds like "marketing sound" and does not recommend it.

## Method notes
Klippel NFS, reference axis at AMT centre. Room 15 °C. Best vertical response about 10° off axis, though ASR doubts it matters. Listening test (Tier D): after an accidentally disconnected tweeter initially sounded warm, the connected speaker was very bright and forward on vocals; with EQ still not enjoyable; severely distorts sub-bass at medium to high level (use a high-pass filter); preferred the Revel M16 in direct comparison.

## Reliability notes
Single purchased sample (partly member-funded); no manufacturer involvement. Impedance and THD read from graphs.
