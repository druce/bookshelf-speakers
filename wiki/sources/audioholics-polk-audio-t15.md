# Audioholics: Polk T15 review (2018-06-29)

## Summary
Outdoor, gated free-air measurement plus listening. Larson found the T15 sounded veiled when level with the ear and much better at about 20° above or below tweeter height, which he says is a deliberate Polk design choice (confirmed by Polk's VP of engineering). He rates it 3.5/5 performance and 4.5/5 value, recommends it for budget surrounds and wall mounting, and warns of a mid-range "cupped" character.

## Method notes
Free-air 7.5 ft high at 1 m, gated at 8.5 ms (resolution lost below about 250 Hz, accuracy lost below 110 Hz), 1/24-oct smoothing; ground-plane bass. No spinorama presented. Tier B (gated).

## Reliability notes
Single sample. Reviewer evaluated at $100 list/$69 sale price. Outlet's rating weights value.
