# AVMentor: ATC SCM19 lab evaluation (2016)

## Summary
Independent lab page with a stated method: quasi-anechoic response above 300 Hz at 2 m, woofer nearfield spliced below. Finds a well-balanced speaker, low sensitivity (about 83 dB), 5.6 Ω minimum impedance, and bandwidth close to ATC's claims.

## Method notes
Quasi-anechoic at 2 m with and without grille (grille effect negligible); nearfield woofer splice; impedance, step, CSD, polars, panel acceleration. Graphs were not downloaded; values are from the text.

## Reliability notes
Single sample, publication year inferred from URL (2016); likely the "v2" generation. No CTA-2034 spinorama. The -6 dB figure (40 Hz) is lower than ATC's 54 Hz and the -3 dB figure; treated with caution.
