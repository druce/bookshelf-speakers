# B&W US: 805 D4 Signature product page

## Summary
Manufacturer page for the Signature version of the 805 D4 (upgraded crossover, motor, top plate and tweeter grille). On 2026-10-11 the B&W page showed $14,000 per pair with a "be first to know" stock form rather than Add to Cart. World Wide Stereo's feed listed the Midnight Blue pair at $14,000 as available, so it is treated as current; stock is inconsistent (Audio Advice shows it sold out at $7,000 per each).
