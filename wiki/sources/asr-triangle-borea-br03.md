# ASR: Triangle Borea BR03 review (2020-03-25)

## Summary
Klippel NFS measurements of a member-supplied pair (US $499 on Amazon at the time). Amir finds an uneven response: a bump near 100 Hz, boosts at 500-1000 Hz, further resonances and tweeter peaking, with discontinuities near 1.19 kHz and 20 kHz. He attributes it to a shallow first-order-like woofer filter letting the woofer and port resonances play too long. He could not decide by ear whether it was good, did not recommend it, but noted it plays loud without bottoming out.

## Method notes
Klippel NFS, tweeter-centre reference axis, grille off, over 1000 points; error below 1% except about 2% near 2 kHz. Listening in mono, EQ trials not effective. ASR advises thick carpet because floor bounce accentuates highs and 600-900 Hz, and tweeter near ear level (vertical directivity).

## Reliability notes
Single owner-supplied sample, reviewer admits uncertainty about his own listening verdict. Tier D listening notes: warm at first, midrange "grungy", highs stand out, slightly boomy, not recommended.
