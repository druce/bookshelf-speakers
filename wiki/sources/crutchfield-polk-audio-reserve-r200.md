# Crutchfield customer reviews — Polk Audio Reserve R200

## Summary
99 Crutchfield reviews: 91 five-star, 7 four-star, 1 three-star. Strong, tight bass for the size (at least 23, often no sub needed) is the headline; 9 call the treble clear but not harsh. 4 say it needs good or 4 ohm-capable amplification. No driver failures. Counts are hand-coded from the clipped page; "keyword-coded" tallies are approximate.
