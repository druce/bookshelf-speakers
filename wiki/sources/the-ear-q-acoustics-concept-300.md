# The Ear: Q Acoustics Concept 300 (Jason Kennedy, 2019-06-20)

## Summary
UK listening review praising the built-in spring isolation and the quiet cabinet. Kennedy found it open and warm, with generous bass, and noted it wants a powerful amplifier.

## Key claims (Tier D)
- Open, spacious, revealing in a relaxed, warm way; port gives a little warmth to the bass.
- Low sensitivity (84 dB) for a 6 Ω speaker; dynamics restrained with a Naim SuperNait 2, better with an ATC P2 power amp.
- Slight forwardness offset by toeing out so axes cross behind the head.
- Compared with PMC and Russell K speakers: those were tighter and cleaner but drier.
- Judged well worth its asking price (GBP 2,999 then).

## Reliability notes
Single reviewer, no measurements; tested without the treble-reduction jumper.
