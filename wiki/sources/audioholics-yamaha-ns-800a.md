# Audioholics: Yamaha NS-800A (James Larson, 2025-06-17)

## Summary
4.5/5 performance and 4.5/5 value review. Free-air gated measurements show a fairly flat response with a high-treble spike (12-20 kHz, centred ~16 kHz), very wide even dispersion, mild crossover waistbanding, a benign impedance load and sensitivity of 86.2 dB. Larson found balanced, accurate sound, impressive bookshelf bass and excellent imaging, with limited dynamic range for a $4.8k pair.

## Method notes
Free-air at 7.5 ft height, 1 m, 11 ms gate: resolution lost below ~250 Hz, accuracy lost below ~110 Hz; 1/24-octave smoothing. Therefore Tier B. No LW deviation or f3 stated.

## Key claims (Tier D)
- Reviewer described sound as balanced and accurate; vocals sharply imaged.
- Bass impressive for a bookshelf, though a sub would add weight; woofer reaches limits at high levels.
- Dynamic range limited for the money; not for filling large rooms or headbanging.
- Compared with NS-2000A, midrange here is flat rather than recessed.
- Piano-gloss finish and build praised.

## Reliability notes
Loaner from Yamaha (disclosed as offered by Yamaha). Single review author.
