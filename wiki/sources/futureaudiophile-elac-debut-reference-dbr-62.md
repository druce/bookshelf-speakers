# FutureAudiophile: ELAC DBR62 (page updated 2026-09)

## Summary
Enthusiast review with affiliate links, listened to on a Yamaha A-S301 with an SVS SB-1000 sub. Positive on midrange, imaging and balance.

- Imaging described as better than most in class.
- Bass tight; deeper extension limited by size, best with a subwoofer.
- At 86 dB sensitivity wants a powerful amplifier for loud listening; placement matters.
