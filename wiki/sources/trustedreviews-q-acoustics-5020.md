# Trusted Reviews: Q Acoustics 5020

## Summary
Positive review: rich, musical, powerful, with more scale than the 3000i series, but not the most analytical.

## Key claims (attributed)
- Describes taut yet agile bass, warm rich tone and a spacious stereo image similar to the 3020i's character.
- Says it lacks the precision and sharpness of the Concept 30 (from memory, reviewer admits).
- Cons: big cabinet for a "bookshelf"; some may prefer a more revealing sound.
- Notes bungs for the rear port and a recommended 0.2 m wall gap.

## Method notes
Listening only with Cambridge Audio CXN v2 and CXA61. Repeats maker specs (53 Hz-30 kHz, 87.9 dB, 3.3 Ω minimum).

## Reliability notes
"Rich/warm" contrasts with Erin's "treble heavy" Klippel note ([eac-q-acoustics-5020](eac-q-acoustics-5020.md)).
