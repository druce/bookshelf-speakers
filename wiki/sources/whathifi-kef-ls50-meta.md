# What Hi-Fi?: KEF LS50 Meta (first reviewed Sept 2020, updated Nov 2024)

## Summary
Five stars on sound, compatibility and build. Called the class leader at the price for transparency and precision.

## Key claims (attributed)
- What Hi-Fi?: gained clarity and finesse over the original; renders low-level detail convincingly.
- Full-bodied for the size but limited depth; the larger R3 Meta reaches lower.
- Tonality smooth and balanced with enough high-frequency bite.
- Expansive, tightly focused, stable soundstage; punchy bass.
- Rarely stressed unless volume is high; needs an amp with some grunt (3.5 Ω minimum, 85 dB).
- Place ~50 cm from the wall and not in a bookshelf.

## Reliability notes
Panel-style outlet; spec sheet in the article lists 7.2 kg weight (KEF: 7.8 kg).
