# What Hi-Fi?: Elac Debut 2.0 B6.2 (Sound 5, Compatibility 4, Build 5)

## Summary
Praised as mature, transparent and rhythmic, adding no character of their own; warns they are unforgiving of poor sources. An update note says the B6.2 has been replaced by the Debut 3.0 series but remains available.

## Key claims
- Open, well-balanced range; more bass weight than the B5.2 but visibly limited at the deepest bass.
- Punchy, rhythmic, nothing pushed forward; dynamics simply follow the source.
- Holds up at low volume.
- Reveals flaws in weak amplification or sources, so not ideal for a budget micro system.

## Reliability notes
Tier D, subjective. Update note is the evidence for the Debut 3.0 replacement.
