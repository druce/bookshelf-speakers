# Role Audio: The Skiff (maker page)

## Summary
The maker's Drupal-era pages (role-loudspeakers.com/skiff and /prices) still list the Skiff at $495/pair satin black and $545 birch, with an order form. The main roleaudio.com site is a JavaScript app that could not be scraped. ASR (2023-09) states it is still manufactured and sold; Amazon's single-unit listing showed "currently unavailable" in search results (not verified live).
