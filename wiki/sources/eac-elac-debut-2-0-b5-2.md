# Erin's Audio Corner: ELAC Debut 2.0 B5.2 (2022-11-16)

## Summary
Klippel NFS review of a self-purchased pair. Erin found a +3 dB broadband step near 1.5 kHz that makes the speaker sound uneven, plus noticeable compression and midrange distortion even with an 80 Hz high-pass. He would recommend it only with EQ (a high-shelf fixes much of it) and prefers the B6.2 if not using EQ. Page body is dated Sep 4, 2022; the index date used here is 2022-11-16.
