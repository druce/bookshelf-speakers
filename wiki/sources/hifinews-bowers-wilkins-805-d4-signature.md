# Hi-Fi News lab report: B&W 805 D4 Signature

## Summary
Compared with the standard 805 D4, the Signature's dished response is more pronounced (a full 8 dB midrange dip preceding a boosted presence and treble peaking at 10 kHz). Sensitivity falls short of the claimed 88 dB, and the impedance minimum is 4.53 Ω with a modest EPDR of 2.1 Ω.
