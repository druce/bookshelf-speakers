# Crutchfield customer reviews — ELAC Debut 2.0 B6.2

## Summary
337 Crutchfield reviews: 287 five-star, 36 four-star, 11 three-star, 1 two-star, 2 one-star. Clarity (~112) and bass good for the size (at least 29, often no sub needed) dominate; treble tone splits. Three driver or electrical failures were reported (3 of 337). Text for about 20 of the oldest flagged reviews was cut off, so counts are a lower bound. ## QC / reliability
C70 (1 star): tweeter failed after about 6 months. C165 (1 star): within 2 years one speaker began shorting the receiver at high volume. C279 (3 stars): distortion in one tweeter on piano at mid-high volume, possibly a single unit. Cosmetic: grille retaining pins break easily (C139); vinyl veneer chipped and peeled after about 7 falls from stands (C136); vinyl wrap a con (C116). Driver or electrical failures: 3 of 337.
