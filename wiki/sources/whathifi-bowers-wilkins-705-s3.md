# What Hi-Fi?: 705 S3 (2022-11-11)

## Key claims
- Scores: sound 4, build 5, compatibility 4 (overall star rating not captured in the raw text).
- Praised the clean, low-distortion, highly resolving and spacious sound, "no unwanted bright edges".
- Criticised a somewhat cool and detached presentation, limited rhythmic drive and low-level dynamics; tuned to sound best at higher volumes.
- Recommends about 1 m from the wall and slight toe-in; found KEF LS50 Meta more engaging at lower cost.
- Launch price cited as $3,400.

## Reliability notes
Subjective, with a large reference room and high-end partner gear.
