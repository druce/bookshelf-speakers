# Stereophile: Totem Signature One measurements (2017-12-28)

## Summary
Atkinson's bench measurements show easy-load impedance, estimated sensitivity 3 dB below claim, panel resonances near 490 and 600 Hz plus strong port-pipe modes, wide even horizontal dispersion to 6 kHz, and a crossover-region suckout above the tweeter axis. He concludes it measured better than its Model 1 predecessors apart from the cabinet and port resonances.

## Method notes
