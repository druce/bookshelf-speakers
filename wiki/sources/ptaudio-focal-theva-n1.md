# pt.AUDIO: Focal Theva N1 (2024-08-11)

## Summary
Favourable but qualified review: recommended for TV, movies and relaxed music; a good second pair rather than a critical-listening first choice. "Reviewer's Choice" graphic, no numeric score.

## Key claims
- Midrange rich and detailed; smooth crossover. More mid-bass than his Vandersteens, so voices bloom and can approach muddy.
- Low extension weaker than his reference; some tracks lacked low-end punch.
- Treble described as soft and precise; relaxed, cozy presentation; transients lost some punch.
- Initial boxiness faded after a couple of days. Driven by Hegel H80.

## Reliability notes
Capture is model-extracted, not verbatim. Single reviewer, no measurements. Contrasts with Audioholics and What Hi-Fi? on treble (soft vs slightly exposed).
