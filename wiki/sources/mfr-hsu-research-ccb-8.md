# Hsu Research CCB-8 manufacturer page

## Summary
Hsu sells the CCB-8 factory-direct at $389 each (one speaker per order line; $778 per pair). It is a ported 8" coaxial constant-directivity horn speaker designed with Don Keele, rotatable to sit vertically or horizontally. The saved capture lacked the spec tab; the spec values below come from a WebFetch extraction of the live page on 2026-10-11 (model-extracted, not verbatim).
