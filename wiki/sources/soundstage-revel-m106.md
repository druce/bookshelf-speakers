# SoundStage!: Revel Performa3 M106 review (2015-04-01)

## Summary
Philip Beaudette praised the M106 as extremely high quality: ultraclear, open and neutral midrange, crisp but not bright highs, good imaging, composed at high volume. Bass was tight and disciplined but a little lightweight until moved nearer the front wall. Highly recommended at $2,000 pair.

## Key claims
- Bass quality emphasised over quantity; "a bit lightweight" until placed closer to the front wall; not for those wanting fat bass.
- Squeaky-clean, uncompressed at high levels; never strained.
- Midrange transparency the greatest strength; highs extended, neither bright nor rolled off.
- Preferred the Revel F206 floorstander for deeper bass; the Amphion Argon3L had fuller bass but he judged the M106 the more accurate.
- Claimed specs listed: 8 Ω nominal, 87 dB/W/m, 50-150 W, LF limits 59/54/39 Hz (-3/-6/-10 dB), crossover 2.3 kHz.

## Method notes
Review used a Bryston B135 SST2 integrated. Measurements by NRC are on [soundstage-nrc-revel-m106](soundstage-nrc-revel-m106.md).

## Reliability notes
Subjective, single reviewer, 2015 sample of the original-production model.
