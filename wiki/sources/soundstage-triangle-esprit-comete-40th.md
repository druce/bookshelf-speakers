# SoundStage!: Triangle 40th Anniversary Comete review (Diego Estan, 2021-09-15)

## Summary
Estan praised the midrange and refined treble, and rated imaging and transparency as nearly on par with Revel M126Be standmounts, but found bass light in extension and weight. He recommends pairing with a sub if bass matters. Reviewed at $2,200 per pair; no numeric rating.

## Key claims (attributed)
- Midrange: forward yet lush, silky, with palpable vocals; he preferred it to the Revel M126Be on voices.
- Treble: neutral, never bright or etched, unlike the Borea BR03.
- Bass: light versus similar two-ways; in-room -3 dB point 38 Hz vs 33 Hz for the BR03 (in-room REW, Tier B-style, room-influenced).
- Dynamics: played cleanly to about 95-100 dB peaks; a 300 W amp never exceeded about 30 W.
- Sensitivity: about 3 dB higher than Revel M126Be in his room (level matching).
- Design notes: 3.8 kHz crossover (per review), 6.5 in cellulose midwoofer, magnesium-dome horn tweeter, dual front ports, made in France; maker claimed 49 Hz-22 kHz, 92 dB (1 W), 4.2 Ω minimum.

## Method notes
Listening in a treated 15 x 12 ft room, 9 ft triangle, about 15 deg toe-in, grilles off, McIntosh MC302 amp, 24 in stands.

## Reliability notes
Single reviewer; measurements are in the companion NRC page ([[soundstage-nrc-triangle-esprit-comete-40th]]). The 92 dB claim in the review differs from the current maker figure of 90 dB.
