# Stereophile review: 705 S3 (Kalman Rubinson, 2023-06-20)

(Page 2 also saved: raw/reviews-critic/stereophile-review-p2-bowers-wilkins-705-s3.md.)

## Key claims
- Reviewer found satisfying bass for the size, great detail and a wall-to-wall soundstage; little toe-in expanded the stage and avoided on-axis brightness.
- Mid to upper bass clean, full and tight; lowest bass and physical impact of big speakers absent; a Fauré Requiem opening chord lost its lowest components.
- Elevated upper midrange and treble aided clarity and soundstage; only at very high levels did it seem bright, and it stayed distortion-free.
- Vs Revel F206 (floorstander): B&W treble more detailed, Revel better bass; vs KEF Blade 2 Meta: KEF far more extended bass. Suggests a good subwoofer to complete it.
- Conclusion: midrange and treble resolution outstanding, bass musically satisfying; small rooms and closer wall placement help bass. Used 24" FS-700 S3 stands (reviewer's samples).

## Reliability notes
Single reviewer in a room he describes as less favourable than a smaller damped one. Subjective (Tier D). Paired with [[stereophile-bowers-wilkins-705-s3]] measurements.
