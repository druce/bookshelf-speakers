# Stereophile: Elac Carina BS243.4 (2019-12-26)

Additional raw pages: raw/measurements/stereophile-elac-carina-bs243-4-page-2.md, ...-specifications.md, ...-measurements.md.

## Summary
Reichert found it the most refined speaker he knows at the price, with a very smooth octave-to-octave balance, and notes it needs about 12 in from the wall. Atkinson's quasi-anechoic MLSSA measurements show a superbly flat mid and treble, sensitivity below spec, a 54 Hz port tuning, and a 4.6 Ω impedance minimum. Directivity: wide horizontally, narrow vertically.

## Method notes
DRA Labs MLSSA, DPA 4006 mic far-field at 50 in, Earthworks QTC-40 near-field, no grille. Quasi-anechoic, so lower confidence below ~300 Hz (Tier B).

## Reliability notes
Sample serial 32302E000037/38. Review is Tier D for sound: Reichert listened on 24 in stands, five feet apart, with Rogue Stereo 100, Schiit Aegir and Line Magnetic LM-518 IA amps; preferred the tube amp's treble. Compared with KEF LS50: Elac more refined and rolled off, KEF more punchy.
