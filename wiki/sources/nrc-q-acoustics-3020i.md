# SoundStage!/NRC: Q Acoustics 3020i measurements (2019-06-15)

## Summary
Anechoic-chamber measurements at Canada's National Research Council, published alongside the SoundStage! review. Sensitivity is 85.6 dB and the listening window shows a bass roll-off that reaches about -3 dB near 80 Hz.

## Extracted values
| Field | Value | Note |
|---|---|---|
| sensitivity_db_2v83 | 85.6 dB | stated; 300 Hz-3 kHz average of listening window, 2.83 V / 1 m |
| min_impedance_ohm | ~3.5 Ω near 250 Hz | graph-read (approx.) |
| f3 | ~80 Hz | graph-read (approx.) from five-point listening-window plot, no value stated |
| LW flatness | roughly +1.5 / -3 dB, 100 Hz-20 kHz | graph-read (approx.), not stated |
| thd_n @ 90 dB | ~3% near 100 Hz; ~1% above 150 Hz | graph-read (approx.), THD+N, measured at 2 m; test level is 90 dB, not 86/96 so not entered in frontmatter |

## Method notes
Five-microphone array (on-axis, 15° left/right/up/down), measured at 2 m and plotted at 1 m, grille off, microphone between tweeter and woofer. Off-axis out to 75° plotted; deviation-from-linearity at 90 dB vs 70 dB was published (not read).

## Reliability notes
Single sample from the manufacturer via SoundStage!. ASR's Amir called the graphs messy but well correlated with his Klippel data. Pre-dates the current 3020c.
