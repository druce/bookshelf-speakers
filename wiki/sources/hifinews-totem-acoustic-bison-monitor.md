# Hi-Fi News: Totem Bison Monitor (2023-06-23)

Raw pages: raw/reviews-critic/hifinews-totem-acoustic-bison-monitor.md (page 1), ...-p2.md (listening), ...-lab-report.md (lab). Lab data Tier B; listening Tier D.

## Summary
Mark Craven rated it Hi-Fi News "Recommended"; the lab found a 5.4 Ω minimum (against an 8 Ω rating), a response bump near 800 Hz-1.3 kHz, and low distortion at 90 dB.

## Key claims (Tier D)
- Craven described tight, quick bass with impressive depth but snap over extension.
- Midrange definition and treble detail "superb"; open soundstage, instruments felt detached from cabinets.
- Little obvious sweet spot; tonal balance held well off-axis; placement easy.
- Wanted a thicker, warmer, larger-scale presentation on one bass-driven rock track.

## Method notes
Hi-Fi News lab: nearfield summed driver/port plus gated freefield response corrected to 1 m, 2.83 V; not a full spinorama.

## Reliability notes
Single pair (L/R). No directivity data. The 850 mm height in the table conflicts with the maker's 333 mm.
