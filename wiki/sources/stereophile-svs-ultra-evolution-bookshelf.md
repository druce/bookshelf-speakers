# Stereophile: SVS Ultra Evolution Bookshelf measurements (Steve Temme, 2026-08-21)

## Summary
Quasi-anechoic measurements by Steve Temme (Listen Inc.). Impedance minimum 3.7 ohm against a 6 ohm rating; sensitivity 87 dB matches the claim; response +/-3 dB from 40 Hz to 26 kHz; THD at 90 dB below 1% above 50 Hz and below 0.5% above 100 Hz. Horizontal directivity is broad up to 15 kHz with lobing at 3-7 kHz.

## Method notes
Conference room 11'11" x 25'3" x 15'8"; speaker on turntable and stand, tweeter axis at 1 m; first floor reflection about 5 ms limits far-field to 200 Hz and above; nearfield spliced at 250 Hz. GRAS 40BD mic, 7-mic vertical array at 15 degree steps. Distortion measured at 17 in and corrected to 1 m for 90 dB.

## Reliability notes
Two review samples (serial numbers in the spec sidebar); gated measurement, so lower confidence below about 300 Hz. The -3 dB point comes from a spliced nearfield, not true anechoic.
