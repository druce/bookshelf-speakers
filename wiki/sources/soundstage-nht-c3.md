# SoundStage! Access: NHT C 3 Carbon Fiber (2020-03-15)

## Summary
Review of the limited-edition carbon-fibre-finish C 3 (same speaker, $1,399.98/pair then). Estan liked the imaging, speed and dynamics, found treble even and mids neutral-leaning but sometimes thin, and bass lacking in weight.

## Key claims
- Imaging "laser-like", smaller and more precisely delineated than the B&W 685 S1.
- Fast attack and recovery; dynamic contrast better than the B&Ws.
- Bass taut but lean; less weight and roundness than the 685 S1.
- Treble even, neither bright nor dull; voices could sound thin with occasional glare.
- Sealed design may tolerate near-wall placement better than ported speakers.
- "A solid performer in every way."
- In-room -3 dB at 33 Hz (miniDSP UMIK-1); in-room, not anechoic, so not entered in the speaker table. The review links NRC anechoic measurements, not retrievable here.
