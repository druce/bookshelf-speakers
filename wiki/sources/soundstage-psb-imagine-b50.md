# SoundStage!: PSB Imagine B50 review (2024)

## Summary
Dennis Burger rates the B50 an unusually capable $699 standmount and gives it a Reviewers' Choice badge. Raw capture is a model-extracted summary, not verbatim.

## Key claims
- Even, neutral midrange and good dispersion (Burger).
- Bass deeper than the cabinet size suggests; works stand-alone in a small (about 10 x 12 ft) room.
- At high volumes little compression; slight cabinet resonance at very high levels that a subwoofer removed.
- Strong value; alternatives named: Monitor Audio Bronze 50 6G, Paradigm Premier 100B.

## Method notes
Listening review; price at review $699/pair. Companion NRC data: [soundstage-nrc-psb-imagine-b50](soundstage-nrc-psb-imagine-b50.md).
