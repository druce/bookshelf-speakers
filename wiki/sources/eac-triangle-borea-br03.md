# Erin's Audio Corner: Triangle Borea BR03 review (2023-08-25)

## Summary
Klippel NFS review of a loaned, broken-in pair. Erin finds the BR03 "okay" rather than good: recessed midrange against a pronounced 4-8 kHz region, resonances and breakups in the upper midrange and lower treble, directivity irregularities near 1.2 kHz (port) and about 4 kHz (crossover), high harmonic and multitone distortion and noticeable compression at high output. Spinorama dataset date is 2023-12-20; the page itself is dated Aug 25, 2023.
