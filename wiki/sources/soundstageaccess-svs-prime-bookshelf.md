# SoundStage! Access: SVS Prime home-theater system (2018)

## Summary
System review in which the Prime Bookshelf served as front L/R. The Bookshelf receives no separate verdict; the whole Prime system is highly recommended. Raw capture is model-extracted (WebFetch), not verbatim.

## Key claims
- Hanada compared two-channel voicing to the Wharfedale Diamond 220, noting just a hint of high-frequency emphasis.
- Bass went lower than the Diamond 220; a sub was optional for two-channel use.
- Easier to drive than the Diamond 220; timbre matched the rest of the Prime line.

## Reliability notes
No independent measurements; spec figures are SVS's. Capture not verbatim.
