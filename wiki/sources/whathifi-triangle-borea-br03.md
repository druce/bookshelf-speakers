# What Hi-Fi?: Triangle Borea BR03 (updated 2025-03-12; originally reviewed 2020)

## Summary
Five stars for sound, 4 for compatibility and build. Praised for sophisticated, detailed sound and scale.

## Key claims
- Sophisticated, detailed sound with a huge sense of scale, larger than B&W 607.
- Bass quantity acceptable for the size; quality and texture good, some may want more muscle.
- Good timing and separation; balanced tonal delivery, not hard or bright, though a very slight treble peakiness; avoid bright electronics.
- Tested with Cambridge Audio CXA61; Triangle suggests at least 40 cm from rear wall.
- Lists 90 dB sensitivity, 4.2 Ω minimum impedance (maker figures).

## Method notes
Listening review; no measurements.

## Reliability notes
Outlet states the original review dates to 2020; this is an updated page. Contrasts with the ASR and EAC measurements (treble and midrange irregularities).
