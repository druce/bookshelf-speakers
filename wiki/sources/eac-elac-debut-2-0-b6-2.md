# Erin's Audio Corner: ELAC Debut 2.0 B6.2 (2022-08-13)

## Summary
Klippel NFS review. Erin called it "quite nice" at about $300/pair, with glare in the 4-6 kHz upper midrange that EQ fixes easily and a smooth ERDI. His one concern was sharp compression below 80 Hz even at moderate levels, confirmed on both speakers of the pair; a subwoofer would remedy it.
