# Archimago: Sony SS-CS5 (M1) vs SS-CS5M2 (2025-11-08)

## Summary
Hobbyist quasi-anechoic comparison of purchased M1 and M2 pairs (no full qSpin was run). Electrical behaviour of the two is nearly identical (min impedance about 5.6-5.7 Ω, box resonance near 62 Hz). The M2 is 1.2 dB louder from 100 Hz to 10 kHz and its above-8 kHz treble bump is significantly reduced versus the M1. Bass extension is the same: -6 dB around 60 Hz. Distortion at 93 dB SPL is mostly under 1% above about 175 Hz.

## Method notes
Listening window = average of on-axis, ±10/20/30° horizontal and ±10° vertical sweeps, quasi-anechoic (gated), miniDSP UMIK-1, grilles ON (unlike Klippel data by others, which is grille off). Impedance measured on his rig. Sensitivity compared by switching speakers at fixed amp gain. Subjective notes (Tier D): reviewer prefers M2 for the calmer treble, finds imaging precise at tweeter height, bass limited, suggests a subwoofer.

## Reliability notes
Single hobbyist sample of each model (M2 build date not discernible; M1 July 2024). Reviewer notes Erin's Audio Corner video showed a 2 dB bass difference between M1 and M2 which he could not reproduce, possibly production variation. Low-frequency data are gated plus nearfield; no numeric f3 or LW deviation published.
