# Stereophile: KEF LS50 Meta (2020-12-16)

## Summary
John Atkinson, who owns the original LS50, found the Meta's low frequencies identical to the original and the treble slightly stronger, with better low-level detail and palpable vocal images. Verdict: highly recommended.

## Key claims (attributed)
- Atkinson: bass sounds identical to the original LS50; clean toneburst reproduction but limited extension and power versus larger standmounts.
- Atkinson: more transparent window into the soundstage and a little more treble energy than the original.
- Atkinson: vocals especially palpable; "reach-out-and-touch" soundstage equal to predecessor.
- Price: just under $1,500/pair at review.
- Amp: Parasound Halo JC 1 monoblocks, on 24" stands.

## Reliability notes
