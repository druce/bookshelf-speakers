# HiFiReport: Revel Concerta2 M16 review

## Summary
Subjective review in a ~75 m² room with an integrated amp. Reviewer found the speaker easy to place, with smooth non-metallic treble, dense midrange and bass "exemplary in this price range"; wide soundstage with no toe-in. Publication date not given (image uploads dated 2025-02).

## Key claims
- Treble: smooth, no metallic character despite aluminium dome; no fatigue.
- Bass: richer than expected for a bookshelf, cannot match floorstanders.
- Soundstage/imaging: wide, enveloping; good in a large room without strain.
- Texture described as slightly relaxed. Mentions a price in the "low $1,300" range (non-US context; not used).
- Notes the glossy finish scratches easily.

## Method notes
Listening only, no measurements.

## Reliability notes
Outlet context unclear (appears to be a translated Asian-market review); distributor-supplied sample likely. Treat as single opinion.
