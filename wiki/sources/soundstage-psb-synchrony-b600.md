# SoundStage!: PSB Synchrony B600 review (2021-11-15)

## Summary
Diego Estan finds the B600 balanced and neutral with remarkably deep bass for a standmount; it carries the Reviewers' Choice designation and he would choose it over the Revel M126Be without a subwoofer.

## Key claims
- Tonally neutral top to bottom, imaging first-rate, large soundstage (Estan).
- Very deep bass: his in-room average measured -3 dB at 25 Hz re 2 kHz; he calls it the best bass he has measured from this type (in-room, not anechoic).
- Played 95-100 dB peaks without strain.
- Versus Revel M126Be ($4,400): same tonality, PSB deeper and punchier in bass and bigger staging; Revel slightly more transparent.
- Price at review $2,499/pair; matching 24" stand $599/pair.

## Method notes
Small treated room (15 x 12 ft), grilles off, toe-in about 15°. NRC anechoic data: [[soundstage-nrc-psb-synchrony-b600]].

## Reliability notes
In-room bass figure includes room gain and must not be credited to the speaker.
