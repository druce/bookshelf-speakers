# SoundStage! Hi-Fi: Q Acoustics Concept 300 (Diego Estan, 2020)

## Summary
Listening review versus the reviewer's Bowers & Wilkins 705 S2. Estan found the Concept 300 neutral to slightly warm, with exceptional bass for a minimonitor, and chose it over his reference.

## Key claims (Tier D)
- Tonal balance essentially neutral, leaning slightly warm and full-bodied; B&W 705 S2 sounded brighter.
- Imaging good but less tightly focused than the 705 S2; images slightly larger.
- In-room -3 dB point 29 Hz by his UMIK-1 measurement (in-room, includes room gain; not anechoic) versus 33 Hz for the 705 S2.
- No audible cabinet coloration; tweeter jumper at -0.5 dB was ideal in his room.
- Conclusion: for a minimonitor it did nothing wrong and almost everything right; worth the price at $4,499/pair then.

## Method notes
Room 15 x 12 x 8 ft, speakers 16 in from wall, toed in 18°, McIntosh amplification. Companion NRC measurements: [nrc-q-acoustics-concept-300](nrc-q-acoustics-concept-300.md).

## Reliability notes
Single reviewer; in-room LF measurement not comparable to anechoic f3.
