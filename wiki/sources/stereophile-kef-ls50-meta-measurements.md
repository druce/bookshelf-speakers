# Stereophile: KEF LS50 Meta measurements (2020-12-16)

## Summary
MLSSA/quasi-anechoic measurements: sensitivity 84.5 dB, EPDR minima of 1.66 Ω (135-140 Hz) and 1.7 Ω (660-725 Hz), inert cabinet, port tuned to 51 Hz, very even 30° horizontal window and uniform dispersion.

## Extracted values
| Field | Value | Note |
|---|---|---|
| sensitivity_db_2v83 | 84.5 dB | stated (B-weighted, 2.83 V/m); within error of claimed 85 |
| epdr_min_ohm | 1.66 Ω @ 135-140 Hz; 1.7 Ω @ 660-725 Hz | stated |
| min_impedance_ohm | not stated in text | impedance only shown in figure |
| port tuning | 51 Hz | stated |
| LW | "superbly even" lower midrange to treble | qualitative, no number |

## Method notes
DRA Labs MLSSA, DPA 4006 mic; far-field gated response, nearfield woofer/port merged below ~300 Hz. In-room spatial average also shown.

## Reliability notes
Two samples auditioned; measurement of one. Quasi-anechoic, so less reliable below ~300 Hz.
