# SoundStage!/NRC: Revel Performa3 M106 measurements (2015-04-01)

## Summary
Anechoic-chamber measurements at the National Research Council of Canada accompanying the SoundStage! review. Stated sensitivity is 85.6 dB (2.83 V / 1 m, 300 Hz-3 kHz listening-window average), below Revel's 87 dB. Charts include on/off-axis responses to 75°, listening window, THD+N at 90 dB, deviation from linearity, impedance and phase. Numeric values beyond sensitivity are graph-read.

## Method notes
NRC anechoic chamber, tweeter-axis measuring position, grille off, measured at 2 m and plotted at 1 m. Listening window is a five-point average (on-axis, ±15° horizontal and vertical).

## Reliability notes
Single sample supplied for the SoundStage! review. The raw capture holds only the measurements page, with values on chart images.
