# ASR: Elac Debut Reference DBR-62 review (2020-03-25)

## Summary
Amir measured a retail-purchased DBR-62 on the Klippel NFS and gave his strongest recommendation, calling the on-axis response flat for a budget speaker with a 610 Hz cabinet resonance and wide horizontal dispersion. Impedance minimum is 5 ohm but phase is acute near 100 Hz, so he advises a strong amplifier.

## Method notes
Klippel NFS, grille off, tweeter axis, 700+ points. Tier D: listening notes, superb balanced sound, impressive bass, resonances only at "ear bleeding" levels; EQ only for a 200 Hz room mode.

## Reliability notes
Single retail-purchased sample. Amp used had 1000 W on tap.
