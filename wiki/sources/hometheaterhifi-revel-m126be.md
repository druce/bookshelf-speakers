# HomeTheaterHifi: Revel Performa M126Be (2018; page shows a 2026-10-07 stamp)

## Summary
Young, self-described non-fan of bookshelf speakers, calls the M126Be among the highest-quality small speakers he has met. Publication year is inferred from the page's 2018 tags and upload paths; the page header shows 2026-10-07.

## Key claims (attributed)
- Treble smooth and extended; beryllium dome matched or beat ribbons and electrostats he had heard recently.
- No bass trickery, no measurable bass bump, so easy to integrate with a subwoofer; bass smooth and undistorted at high levels.
- No audible distortion even at high levels; imaging excellent over a wide range of positions; magnetic grilles did no audible harm.
- Dislikes: price and the piano-black finish (per outlet's summary).
- His own REW/UMIK in-room measurements, which he says confirm what he heard (Tier C/hobbyist; not used for frontmatter).

## Method notes
Listening with Arcam AVR550 and Emotiva amplifiers, sub for about half the listening.
