# Steve Huff: Buchardt E50

## Summary
Enthusiastic blog review after 2-3 weeks of listening. Huff finds a clear, natural, near-neutral and musical speaker with a large soundstage, excellent imaging and very good bass for the size, needing no subwoofer in small-to-mid rooms.

## Key claims
- Sound called sweet, natural, open and three-dimensional with a clear, focused midrange; not plump or juicy.
- Bass solid and tight, "best sounding" for a bookshelf under $10k he has heard; no subwoofer needed unless the deepest bass is wanted.
- Soundstage bigger than any earlier Buchardt; imaging among the best at the price.
- Highs smoother than the Dynaudio Heritage Special, which he finds hotter.
- Easily driven by quality watts in a 13x18 ft room; maker quotes 4-6 Ω and 85 dB; price he cites $4,000-4,300 including shipping and taxes.

## Method notes
Listening with Orchard Audio monoblocks and Eversolo A6 DAC; also Nagra amp; no measurements.

## Reliability notes
Independent blogger; also earns affiliate links; enthusiastic tone. Not a lab source.
