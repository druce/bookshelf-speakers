# SoundStage!/NRC: B&W 805 D4 measurements

## Summary
Anechoic NRC measurements published with the SoundStage! Ultra review. Only one number is given in text (sensitivity 84.4 dB, 300 Hz-3 kHz listening window); everything else is in graphs. The listening window rises about 5-8 dB from the midband to a peak near 9-10 kHz, and the impedance bottoms out around 4.3 Ω.

## Extracted values
| Field | Value | Note |
|---|---|---|
| sensitivity_db_2v83 | 84.4 dB | stated, averaged 300 Hz-3 kHz on listening window, 2.83 V/1 m |
| min_impedance_ohm | ~4.3 Ω @ ~200 Hz (also ~4.4 Ω near 45 Hz) | graph-read (approx.) |
| impedance peaks | ~41 Ω @ 25 Hz, ~45 Ω @ 80 Hz, ~33 Ω @ 1.9 kHz | graph-read (approx.) |
| THD+N at 90 dB (2 m) | ~2% near 100 Hz, ~0.6% or less from about 300 Hz to 5 kHz, ~1% near 7-8 kHz | graph-read (approx.), THD+N incl. noise, relative to a ~93 dB curve |
| listening window | ~88 dB at 100 Hz, ~83-84 dB at 500 Hz-2 kHz, ~91 dB peak near 9 kHz | graph-read (approx.); no deviation figure published |
| f3 / f6 | not stated | not estimated |

## Method notes
Anechoic chamber at NRC Canada; microphone between woofer and tweeter; grille off; measured at 2 m, plotted at 1 m; THD and linearity at 90 dB SPL. Listening window is the five-point average (on-axis, ±15° horizontal and vertical).

## Reliability notes
Single sample supplied by B&W. Reviewer and measurement are from the same site. The graph-read values are approximate; the THD graph is at 90 dB, not 86/96 dB, so the frontmatter THD fields stay null.
