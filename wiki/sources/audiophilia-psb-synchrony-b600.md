# Audiophilia: PSB Synchrony B600 (2022-11-11)

## Summary
Maxwell Geiger awards a Star Component and calls the bass "incredible" for a standmount, with a large soundstage and a balance between precision and musicality.

## Key claims
- Highs initially a bit hot relative to his laid-back Wharfedale Diamonds, then acclimated.
- Soundstage extends beyond the speakers; orchestral detail and dynamics handled well.
- Bass depth far beyond the claimed 50 Hz (-3 dB); needs room to breathe and a capable amplifier (listing says 88 dB sensitivity, 6 Ω nominal, 4 Ω minimum).
- Price at review $2,799/pair.

## Method notes
Two rooms (14 x 20 ft and 13 x 14 ft), several amplifiers, no measurements.

## Reliability notes
Subjective only; sensitivity quoted from the maker's sheet differs from the 86 dB on the current page.
