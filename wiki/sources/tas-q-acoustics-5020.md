# The Absolute Sound: Q Acoustics 5020 (Jason Methfessel)

## Summary
Very positive review at $899 per pair. Methfessel praises low-level detail, dynamics and a balanced, non-fatiguing response while noting a bass roll-off that starts just below 70 Hz.

## Key claims (attributed)
- Describes frequency balance as "flat" with no treble bump, and never fatiguing.
- Finds bass roll-off beginning just below ~70 Hz; thinks the 53 Hz (-6 dB) spec is generous; cello slightly lean.
- Says transients tight, vocal clarity high; slight veiling versus more transparent speakers.
- Powered by a 150 W Hegel H190v; room about 1,600 cubic ft.

## Method notes
Listening only. His "flat" description conflicts with Erin's "treble heavy" Klippel verdict ([eac-q-acoustics-5020](eac-q-acoustics-5020.md)).

## Reliability notes
Loaner conditions not stated in the saved text; price cited ($899) is below the maker's current $1,199.
