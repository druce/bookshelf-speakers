# Erin's Audio Corner: Acoustic Energy AE100 Mk2 review (2023-11-29)

## Summary
Klippel NFS measurement of a viewer-loaned pair. Erin found good directivity but a midrange dip (300-800 Hz), a tweeter sitting about 3 dB above the midrange, and resonances near 300 Hz, 750 Hz and 5 kHz. He concluded it sounds hollow and poor without EQ, despite trying about eight placements, and reported rave reviews elsewhere do not match his experience (Tier D listening notes).

## Method notes
Klippel Near-Field Scanner, reference point at tweeter, grille off, speaker broken in. THD at 86 and 96 dB @1 m and compression sweeps (referenced to 76 dB, tested at 86/96/102 dB) from Klippel. Impedance measured at 0.10 V RMS.

## Reliability notes
Single loaned sample (from a viewer). Graph-read values are approximate. Erin explicitly notes disagreement with positive reviews elsewhere.
