# Stereophile: Dynaudio Special Forty measurements (2018-08-30)

## Summary
John Atkinson's MLSSA quasi-anechoic measurements: sensitivity 84.4 dB, minimum impedance 4.9 Ω at 178 Hz, and a rear port tuned to about 51 Hz. He found a strong sidewall mode at 512 Hz and a port resonance spike near 700 Hz, linked to a midrange problem he heard. Dispersion is even up to 7 kHz, and the first-order crossover produces a vertical suckout above the tweeter axis.

## Method notes
DRA Labs MLSSA with DPA 4006 microphone for far-field (anechoic-style, 50"), Earthworks QTC-40 for nearfield and room response. Port-open measurement. Quasi-anechoic, so lower confidence below about 300 Hz (Tier B). No THD or compression measured.

## Reliability notes
Single sample supplied for review. JA ties the measured cabinet mode to a problem he heard in auditioning.
