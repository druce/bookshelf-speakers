# StereoNET: AE100 Mk II review

## Summary
Listening review (Applause Award category, 2022) priced at GBP 259. Paul Sechi found accurate tonality, fine staging and good timing, with bass not quite tight and some lower-midband thinness depending on placement.

## Key claims
- Accurate tonality, fine soundstage and image placement; "propulsive", engaging sound with good timing.
- Treble surprisingly well controlled, not shouty; some lower-mid warmth missing on stands in open room.
- Placement-sensitive: near-wall/credenza placement added bark and bottom-end reinforcement; best on stands with 25 cm to rear wall.
- Recommends at least 40 W per channel for best results; verdict positive.
- Physical notes: 15 mm HDF cabinet, rear slot port, crossover 2.9 kHz; he measured the woofer cone at 105 mm (roll-surround inside); maker claims 6 Ω, 87 dB.

## Method notes
Listening only, Primare 100 W integrated, stands 625 mm.

## Reliability notes
Single reviewer; publication date not captured. UK-oriented review.
