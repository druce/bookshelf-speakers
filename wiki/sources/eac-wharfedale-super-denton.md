# EAC: Wharfedale Super Denton review (2024-04-28)

## Summary
Klippel NFS measurement of a viewer-loaned pair. Erin reports a preference score of 5.9 and finds the speaker good but not perfectly neutral: mild dips 200-500 Hz and near 3 kHz, a pronounced resonance near 660 Hz, decent but not deep bass, low harmonic distortion at 96 dB but higher multitone midrange distortion and compression at 102 dB. No raw Klippel export was available in the repo, so values below are graph-read or as stated by the reviewer.

## Method notes
Klippel NFS, reference at the tweeter, grille on (grille-off spin also provided). Asymmetric mirror-image layout; sample measured had tweeter left, midrange right. Break-in done. Listening (Tier D): compared with KEF Blade 2 Meta and Morel Avyra 633; heard slightly reduced mid/upper mids; prefers the Linton but likes this one; bass decent but kick drums less pronounced.

## Reliability notes
