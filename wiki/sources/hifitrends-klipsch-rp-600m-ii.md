# HiFi Trends: RP-600M II review (2022-07-08)

## Summary
Review sample supplied by Klipsch. Overall 8.7/10 (build 9.0, features 9.0, sound 8.0). Finds improved imaging, detail and bass control over the original but a brighter top end that needs careful electronics matching.

## Key claims
- Cash (Tier D): lively, engaging, authoritative; focused centre image and large soundstage; preferred speakers pointed straight ahead.
- Better resolution, bass punch and control than the original 600M.
- A bit bright at times on a solid-state amp; a 9 W tube amp calmed it.
- Build feels more solid than the original; cork pads grip well.

## Method notes
Listening with Audiolab Omnia and Cayin MT-12N tube amp, 24-inch stands about 3 ft from walls.

## Reliability notes
Manufacturer-supplied sample, affiliate links on page. No measurements.
