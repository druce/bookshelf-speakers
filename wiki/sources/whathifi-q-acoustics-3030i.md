# What Hi-Fi?: Q Acoustics 3030i

## Summary
Five-star review (sound, compatibility and build each 5). An update note says the 3030i has been replaced by the 3000c series and the older model remains available.

## Key claims (attributed)
- Describes a smooth, cohesive, slightly understated balance, with well-integrated, weighty bass for the size.
- Says it handles high volumes more comfortably than expected for the price.
- Placement unfussy: best about 40 cm from the wall, tolerant of closer; port is rear-firing.
- Rivals Dali Oberon 1 and B&W 607 judged more vivid and exciting.
- Cons: tough competition.

## Method notes
Listening only. Lists spec 46 Hz-30 kHz (claimed), 6 Ω, 88 dB.

## Reliability notes
Date of the supersession note is not given.
