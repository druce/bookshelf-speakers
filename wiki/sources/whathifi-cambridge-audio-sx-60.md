# What Hi-Fi?: Cambridge Audio SX-60

## Summary
Launch-era review of the first-generation SX-60. Verdict: good, upbeat speakers, "more functional than special". Star rating not captured in the saved text (a search summary indicated 4 stars; unverified).

## Key claims (attributed, paraphrased)
- Confident, energetic, bouncy sound; well balanced.
- Top end prominent but not too bright; midrange direct.
- Bass rich but tight and agile; needs ~30 cm behind the speaker or bass becomes overpowering.
- Compared against Wharfedale Diamond 121, which sounded clearer with more dynamic reach.
- Cons: could do with more refinement.

## Method notes
Subjective, in-house test rooms; 2013 review of the original model, which may predate the current "V2" label.

## Reliability notes
Date is old relative to current production; dimensions match current spec.
