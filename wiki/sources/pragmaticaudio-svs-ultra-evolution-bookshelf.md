# Pragmatic Audio: SVS Ultra Evolution Bookshelf (2026-04-13)

## Summary
Six-week review in three rooms. Praises bass extension, claimed to reach toward 30 Hz in room, and the extension and smoothness of the treble.

## Key claims
- Reviewer says bass is in a different tier from the Polk ES20 and Fosi SP601, and fuller than a KEF LS50 Meta without a sub; the KEF images more precisely.
- Reports in-room bass extension toward 30 Hz (in-room, includes room gain; not anechoic).
- Room correction was beneficial because room modes become audible.
- Price $599 each / $1,199 per pair.

## Reliability notes
Unit supplied by SVS and its distributor; in-room claims; smaller outlet.
