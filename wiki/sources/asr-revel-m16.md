# ASR: Revel M16 review (2020-03-05)

## Summary
Klippel NFS measurement of a purchased M16 pair. Amir finds near-flat listening-window response apart from a bass hump near 100 Hz and treble roll-off above ~10 kHz, very smooth directivity horizontally, and rising distortion below the 2.1 kHz crossover. He awarded his top recommendation based on measurements plus listening (Tier D), while disclosing a Harman relationship.

## Method notes
Klippel Near-field Scanner, tweeter axis, grille off, 1000+ points. Reviewer notes the HF roll-off differs from Harman's own published measurement. Max SPL not measured; Amir says output "was not there" subjectively and woofer could be made to distort when pushed.

## Reliability notes
Single sample purchased by the reviewer's company. Disclosed conflicts of interest with Harman. Impedance values are graph-read only.
