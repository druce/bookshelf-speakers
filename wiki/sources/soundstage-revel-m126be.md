# SoundStage! Hi-Fi: Revel PerformaBe M126Be (2019-08-01)

## Summary
Beaudette calls the M126Be the finest speaker he has heard in his room and a benchmark. Review carries a Reviewers' Choice badge. NRC measurements accompany it ([[soundstage-nrc-revel-m126be]]).

## Key claims (attributed)
- Even tonal balance, exceptional clarity and resolution, precise imaging with a wide, deep stage.
- Midrange purity; cymbals and treble lively but not distracting.
- Bass clean, kicks well, but not as deep as bigger speakers; needs larger speakers or a subwoofer for drum-impact weight (spec -6 dB at 54 Hz).
- Dynamics as good as any speaker of similar size he had heard; never approached distortion.
- Preferred it to Revel Performa3 F206 floorstanders for imaging, though the F206 gave more weight and venue space.
- Minor nits: visible driver screws, gloss black shows dust; sensitivity "a touch low".

## Method notes
Listened on 24" stands, Bryston B135 SST2 integrated, mostly CD/file sources; Revel F206 comparison.

## Reliability notes
Enthusiastic single-reviewer opinion; Tier D, attributed only.
