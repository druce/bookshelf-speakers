# The Absolute Sound: Wilson TuneTot (2018-09-12)

## Summary
Favourable listening report without a numeric rating. Bass is articulate with the port plugged but limited in weight and extension; the speaker is a demanding load.

## Key claims
- Plugging the rear port made bass significantly more articulate (two rooms).
- Bass and drums had satisfying impact but not floorstander weight or extension.
- ISOBase platform improved imaging, detail and bass definition.
- Not an easy load; needs amplifiers with robust power supplies.
- Launch price $9,800 pair ($10,500 upgrade colours).

## Method notes
Listening with Angstrom Stella, Bel Canto evo 6, Pass Aleph 0s, Parasound A23, Devialet Expert 130 and Peachtree Nova 150.

## Reliability notes
Capture is model-extracted. No measurements.
