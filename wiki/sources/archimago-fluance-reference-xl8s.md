# Archimago: Fluance Reference XL8S (2020-11-29)

## Summary
Hobbyist "qSpin" measurement (gated 1 m plus summed nearfield) of a purchased walnut XL8SW pair. Listening window is not flat, with bumps near 90 Hz, 1.2 kHz and 10 kHz and roughness between 1.5 and 8 kHz. Impedance is an easy load apart from a 3.5 Ω EPDR dip near 150 Hz. Distortion is low at 85 dB but rises at 90 dB.

## Method notes
qSpin: gated 1 m response blended with summed nearfield incl. ports; amplifier Emotiva XPA-1L. Horizontal stay within ±30°; sit at or slightly below tweeter axis (0 to -25° vertical). Crossover 3.1 kHz coincides with a vertical-polar dip near 0°. No numeric LW deviation, f3 or beamwidth published; none computed.

## Reliability notes
Single hobbyist rig, single pair (serial 2005023xx). Tier B; low-frequency data merges nearfield measurements. Values read from text and graphs.
