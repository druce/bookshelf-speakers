# SoundStage! Access: Paradigm Monitor SE Atom (2018-10-15)

## Summary
Subjective review at the $298/pair launch price; Reviewers' Choice badge, no numeric score. Capture was model-extracted, not verbatim.

## Key claims
- Hans Wetzel found tonal balance natural and well balanced, with nothing accentuated; slightly shouty at the top end at extreme volume.
- Bass depth described as remarkable for the price, reaching nearly as low as a KEF LS50 but less controlled.
- Treble clear and airy; mids detailed with no boxiness.
- Wide sweet spot and a convincing soundstage; dynamics good with limits at very high volume.
- Judged it better than the Aperion Intimus 5T and heard no concessions to price.

## Method notes
Listening with Hegel H360 integrated; no measurements.

## Reliability notes
Reviewed against ASR's measured brightness; the two disagree on treble (ASR: forward highs). Single reviewer; pricing partner gear far above the speaker.
