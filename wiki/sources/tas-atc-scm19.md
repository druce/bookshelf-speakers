# TAS: ATC SCM19 (Neil Gader, 2014-08-12)

## Summary
Gader, a long-time ATC owner, gives a "highest recommendation".

## Key claims
- Neutral across the midband, presence and treble with a comforting warmth in lower mids/upper bass.
- Bass reaches the upper-40 Hz range with tight, tuneful sealed-box character; no true bottom octave.
- Strong dynamics and low-level resolution; wide, even dispersion so no tight sweet spot.
- At 85 dB sensitivity it needs power and won't be at its best at low volumes.
- New in-house tweeter and crossover tweaks said to remove earlier nasality.

## Reliability notes
Subjective Tier D; reviewer is a declared ATC fan; reviewed an earlier (2014) production run, possibly before the v2 designation.
