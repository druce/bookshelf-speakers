# Audioholics: Revel Concerta2 M16 review (2016-07-11)

## Summary
Review with outdoor gated measurements (Tier B for the numbers, Tier D for the listening). Larson praised soundstage/imaging and balanced sound, found response "quite linear for the price", and rated performance and value 4.5/5 stars.

## Key claims
- Imaging/soundstage: "superlative"; instrument placement and timbre convincing (Tier D).
- Bass: limited without subwoofer, especially for electronic music; composure at high levels, congested only at extreme volume.
- Off-axis response follows on-axis; uniform to ~50° horizontally; vertical lobing dip just under 2.5 kHz at 20-50° judged benign.
- Recommended at $900/pair MSRP as good value.

## Extracted values
| Field | Value | Note |
|---|---|---|
| sensitivity | 85.5 dB (2.83 V/1 m) | measured; maker claims 86 dB |
| impedance | low valley centred near 150 Hz, no minimum value stated | text only; reviewer judged 6 Ω rating hard to dispute |
| on-axis | "impressively flat", ~100 Hz bump, ripples above 6 kHz suspected rig artifacts | no tolerance numbers given |
| pricing | MSRP $900/pair (2016) | historical |

## Method notes
Measured outdoors at ~9 ft height, gated at 14 ms; reviewer states accuracy is lost below ~70 Hz and resolution below 140 Hz, so no f3 is taken from it. Listening in 24x13 ft room with Pioneer SC-05 receiver, no sub.

## Reliability notes
Quasi-anechoic, single sample; numbers given are limited to graphs and text. Review is ~10 years old (Concerta2 era; same model as current).
