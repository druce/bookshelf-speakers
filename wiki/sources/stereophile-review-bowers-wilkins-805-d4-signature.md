# Stereophile: 805 D4 Signature review

## Summary
John Atkinson finds an uncoloured midrange with piano and voice and no objectionable brightness despite the raised treble, calls it highly recommended for small-to-medium rooms, and admits it loses the lowest frequencies and ultimate loudness. Page 2 of the raw capture is saved as `raw/reviews-critic/stereophile-review-bowers-wilkins-805-d4-signature-p2.md`.

## Key claims
- Piano and vocals reproduced with no discernible coloration; stable, accurate imaging.
- Top octave has more "air" than strictly accurate versus his KEF LS50 and GoldenEar references, but clean; not bothered by brightness.
- Low frequencies lightweight in his room; deepest synth bass only hinted at; midbass weight sufficient.
- Sound hardened at higher volume with the NAD M10 integrated (tied to the current-hungry load).
- Verdict: highly recommended for small-to-medium rooms; "steps out of the way of the music" (paraphrased).
- Price at review: $13,500 per pair (standard 805 D4 $9,500).

## Method notes
Listening on Celestion stands with Parasound JCA100 Tribute monoblocks and NAD M10; toe-in so axes cross a foot in front of the listener.
