# SoundStage!/NRC: Revel PerformaBe M126Be measurements (2019-08-01)

## Summary
Anechoic-chamber measurements at Canada's National Research Council accompanying the SoundStage! review. Sensitivity 85.25 dB; impedance bottoms near 4.5 Ω at about 200 Hz with peaks near 25 Ω around 95 Hz; THD+N at 90 dB is highest around 100 Hz.

## Extracted values
| Field | Value | Note |
|---|---|---|
| sensitivity_db_2v83 | 85.25 dB | stated, averaged 300 Hz-3 kHz on listening window, 2.83 V/1 m |
| min_impedance_ohm | about 4.5 Ω @ about 200 Hz | graph-read (approx.) |
| impedance peaks | about 19 Ω near 38 Hz, 25 Ω near 95 Hz, 21 Ω near 2 kHz | graph-read (approx.) |
| thd+n @ 90 dB (2 m) | about 4% near 100 Hz (62 dB vs 90 dB), about 1.8% spike near 330 Hz, falling below 0.3% above 500 Hz | graph-read (approx.); THD+N, 50 Hz-10 kHz; level referenced at 2 m with 9.32 V input |
| frequency response | on-axis, 15°/30°/45°/60°/75° and 5-point listening window plotted | graphs only; no deviation value extracted |

## Method notes
Mic 7" above the tweeter dome (manufacturer-specified), grille off, measured at 2 m and plotted at 1 m. Listening window is a 5-point average (on-axis, ±15° horizontal and vertical), not the CTA-2034 nine-point window. Sample is the one used in the SoundStage! review.

## Reliability notes
Anechoic, but no full spinorama. Graph-read numbers are approximate. THD+N includes noise, so is not directly comparable with Erin's THD.
