# SoundStage!: Focal Sopra No1 (Diego Estan, 2020-03-15)

## Summary
Estan made the No1 his new reference minimonitor, praising midrange, imaging and treble, with bass output the only reservation at the $9,990/pair price. Anechoic NRC measurements are on [soundstage-nrc-focal-sopra-n1](soundstage-nrc-focal-sopra-n1.md).

## Key claims (attributed, paraphrased)
- Midrange described as liquid-smooth, forward and lively, "the calling card".
- Imaging and transparency "beyond reproach"; very wide, deep soundstage; speakers disappear.
- Beryllium treble called the finest he had heard in his room, extended and airy; versus Revel PerformaBe M126Be it had roughly 1 dB more output at 3-10 kHz and occasionally over-emphasised sibilants at high volume.
- Bass taut and fast with good dynamics but not the last word in output or extension; in-room -3 dB at 33 Hz re 1 kHz (his own mic measurement, in-room) versus 29 Hz for Q Acoustics Concept 300.
- Listened at 20 degrees toe-in, 9 ft triangle, 16 in from front wall, no subwoofer.
- Specs quoted: 45 Hz-40 kHz ±3 dB, 89 dB, 8 Ω nominal; 16.5x11x15.5 in, 42 lb each; five-year warranty. Stands $1,980/pair.

## Method notes
Reviewer's room treated; amplification McIntosh MC302 (8 Ω taps). Pre-production variation not discussed.

## Reliability notes
Subjective, single reviewer, single sample pair (supplied in a custom Chameleon finish).
