# Audioholics: NHT Super Stereo system (2014-05-07)

## Summary
Short preview-style review of the SuperZero 2.1 paired with the Super 8 subwoofer ($600 system). It is a system impression, not an independent bench test; the only measurements shown are NHT-supplied.

## Key claims
- Munz called the sound "phenomenal": highs natural and airy, no flinching at extreme volume, fairly linear with little coloration.
- Positioned as a small-room 2.1 system; not for reference levels in large spaces.
- Maker figures quoted: 8 ohm nominal, 4.3 ohm min, 87 dB at 2.83 V, 85 Hz-20 kHz +/-3 dB (Tier C, not Audioholics measurements).

## Reliability notes
Article states it is a preview; the FR/impedance curve is courtesy of NHT. Listening was with the subwoofer.
