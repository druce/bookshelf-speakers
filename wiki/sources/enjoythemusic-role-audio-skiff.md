# Enjoy the Music: Role Audio Skiff (2006-05)

## Summary
Stone found the Skiff linear, imaging-oriented and ideal for desktop near-field listening, but needing a subwoofer. Old review (price then $199 each). Capture is WebFetch-extracted, not verbatim.

## Key claims (attributed)
- Called its frequency response "remarkably linear"; drier balance than the Aperion 422 with less lower-mid and upper-bass energy.
- Needs a subwoofer for satisfying bass.
- Speakers "virtually disappeared"; near-ideal close-field monitor.
- No noticeable compression but needs a strong amplifier for loud levels and self-limits at peaks.
- Possibly an ideal desktop speaker for those who want speakers heard, not seen.

## Method notes
Listening only, with a 10" subwoofer.

## Reliability notes
2006 vintage; contradicts ASR's measured resonances, which suggests either unit variation or listening versus measurement difference.
