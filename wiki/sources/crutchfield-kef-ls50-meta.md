# Crutchfield customer reviews — KEF LS50 Meta

## Summary
143 Crutchfield reviews: 130 five-star, 6 four-star, 5 three-star, 2 one-star. Imaging and clarity dominate the praise; bass depth (15 advise a sub), loudness limits and amplifier demands (7) are the recurring reservations. Two product defects were reported (2 of 143). Counts are hand-coded from the clipped page; "keyword-coded" tallies are approximate.
