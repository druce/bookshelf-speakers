# SoundStage! Hi-Fi: Paradigm Persona B (Roger Kanno, 2017-12-01)

## Summary
Reviewers' Choice rave. Kanno found it neutral, very detailed and effortless, with bass that is fast and controlled for its size. Review price was $7,000/pair.

## Key claims
- Bass: exceptionally good for a bookshelf and fast and well defined, though the lowest octave is absent.
- Mids/highs: among the smoothest and clearest he had heard; no etched or harsh quality.
- Imaging: very precise and stable; large soundstage.
- Dynamics: can play very loud with low distortion.
- Amplification: sound improved with Anthem M1 monoblocks over an NAD M27 (170 W), but it does not require a huge amp.
- Verdict: "ridiculously good"; he doubted much better could be had without spending far more.

## Method notes
Room: large; Anthem Statement D2 processor/DACs, Anthem M1 and NAD M27 amps; several months of listening.

## Reliability notes
Single reviewer, subjective (Tier D). Note the contrast with the measured upper-treble lift and presence dip in [hifinews-paradigm-persona-b](hifinews-paradigm-persona-b.md) and [soundstage-nrc-paradigm-persona-b](soundstage-nrc-paradigm-persona-b.md); forum posts (not ingested) repeat claims of brightness.
