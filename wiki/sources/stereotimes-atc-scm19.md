# Stereo Times: ATC SCM 19 (Allen Edelstein)

## Summary
Long design-focused review; price then $4,299/pair; undated page, probably the original generation.

## Key claims
- Solid "A" for a small speaker; reviewer would buy it; only wish is more bass.
- Sealed, maximally-flat alignment; honest -6 dB at 55 Hz spec but bass sounds fuller than that suggests.
- Rated as dynamically linear and "alive", coped with very loud piano peaks without compression he could hear.
- Tonality varies with the recording rather than carrying a house voice; best with a strong solid-state amp near the 300 W end.

## Reliability notes
Tier D; no measurements; date unknown.
