# EAC: Dynaudio Emit 20 review (2023-11-12)

## Summary
Klippel NFS measurement of a manufacturer-loaned pair (maker did not see data before release). Erin finds robust bass to about 40 Hz (F3/F10 54/33 Hz), low distortion down to about 40 Hz, wide consistent radiation, but a woofer resonance near 700 Hz that muffles the midrange, a peak near 4 kHz and heavy output above 10 kHz. He prefers them turned 10-15° off-axis. The page body is dated 2023-11-12; dataset metadata lists 2023-11-22.

## Method notes
Klippel Near-Field Scanner, reference at tweeter, grille off, broken in. Raw woofer impedance outside the box shows the same resonance; Erin's CLD on basket spokes did not fix it. Impedance at 0.10 V RMS.

## Reliability notes
Single loaned sample. Computed sensitivity (83.2 dB) is 2.8 dB under the maker's 86 dB. THD and compression from images.
