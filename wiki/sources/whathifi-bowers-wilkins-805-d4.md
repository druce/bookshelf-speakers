# What Hi-Fi?: 805 D4

## Summary
What Hi-Fi? describes a beautifully built, sonically accomplished standmount with class-leading clarity and imaging, a deserving shortlist entry rather than a runaway winner. It did not capture a star rating in the saved text.

## Key claims
- Imaging expansive and layered; clarity and resolution called class-leading; agile, articulate and analytical yet involving.
- Tonal balance even; bass decent for the size but rivals (KEF Reference 1, ProAc K1, Fyne F1-8) dig deeper.
- Cons: they come alive only when pushed and sound a touch lifeless at low volume; tough competition.
- Responds well to a powerful amplifier; quoted minimum impedance 4.6 Ω.

## Method notes
Listening with Naim, Burmester and Rotel Michi electronics. Specs quoted from B&W.

## Reliability notes
Single sample; review follows 2022 launch; no measurements.
