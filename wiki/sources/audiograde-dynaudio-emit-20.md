# Audiograde: Dynaudio Emit 20 (2022-06-17)

## Summary
Calls the Emit 20 arguably the sweet spot of the Emit range at 825 GBP: balanced scale, dynamics, pace and seamless driver integration; Best Value award.

## Key claims (attributed)
- Energetic and authoritative pace on rhythmic tracks; sibilance minimal, smooth rather than bright.
- Soundstage wider and noticeably deeper than the Emit 10.
- Rolls off short of the Emit 30 in ultimate scale, but suits mid-sized rooms.
- Bass-port foam bung provided for tuning near walls.

## Reliability notes
UK outlet; reviewer in 7x4.5 m room; no measurements.
