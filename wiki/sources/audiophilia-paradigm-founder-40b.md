# Audiophilia: Paradigm Founder 40B (2023)

## Summary
Subjective review, loaner pair, medium treated room, ~$17K system; recommended.

## Key claims
- Anthony Kershaw called the 40B an imaging and soundstage standout with wide dispersion, easy to drive.
- Described the voicing as middle-of-the-road and suited to many genres; bass respectable but limited on heavy electronic tracks.
- Cited price $2,240-2,400 per pair; recommended for sub-$3,000 shopping.

## Method notes
Marantz 40n integrated, Thorens TD 1600; compared against Harbeth P3ESR XD, Acelec Model 1, Xavian Perla.

## Reliability notes
Spec table quotes "8 ohms" and 92 dB; he did not measure. Loaner from Atlas Audio Video (a dealer).
