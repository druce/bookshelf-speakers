# Sound On Sound: Avantone CLA-10 (Dec 2018)

## Summary
Reviewer finds it "an NS-10 in all but name" with matching bass but too bright. Capture is model-extracted, not verbatim.

## Key claims
- Treble said to carry about 3-4 dB more energy than an NS-10M Studio in 2.5-7 kHz; hissy and tiring over long sessions.
- Bass fast and accurate with very low group delay; imaging well focused with forward mids.
- Warns against using it as a mix reference unless the balance is accepted or corrected with EQ; push-on terminals and wood screws seen as less robust than the original.

## Reliability notes
Studio-monitor context; the 3-4 dB claim appears to be from the reviewer's measurements but could not be verified verbatim. Consistent with ASR's finding of a strong upper-mid/treble boost.
