# napilopez: Amphion Argon1 quasi-anechoic spinorama (2020-03-03)

## Summary
Hobbyist quasi-anechoic spinorama and directivity measurements of a purchased pair. He reports rising on-axis treble that falls off quickly off-axis, flattest response 15-30 degrees off-axis, nice horizontal directivity, messiness at 400-900 Hz, and favours minimal toe-in. Listening notes: neutral timbre, clear vocals, surprising bass for 5" woofers.

## Method notes
Quasi-anechoic (gated) measurements, horizontal 0-90 degrees, vertical 0/5/10/15 degrees plus ceiling/floor reflection estimates. Images are in the thread; none were saved as data.

## Reliability notes
Hobbyist rig, stated method, Tier B. Low-frequency data less reliable below roughly 300 Hz. The reviewer first posted partial data then completed it. Preference score depends on digitisation.
