# Audioholics: Focal On Wall 302 review (2025-04-23)

## Summary
Home-theater-focused review of the 302 as an LCR/surround on-wall speaker, with listening in an Atmos system plus sensitivity, in-room response and impedance/phase measurements by Gene DellaSala. Conclusion: recommended; 4 stars each for performance and value (page rating stars); needs a subwoofer below about 50 Hz. Review date from the page body (the raw header says unknown).

## Method notes
In-room measurement at 1 m (room influence included; the page does not describe the placement used). It is neither a spinorama nor a gated anechoic measurement, so it is treated as Tier B for electrical (impedance/phase) and sensitivity values only, and as indicative only for frequency response. Per the on-wall addendum, bass in a wall-mounted measurement includes boundary gain.

## Key claims (Tier D, attributed)
- Reviewer described the 302 as capable of rivalling and sometimes outperforming bookshelf designs, played loud without strain, and found wall mounting enhances bass.
- Listed cons: needs a subwoofer, large size, below 50 Hz bass lacking, expensive (US$1,749 each per the page).
- Used with dual SVS SB-3000 subwoofers in the listening tests.

## Reliability notes
Single sample, reviewer-run measurement chain not documented in detail. The 88 dB figure cannot be compared directly with anechoic sensitivities.
