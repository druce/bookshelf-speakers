# What Hi-Fi?: Dynaudio Emit 20 (2021-09-22)

## Summary
Scores listed: Sound 4, Compatibility 5, Build 5. Praised for scale and a refined, sweet treble and mid; dynamics and cohesion trail the KEF LS50 Meta.

## Key claims (attributed)
- Big sound with impressive scale and a wide-open soundfield for a standmount.
- Good weight to low frequencies; highs and strings sweet and refined; detailed without being fatiguing.
- Longer listening revealed a lack of cohesion and dynamic nuance versus the LS50 Meta.
- Advises keeping the rear port away from walls; a little toe-in improved image focus.

## Method notes
Reviewed on Burmester and Naim electronics. Tested in the UK; no measurements.
