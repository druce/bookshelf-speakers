# Stereophile review: MoFi SourcePoint 8 (Kalman Rubinson, 2023-08-17)

Raw pages: page 1 above, page 2 at raw/reviews-critic/stereophile-review-mofi-sourcepoint-8-p2.md.

## Summary
Rubinson used the speakers with Benchmark AHB2 amplifiers on Solidsteel stands and found them balanced, wide-range and non-fatiguing. No numeric rating.

## Key claims (attributed)
- Presentation relatively forward and engaging, sharp detail, no brightness and no artificial "air" (Rubinson).
- Bass tight and powerful above 50 Hz but little output below 50 Hz; did not bottom out at high levels short of neighbour-annoying volume; not equal to far larger speakers or a subwoofer.
- Best imaging with toe-in about halfway between straight ahead and aimed at the listener; wide sweet spot; judge at ear level.
- Compared with the B&W 705 S3: B&W more open with wider, taller stage but elevated treble; MoFi more tonally self-effacing.
- Compared with Revel Performa3 F206: differences elusive; Revel had more extended low bass, MoFi seemed fuller on most material.
- Works as a competent center channel in either orientation.
- Caveats: no deep bass, can't match some larger speakers at high levels, evaluate at ear level.

## Reliability notes
Single reviewer, large room (about 11 ft listening distance). Measurements are in [[stereophile-mofi-sourcepoint-8]].
