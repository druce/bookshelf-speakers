# What Hi-Fi?: Elac Debut B5.2 (5 stars)

## Summary
Scored 5/5 for sound, compatibility and build; repeat What Hi-Fi? Award winner in the budget class.

## Key claims
- Called "arguably the most capable budget standmounters" the outlet has heard (verdict line).
- Tonally even-handed and balanced with poor recordings; less sweet than Q Acoustics and less warm than Dali rivals, but ahead on insight and composure.
- Presentation leans toward analysis over smoothness or warmth; less forgiving of partnering electronics.
- Precise, cohesive timing; wide soundstage with little toe-in fuss; works best about 30 cm from the wall on rigid stands.
- Notes (in later deal coverage seen in search) that the DB53 succeeds it.

## Method notes
Subjective listening in What Hi-Fi? test rooms; review tested at UK/US budget price.

## Reliability notes
Tier D; no measurements. Review date 2023-10-16 appears to be a refresh of the 2019 original.
