# Erin's Audio Corner: Aperion Verus V8B review (2023-06-19)

## Summary
Klippel NFS measurement of a manufacturer-loaned pair (not seen by Aperion before publication). Erin found a significant 100-500 Hz mid-range scoop and resonant peaks near 4 kHz and 10 kHz, low bass distortion, a reasonably wide radiation pattern and good build, and concluded buyers should explore other options at the price.

## Method notes
Klippel NFS, grille off. Reviewer notes THD at 86/96 dB @1 m and compression sweeps at 86/96/102 dB. Impedance at 0.10 V RMS. Erin says compression issues are noticeable above 1 kHz (graph shows only about 1 dB).

## Reliability notes
Single manufacturer-loaned sample. Graph-read values are approximate. The page title says "Verus V8B", matching the Verus III Concert V8B (same drivers and specs).
