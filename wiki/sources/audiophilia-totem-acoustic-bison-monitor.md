# Audiophilia: Bison Monitor (2023)

## Summary
Subjective review at US $2,250/pair with Naim electronics; recommended under $3,000.

## Key claims
- Kershaw found bass fairly deep for the size, reaching about 40 Hz by claim, but not like full-range speakers.
- Soundstage wide and deep in a medium room; imaging precise.
- Handled demanding orchestral material without strain; punches above its size.
- Placement easy; works straight-firing, toed-in or nearfield.

## Method notes
Naim NSC 222 / NAP 250; brief trial with a Marantz 40n.

## Reliability notes
Single reviewer, no measurements; price then $2,250 versus $2,300 now.
