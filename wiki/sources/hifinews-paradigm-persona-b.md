# Hi-Fi News lab report: Paradigm Persona B

## Summary
Hi-Fi News measured a lively treble-axis response with a presence-band suckout and about +/-4.6 to 4.8 dB response errors. Sensitivity is above Paradigm's anechoic spec; bass reaches 42 Hz (-6 dB re 200 Hz); impedance falls to 3.8 Ω, which the lab says suggests a 4 Ω nominal rating despite Paradigm's "8 ohm compatible".

## Method notes
Quasi-anechoic gated response with nearfield bass summation, treble-axis; grille effects shown. Publication date not on the captured page.

## Reliability notes
Tier B; low-frequency data from nearfield summing, lower confidence than a full anechoic. Two samples (pair matching reported).
