# StereoNET: Wharfedale Super Denton (2024-11-08)

## Summary
8.5/10 with an Applause Award. Describes a mature, balanced, smooth speaker with more bass weight than expected and an immersive soundstage, but less definition and air than pricier standmounters.

## Key claims
- Midrange and treble smooth with no harshness; treble may seem subdued at first.
- Bass controlled, fast, no boom unless within ~20 cm of rear wall; less extension than the Linton.
- Dynamics relaxed with little compression at normal levels; not a party speaker.
- Imaging wide and three-dimensional.

## Method notes
Listening with Rega Aethos and Cyrus 40 amplification; Chord DAVE source.

## Reliability notes
Single reviewer; Capture was model-extracted.
