# Stereophile: F500SP review by Ken Micallef (2022-02-02)
Page 2 is saved at raw/reviews-critic/stereophile-review-fyne-audio-f500sp-page2.md. Measurements are in [stereophile-fyne-audio-f500sp](stereophile-fyne-audio-f500sp.md).

## Summary
Micallef found the F500SP precise but not analytical, with a wide, deep soundstage, good texture and taut bass, though lighter in weight than larger-woofer designs. He found it amplifier-sensitive and less forgiving of near-wall placement than Fyne's BassTrax claims suggest.

## Key claims (attributed)
- Bass: coherent, taut "actual notes" with no bloat, but less weight than larger speakers.
- Imaging and staging: wide, deep, immersive soundfield; images fleshy and chunky versus the GoldenEar BRX, which was more focused and resolving.
- Tone and treble: detailed, textured, low in apparent distortion; could turn forward or glary on some recordings and with the Parasound Hint 6.
- Amplifier matching: glare with Parasound Hint 6 Halo, warmer with Heed Elixir, better with Schiit Ragnarok 2, best with Ayre EX-8 2.0.
- Placement: best about 33" from front wall, 59" apart; near-wall placement less forgiving than expected.
- Compared with GoldenEar BRX ($1,599) and Polk Legend L100: wider, deeper stage but less focus than BRX; more weight than Polk.
- Verdict: recommended auditioning; no numeric rating.

## Method notes
Listened mostly to vinyl on 24" stands with four integrated amps. Price at review: $1,995 gloss, $2,295 walnut; FS6 stands $995.

## Reliability notes
Single reviewer, subjective; amp-dependence limits generalisation.
