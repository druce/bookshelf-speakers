# StereoNET: Paradigm Founder 40B (2023)

## Summary
Subjective review at £2,400; Applause Award category. No measurements.

## Key claims
- David Price described a punchy, lively sound nearer classic JBL/Klipsch than British monitors, yet close to neutral.
- Standout soundstaging, pin-sharp imaging, crisp midband and fast dynamics.
- Caveats: a subtle "cuppiness" in the midband, a slightly upholstered/warm bass in his room, tweeter less transparent than a NEAT Ministra.
- Best on 24-inch stands about 20 cm from the rear wall with slight toe-in; benefited from a robust solid-state amp.

## Reliability notes
Single reviewer; figures like 89 dB/1 W and 69 Hz are the maker's claims.
