# MusicTech: Avantone CLA-10 (2018-12-05)

## Summary
Studio-monitor review of the passive CLA-10 as an NS10 recreation; no score. Capture is model-extracted, not verbatim.

## Key claims
- Reviewer found it a fair recreation of the NS10's look and sound, but considerably brighter than his own NS10s with treble over-exposed.
- Bass clean and fast when audible, but little low bass; the speakers can seem to shout and mask the low end.
- Would not recommend as sole monitors; suggests auditioning first.

## Reliability notes
Subjective, studio-use frame (mix reference), not home hi-fi. Paraphrased from a model summary.
