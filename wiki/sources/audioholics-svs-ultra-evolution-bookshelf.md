# Audioholics: SVS Ultra Evolution Bookshelf (Jacob Green, 2026-09-09)

## Summary
Subjective review, 5/5 performance and 5/5 value. No lab measurements appear in the article, so it counts as Tier D here, not Tier A.

## Key claims
- Green found weightier, more substantial bass than any 6.5 in two-way he had heard near the price; thought they sounded more like a tower.
- Tonality balanced, on the warm side of neutral with non-fatiguing, smooth treble from the diamond-coated dome.
- Compared with the RBH Inspire 5: more body and power, less incisive; vs Fyne F500S: more forgiving and laid-back; vs Paradigm Premier 220B V2: warmer and fuller but less transparent.
- Easy to drive; a 25 W Rotel integrated was his favourite partner. Cons: glossy finish shows fingerprints.
