Status evidence is mixed: World Wide Stereo lists it in stock at $374 per pair (2026-10-11), but Audio Advisor marks it discontinued/no longer available, and Gear Patrol and What Hi-Fi? say it is being replaced by the Debut 3.0 DB63.
