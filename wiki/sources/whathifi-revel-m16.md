# What Hi-Fi?: Revel Concerta2 M16 review

## Summary
UK review calling the M16 one of the best speakers below £1000. Praise for dynamics, bass authority and driver integration; caution about system matching.

## Key claims
- Bass: deep, powerful, agile; likened to small floorstanders.
- Dynamics: large-scale crescendos without hardening or compression at high levels.
- Imaging: wide, precisely focused stage; little sensitivity to toe-in.
- Midrange: well integrated.
- Caveat: a "ruthless edge" in clarity that can be provoked by bright partnering kit; less sweet in the tweeter than an ATC SCM 11.
- Priced at £950 in the UK; verdict "bundle of fun" but not the most refined.

## Method notes
Subjective only; no measurements. Date shown is as scraped from page (2018-10-08), review itself likely older.
