# Phile-web: JBL Stage 250B review (2024-09-18)

## Summary
Japanese-language listening review reprinted from Audio Accessory vol. 194, marked as provided by Harman International (sponsored), using an Arcam A5 amp. Uniformly positive.

## Key claims
- Ohashi described the 250B as direct, image-focused and energetic, in what he called true JBL character.
- Sense of space and solidity do not match higher JBL series, but sound is free of added coloration.
- Acoustic bass level was even across pitches with good body.
- Wide HDI dispersion gives an enveloping spread; presented as a strong value for newcomers.
- Price quoted 55,000 yen per pair (Japan).
