# Erin's Audio Corner: Audio Craftsmen Victoria (2025)

## Summary
Klippel NFS review of a loaned pair. Measured sensitivity is about 84 dB (claimed 88). Bass is limited (f3 about 65 Hz), a 200-800 Hz midrange dip makes vocals recessed, horizontal dispersion is wide and vertical narrow. Distortion is low at moderate levels. Erin's listening notes are cool on the tonal balance.
