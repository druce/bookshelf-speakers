# Hi-Fi News: Spendor Classic 3/1 lab report (2021-09-06)

## Summary
Paul Miller's lab report measured sensitivity near 85 dB against an 88 dB claim, a benign impedance (min 5.7 ohm, behaves like a 6 ohm speaker), and a generally orderly, slightly downtilted response with two 2-3 dB dips. Bass extends to 45 Hz (-6 dB) helped by a port tuned to 34 Hz; distortion is low.

## Method notes
Hi-Fi News in-room/quasi-anechoic method: near-field summed driver/port for bass, free-field corrected to 1 m at 2.83 V above; on an axis midway between tweeter and mid/bass. No spinorama, so no directivity or listening-window data.

## Reliability notes
Single pair (left/right shown). Tier B per the evidence hierarchy: lower confidence below about 300 Hz. Lab text is the source; graphs not digitised.
