# SoundStage!/NRC: PSB Imagine B50 measurements (2024-04-01)

## Summary
Anechoic-chamber measurements at the National Research Council of Canada. Voltage sensitivity 83.8 dB, a minimum impedance near 4.1 Ω and THD+N near 2-2.5% at 90 dB SPL at 2 m. No full CTA-2034 spinorama is published, so directivity comes from off-axis curves only.

## Extracted values
| Field | Value | Note |
|---|---|---|
| sensitivity_db_2v83 | 83.8 dB | stated: LW average 300 Hz-3 kHz, 2.83 V/1 m |
| Listening window (5-point) | about 81-86 dB from 100 Hz to 20 kHz; falls steeply below about 80 Hz | graph-read (approx.); no deviation figure stated |
| LW -3 dB point | roughly 70-75 Hz | graph-read (approx.), coarse |
| min impedance | about 4.1 Ω at about 150-200 Hz | graph-read (approx.) |
| Impedance peaks | about 19 Ω near 60 Hz, about 26 Ω near 1.3 kHz | graph-read (approx.) |
| THD+N @ 90 dB SPL, 2 m (about 96 dB at 1 m) | about 1.8% near 100-200 Hz, up to about 2.5% near 2 kHz | graph-read (approx.); 50 Hz-10 kHz; not exactly the 96 dB/1 m convention |

## Method notes
Microphone position 12 in. up from cabinet bottom (manufacturer-specified), grille off, measured at 2 m and plotted at 1 m. Listening window is a 5-point average (on-axis, ±15° horizontal and vertical).

## Reliability notes
Independent lab, single sample. Low-frequency behaviour below ~100 Hz from a chamber of this type carries more uncertainty than the Klippel near-field result.
