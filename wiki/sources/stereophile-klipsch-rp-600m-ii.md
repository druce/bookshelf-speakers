# Stereophile: RP-600M II follow-up (Aug 2023, Vol. 46 No. 8)

## Summary
Short follow-up to the author's 2019 review of the original. He finds the II more refined, with even bass for a ported standmount, and drives it successfully with a 3.5 W tube amp and a $125 class-A amp. No lab measurements are in this piece.

## Key claims
- Reichert (Tier D): the larger horn and new woofer add weight, presence and low-signal delicacy; treble smoother with less crossover-region noise.
- Bass in the 50-500 Hz range sounded unusually even for a moderately priced ported standmount (in his room).
- Preferred the old model with grilles on and the new one with grilles off.
- Plays lively with low-power, high-output-impedance tube amps; the sound followed the amp's character.
- Calls it "already a classic". Price quoted $649/pair; 18 lb; 16 x 8 x 13 in.

## Method notes
Subjective listening in the reviewer's own system; no measurements.

## Reliability notes
Enthusiastic narrative piece; spec comparison to the original is useful. Not an independent measurement.
