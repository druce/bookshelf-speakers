# Stereophile: Q Acoustics Concept 300 (John Atkinson, 2020-01-03)

Other raw files: `raw/measurements/stereophile-specs-q-acoustics-concept-300.md` (specs sidebar), `raw/reviews-critic/stereophile-review-q-acoustics-concept-300.md` and `...-p2-...` (review text).

## Summary
MLSSA quasi-anechoic farfield plus nearfield measurements. Response is very flat to the mid-treble with a slight top-end rise, port tuned to 44 Hz, and the load is not demanding (min 4.9 Ω). Atkinson's listening verdict was an enthusiastic recommendation (Tier D, below).

## Method notes
DRA Labs MLSSA, DPA 4006 mic farfield at 50 in (30° horizontal window average on tweeter axis), Earthworks QTC-40 nearfield and in-room. Grilles off for review samples. Impedance via MLSSA, checked with DATS V2.

## Key claims (Tier D, John Atkinson)
- Transparent, neutral, with stable well-defined imaging; low frequencies more extended than expected for the size.
- Midbass could sound tubby with the Lamm amplifiers; foam port plugs helped, preference depended on music.
- More high-treble energy than his KEF LS50s even with tweeter trimmed 0.5 dB; can be unforgiving of bright electronics.
- Played louder in the bass than the LS50 before straining.
- Enthusiastic recommendation; stands and tripod looks will not suit all tastes.

## Reliability notes
Single pair, Stereophile quasi-anechoic (lower confidence below about 300 Hz, nearfield-spliced). Price then $4,499.99/pair with stands.
