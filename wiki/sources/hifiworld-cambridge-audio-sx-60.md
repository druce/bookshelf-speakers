# Hi-Fi World: Cambridge Audio SX-60 (2021-10-29)

## Summary
UK magazine lab review with a short measured-performance section (driver and port response, impedance, sensitivity) plus listening. Finds an accurate, smooth-tweeter speaker with restrained bass, forward upper mids and a little boxiness. Scored 8/10.

## Extracted values
| Field | Value | Note |
|---|---|---|
| sensitivity | 86.6 dB (text) / 87 dB (measured-performance section) | reference level not stated; assumed 2.83 V/1 m, could be 1 W (~+1.3 dB at 6 Ω) |
| impedance | "6 Ω" measured with pink noise; bass unit DCR 4 Ω | min impedance and frequency not stated; graph axes non-linear, not read |
| crossover | ~3 kHz | from impedance trace, per reviewer |

## Method notes
Hi-Fi World's lab method is not described in this article (axis, distance, gating unstated); treated as Tier B with low confidence below ~300 Hz and no off-axis data. Listening with Creek Voyage iA20 integrated amp. Graph images are in the raw page; only the combined FR/impedance image was saved.

## Reliability notes
Single sample, supplied by distributor. Republished by 7Review (affiliate site); original magazine page not checked. Internal inconsistency 86.6 vs 87 dB. Reviewer's "6 Ω" differs from the maker's 8 Ω.
