# Erin's Audio Corner: Klipsch RP-600M II review (2022-05-22)

## Summary
Klippel NFS measurement of a purchased sample (grille off primary, grille on also measured). Erin calls it one of the more neutral Klipsch speakers, with good horizontal dispersion, weaker vertical directivity and a treble boost from about 8 kHz. He recommends turning the speakers about 20 degrees outward or using the grille to tame the tilt. Measured sensitivity is far below Klipsch's 94.5 dB claim.

## Method notes
Klippel Near-Field Scanner; reference plane at the tweeter; grille off primary. Instantaneous compression test is a 2.7 s log sweep at 76/86/96/102 dB @ 1 m normalized to the 76 dB result. THD plots at 86 and 96 dB @ 1 m. Price in the review was about $769/pair in 2022.

## Reliability notes
Single purchased sample, no manufacturer involvement. Distortion and impedance values read from graphs. Review is deliberately brief; subjective notes are short.
