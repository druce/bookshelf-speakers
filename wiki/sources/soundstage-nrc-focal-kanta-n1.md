# NRC anechoic measurements: Focal Kanta N°1 (2019-06-01)

## Summary
Anechoic-chamber measurements published with [soundstage-focal-kanta-n1](soundstage-focal-kanta-n1.md). Sensitivity 86.5 dB, listening window within roughly ±2.3 dB with a presence/upper-mid trough, impedance minimum in the mid-4 Ω range, THD+N about 1.3% at moderate-high level.

## Extracted values
| Field | Value | Note |
|---|---|---|
| sensitivity_db_2v83 | 86.5 dB | stated, 300 Hz-3 kHz listening-window average |
| lw_deviation_db | about ±2.3 dB (83.9-88.5 dB, 300 Hz-10 kHz) | graph-read (approx.); dip near 2.5-3 kHz and notch near 5.5 kHz |
| LW low-frequency roll-off | about 3 dB down near 75 Hz re 87 dB midband | graph-read (approx.); not entered as f3 |
| min_impedance_ohm | about 4.5 Ω @ about 200 Hz | graph-read (approx.); peaks about 28 Ω at 35 Hz, 23.5 Ω at 85 Hz, 37.5 Ω at 5 kHz |
| thd_96db_pct | about 1.3% (100 Hz-600 Hz); falls to <0.5% above about 1 kHz | graph-read; THD+N at 90 dB measured at 2 m, about 96 dB at 1 m by distance scaling |
| Setup | microphone between tweeter and midrange-woofer; grille off | |

## Method notes
Same NRC protocol as other SoundStage! entries: 2 m measurement plotted at 1 m, off-axis curves to 75 degrees, five-point listening window.

## Reliability notes
Single sample; graph reads only; no spin-o-rama export.
