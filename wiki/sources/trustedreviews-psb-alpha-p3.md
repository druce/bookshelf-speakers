# Trusted Reviews: PSB Alpha P3 (2019-09-19)

## Summary
Verdict: poise and agility uncommon at the price; worthy of consideration if you need a genuinely compact bookshelf speaker. Main weakness is the low end.

## Key claims
- Scale: sounds larger than the cabinet suggests; tracks volume rises without fluster.
- Treble: refined and detailed, "bite and shine" well judged even at high volume.
- Tonal balance: on the cool side of neutral; smooth driver integration; composed off-axis.
- Bass: lacks extension and rigour; kick drum fuzzy; larger Q Acoustics 3010i/3020i more composed.
- Price at launch: GBP 199 / $219.

## Reliability notes
Subjective Tier D; no measurements; star rating not captured.
