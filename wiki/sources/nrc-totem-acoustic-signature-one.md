# NRC (SoundStage!): Totem Signature One measurements (2018-04-01)

## Summary
Anechoic-chamber measurements at the National Research Council of Canada accompanying the SoundStage! review. Sensitivity 83.0 dB, well below the 87-87.5 dB claim. No numeric f3, LW deviation or spinorama is published; values below come from the stated sensitivity and from reading the graphs.

## Extracted values
| Field | Value | Note |
|---|---|---|
| sensitivity_db_2v83 | 83.0 dB | stated; averaged 300 Hz-3 kHz on listening window, 2.83 V/1 m |
| min_impedance_ohm | about 7.7 Ω near 200 Hz | graph-read (approx.); second dip about 8.7 Ω near 45 Hz |
| impedance maximum | above 50 Ω near 85 Hz (off-scale); about 25 Ω near 1.3 kHz | graph-read |
| listening-window response | plateau about 83 dB 100-500 Hz; falls through 80 dB near 85 Hz; 700 Hz spike to about 89 dB; dip to about 80 dB near 4 kHz | graph-read; measured at 2 m, plotted at 1 m; low-frequency detail limited by chamber |
| THD+N at 90 dB (2 m) | about 2.5% near 100 Hz, about 2-3.5% 200-300 Hz, below about 1% above 500 Hz | graph-read, from curves about 55-58 dB under a 90 dB trace; not entered as thd_86/96 because level is at 2 m |

## Method notes
Microphone between tweeter and midrange, grille off; on-axis, 15/30/45/60/75 degree off-axis, listening window (5-point average), THD+N at 90 dB, deviation from linearity (90 vs 70 dB), impedance and phase.

## Reliability notes
Single sample supplied for review. Charts only; no tabulated numbers beyond sensitivity. The narrow response disturbance near 650-700 Hz matches the port-pipe and panel resonances Stereophile found ([stereophile-totem-acoustic-signature-one](stereophile-totem-acoustic-signature-one.md)).
