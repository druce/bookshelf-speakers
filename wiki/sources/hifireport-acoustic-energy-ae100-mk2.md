# HiFiReport: AE100 MK2 review

## Summary
Very positive Taiwan-market review (price about US$750 there). Praises expansive soundstage, energetic sound and value; judges it clearly better than the first-generation AE100.

## Key claims
- Wide, deep, tall soundstage; speakers "disappear"; precise but not sharp imaging.
- Slightly warm, bright but not harsh; midrange warm yet resolving; bass supple and elastic.
- Holds balance at higher volumes; bass extension and quantity better than Mk1.
- Notes larger cabinet and aluminium-alloy mid-bass driver as key upgrades (maker pages call the cone paper; unresolved).

## Method notes
Listening only; amplifier details not captured.

## Reliability notes
Date not captured; market price not US. Strongly positive and at odds with the Klippel-based review.
