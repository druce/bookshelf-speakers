# TONE Audio: Focal Kanta no.1

## Summary
A system-style review (Naim Uniti Star bundle) calling the Kanta no.1 "absolutely lovely" at $6,590/pair without stands; praises dynamics and dispersion, easy amplifier matching.

## Key claims (attributed, paraphrased)
- Compared with Sopra and Utopia, slightly warmer and less delineated, but with high dynamic range, tonal neutrality and low distortion.
- Great horizontal and vertical dispersion, so placement is not critical to reach about 90% of performance; rake angle adjustment refines soundstage.
- Bass sufficient in small rooms unless the music has subterranean lines.
- Nominal impedance "close to 4 ohms"; suggests starting with the 4 Ω amp tap.
- Worked well with about ten amplifiers including tube amps.

## Method notes
Listening on 24 in sand-filled stands; no measurements.
