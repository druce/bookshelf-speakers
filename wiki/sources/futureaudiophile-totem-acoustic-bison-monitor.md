# FutureAudiophile: Bison Monitor (2025)

## Summary
Dewhirst found the speaker neutral and uncoloured, with unremarkable bass.

## Key claims
- Called the sound very neutral; no colouration detected versus other speakers at hand.
- Bass detailed but not deep; a subwoofer would likely be wanted.
- Pipe organ and piano sounded natural; saxophone highs without harshness.
- Industrial design plain.

## Method notes
Anthem MCA 225 Gen 2 amp, SPL Elector preamp, Benchmark DAC3 B. Publication date not shown (images dated 2025/09).

## Reliability notes
Affiliate-supported site; single reviewer; no measurements.
