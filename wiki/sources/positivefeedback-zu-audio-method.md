# Positive Feedback: Zu Audio Method (Dean Seislove, 2025-08-12, Issue 140)

## Summary
Favourable listening review in a small room on 23-inch stands and in a larger main room. Liked immediacy and imaging; says it lacks molar-rattling bass and recommends subs for more.

## Key claims
- Reviewer described the sound as lively with strong immediacy, even on processed recordings.
- Soundstage extended wide past the speakers; horn accents bright and snappy but not harsh.
- No deep or heavy bass; subwoofer(s) advised for scale.
- Plays loud enough to fill a largish room, though limits show there.
- Works lying flat or upright; wide off-axis listening; usable as a center speaker (cabinet large for that role).
- Quotes maker specs of 93 dB at 2.8 V, 8 Ω, 70 Hz-20 kHz (3 dB) 2pi.

## Reliability notes
Subjective only; single reviewer; Zu supplied the speakers. Quotes 93 dB where Zu's current page says 94 dB.
