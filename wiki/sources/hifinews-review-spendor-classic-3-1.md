# Hi-Fi News review: Spendor Classic 3/1 (Ed Selley, 2021-09-06)

## Summary
Outstanding-rated (HFN badge) review at the time of a UK price of GBP 2,730. Praised for seamless, effortless sound with little cabinet colouration, good bass grip and integration, and easy partnering. Page 2 raw: `raw/reviews-critic/hifinews-review-spendor-classic-3-1-p2.md`.

## Key claims
- Selley described the sound as seamless, open and effortless, with the speaker "virtually disappearing" from the soundstage.
- Bass: extension and grip held up on complex low-end tracks; some similarly priced speakers deliver more outright shove, but he never felt short-changed.
- Voices and fine detail recreated naturally and not overcooked; handled the full range of partnering amps without strain.
- Pushed to anti-social levels it hardens somewhat, but he judged it loud enough for most domestic use.
- Verdict: "buy with your head, win your heart"; tonal accuracy plus a sense of joy. Dedicated stands (GBP 995) used for all listening.
- Design notes: 3.7 kHz crossover, bi-wire/bi-amp terminals, EP77 polymer cone, rear port.
