# Hi-Fi News lab report: B&W 705 S3 Signature

## Summary
Paul Miller's lab report, printed with the October 2024 review, measures the speaker as having a tilted-up response (about 8 dB lift from 1 kHz to 10 kHz). Sensitivity is well under the claimed 88 dB at 1 kHz but closer when averaged over a wide band. Impedance is easier than claimed (minimum 4.7 Ω) with a modest EPDR.

## Method notes
HFN freefield response corrected to 1 m at 2.83 V with nearfield driver/port summing below the gating limit; impedance sweep 20 Hz-20 kHz. Date of the online lab page not shown; review issue dated 2024-10-23.
