# HiFiReport: Dynaudio Emit 20 review

## Summary
Reviewer found bass quality beyond expectation for a bookshelf (a colleague said it sounded like floorstanders) and a studio-monitor-like, detailed, non-harsh character; recommends it as high value and amp-tolerant.

## Key claims (attributed)
- Bass solid and punchy around 50 Hz electronic bass; double bass lively and elastic.
- Soft dome treble gives resolution without harshness.
- Placed about 50 cm from the back wall with panels behind; foam port plugs advised near walls.
- Quotes low-frequency limit 39 Hz (-6 dB) and crossover 3.8 kHz (maker data).

Publication date and author not captured; no measurements by the reviewer.
