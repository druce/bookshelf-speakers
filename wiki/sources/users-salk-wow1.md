# User reports: Salk WoW1 (clip)

## Summary
Few true owner reports: about 2 own-position or ownership remarks (anecdotal) amid many reactions to ASR's measurements. No QC pattern.

## Themes (counts)
- Own-position measurements or listening confirm the lower-midrange dip: 1 owner (CDMC).
- Low sensitivity / needs a strong amp: 2 (CDMC; ASR members citing the spec gap).
- Speculation of a defective ASR sample or wrong crossover: at least 3 members, unconfirmed.
- Salk build quality and customer-service reputation praised: 3 members (secondhand).
- Subwoofer recommended for a near-field setup: 1 (search summary of a personal write-up).

Fewer than 5 owner reports; anecdotal.
