# SoundStage!: Focal Kanta No1 (Doug Schneider, 2019-06-01)

## Summary
Schneider called the No1 the most accurate and revealing Focal he had reviewed, still with a touch of liveliness, but limited in the bottom octave. Priced then at $5,999/pair.

## Key claims (attributed, paraphrased)
- Described a "boxless" sound and strikingly convincing imaging, attributing some of it to the smooth polymer baffle.
- Open, clean midrange and highs; cleaner than GoldenEar Triton One.R in mid and treble.
- Bass stops around 40 Hz "with a whimper"; far less depth and power than the Triton One.R; bass got lighter from a 4 Ω tap of a tube amp.
- At very loud listening level on a close-miked voice track, heard no distortion, edginess or compression.
- Called it "the one" he would go to first for a topflight two-way minimonitor.
- Specs quoted: 46 Hz-40 kHz ±3 dB, 88 dB, 8 Ω nominal / 3.9 Ω minimum; 16.6x9.2x13.4 in, 28.1 lb. Stand $999/pair.

## Method notes
Driven by 500 W solid-state monos and by 60 W tube monos; 9 ft spacing; no subwoofer.

## Reliability notes
Subjective, single reviewer.
