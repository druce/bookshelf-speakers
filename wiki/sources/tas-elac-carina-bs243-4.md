# The Absolute Sound: Elac Carina BS243.4 (2020-03-13)

## Summary
Gader describes a ripe, slightly dark midrange, smooth fatigue-free JET treble and surprising bass weight and dynamics for a 5.25 in driver. Placement matters because of strong mid-bass.

## Key claims (attributed, paraphrased)
- More nuanced, more linear and finer in low-level micro-dynamics than Elac's Debut and Uni-Fi.
- Neutral midband, not tilted up; vocals slightly darker and chestier; slightly dry compared with his ATC reference.
- Solid bass extension into the 50-60 Hz range; strong midbass output; he pulled the speakers at least a couple of feet from the back wall and still heard some bass thickness.
- Dynamics: swings with the best small monitors even at higher levels; large symphonic works sound somewhat dry.
- Imaging and soundstage above average for the class; more forward, monitor-like presentation.
- Verdict: rates it very high for musicality and value. No numeric score in the article.

## Method notes
Subjective only; no measurements. Price then $1,199/pair.
