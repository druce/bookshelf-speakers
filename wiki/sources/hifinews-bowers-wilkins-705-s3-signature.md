# Hi-Fi News: B&W 705 S3 Signature review

## Summary
Mark Craven finds a rich, energetic, forward-leaning speaker with good image detail and punchy bass, and gives it 87% for sound quality. He links the sparkle to the presence/treble emphasis in Paul Miller's lab report.

## Key claims
- Sound described as rich, energetic and suited to any genre; detailed, authentic imaging.
- Bass punchy and deep for size; double bass "detailed, textured and lifelike".
- Treble emphasis gives a sparkly, forward presentation; generally controlled, but he nudged the level down on a trebly recording.
- Dynamic, large-scaled presentation; bass impresses even away from boundaries; reviewer advises following B&W positioning guidance.
- Build/finish superb. Hi-Fi News sound quality score: 87%.
- Spec detail: two-way crossover at 3.5 kHz; same 345 x 192 x 297 mm size as the standard 705 S3; UK price GBP 2,600 for standard model.

## Method notes
Listening review with Primare A35.2 power amp, Michi P5 S2 preamp/DAC, Hegel Viking CD.

## Reliability notes
