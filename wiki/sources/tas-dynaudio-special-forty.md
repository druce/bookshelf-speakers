# TAS: Dynaudio Special Forty review (Chris Martens, 2018-01-30)

## Summary
A strongly positive review: Martens describes a full-bodied, expressive sound with deep bass for the size, big dynamics and a large soundstage, and calls it very highly recommended and a terrific value at $2,999/pair.

## Key claims
- Sounds like a larger floorstander if heard behind a scrim; neutral without warm colouration but rich tonal colours.
- Bass: deep, with weight and punch; ran ports open in a moderately large room (foam bungs supplied).
- Dynamics and three-dimensional soundstage described as outstanding; vocals with palpable presence.
- No criticism of the cabinet, mids or treble recorded.

## Method notes
Rega Osiris integrated, PS Audio DirectStream DAC, treated room.

## Reliability notes
Single reviewer; enthusiastic prose, no measurements; does not mention the resonances found by Stereophile and Erin's Audio Corner.
