# Steve Huff: Audio Craftsmen Victoria (2025-01-05)

## Summary
Enthusiastic subjective review on a very expensive reference system; calls it a "high end bargain" at $3,995.

## Key claims
- Refined, smooth, intimate sound with sweet, silky midrange and smooth treble, a slight smoky warmth and politeness.
- Compared with a large open-baffle speaker it is smaller-sounding but cleaner and more refined.
- Sounds fine at low level but slightly thinner; bass hits harder with a stronger DAC/amp chain.
- Worked well even with $1,400 mono amps; best placement about 8 ft apart toed in to the ear for more midrange immediacy.
- Praises double-walled cabinet and SEAS drivers.

## Reliability notes
Tier D; a hobbyist-enthusiast site, loaner context not stated in what I read; no measurements. Conflicts with the Klippel result (recessed midrange, limited bass) from [[eac-audio-craftsmen-victoria]].
