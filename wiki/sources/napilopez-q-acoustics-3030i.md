# napilopez: Q Acoustics 3030i quasi-anechoic spinorama (2020-04-10)

## Summary
Hobbyist (napilopez) quasi-anechoic CTA-2034 measurement of the 3030i with near-field bass spliced to the listening window at 385 Hz. He finds neutral on-axis and listening-window tonality, a resonance near 1.2 kHz, wide directivity up to ~6 kHz and noticeably more bass than the 3020i.

## Method notes
Quasi-anechoic gated measurement (taller aspect ratio than Klippel setups); bass below 385 Hz from near-field splice, so low-frequency extension carries more uncertainty (Tier B). Driver near-field plots were taken a few mm from drivers.

## Reliability notes
Single sample, author-measured; the spinorama.org record is webplotdigitizer data. Author wrote "Better than the 3020i, and more bass" (Tier D opinion).
