# Erin's Audio Corner: Buchardt E50 (Klippel)

## Summary
Erin's review is a YouTube video with no written page, so this source rests on the Klippel NFS export and the spinorama.org summary page. The E50 measures with a listening window within about ±1.9 dB, f3 near 42 Hz and sensitivity near 83 dB, which is low. Preference score is 5.84 (7.61 with a subwoofer). No impedance, distortion or compression numbers could be read for this source.
