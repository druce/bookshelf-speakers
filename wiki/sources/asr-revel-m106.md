# ASR: Revel M106 review (2020-06-26)

## Summary
Klippel NFS review of the Performa3 M106 (6.5" two-way). Amir reports very smooth early-window response, a predicted in-room response close to ideal, and distortion that "hugs the bottom" at 86 dB and barely rises at 96 dB above about 1 kHz. He notes woofer beaming against a wider tweeter beam produces an off-axis dip near crossover, and that impedance means it needs a capable amplifier. His conclusion calls it one of the most perfect bookshelf speakers he has heard and gives his strongest recommendation.

## Method notes
Klippel NFS, >800 points, anechoic-equivalent. The sample was a pre-owned unit with full packaging purchased and drop-shipped to the reviewer. Dealer relationship (Madrona Digital) disclosed. Distortion at 86 dB and 96 dB SPL @1 m is shown relative and absolute.

## Reliability notes
Single, not-new sample. Graph-read values approximate. LF distortion near 100 Hz is displacement/motor driven, as for most small ported speakers.
