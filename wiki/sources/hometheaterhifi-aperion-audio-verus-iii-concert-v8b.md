# HomeTheaterHifi: Verus III Concert V8B review

## Summary
Listening plus bench test: 1 m on-axis dual-gate response, single-tone THD, step response, CSD and impedance. Reviewer calls it a winner: tight bass, clear mids, smooth treble. Publication year is about 2023 (image uploads 2023-02/03); the fetch header date of 2026-10-07 is unreliable.

## Method notes
Microphone 1 m on-axis; dual-gate impulse-derived response, not anechoic below a few hundred Hz. Listening on Pass Labs X600.8 with subwoofers active in some tests.

## Reliability notes
Single reviewer; no spinorama or off-axis data. His 4.5 Ω minimum disagrees with Erin's 5.7 Ω above 80 Hz (different method, sample and voltage).
