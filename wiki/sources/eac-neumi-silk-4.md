# EAC: Neumi Silk 4 review (page dated 2023-10-26; indexed 2023-11-06)

## Summary
Erin measured a manufacturer-loaned Silk 4 on the Klippel NFS. He finds resonances near 600 Hz and 1 kHz (likely port-related), a slightly laid-back treble in the nearfield, F3 of 83 Hz and F10 of 55 Hz, and surprisingly clean output for its size. Verdict: a solid $150 little speaker, much improved with EQ.

## Method notes
Klippel NFS, reference point at tweeter, grille off, speaker broken in. Multitone distortion tests run full-range and with an 80 Hz brick-wall high-pass (graphs only; no max-SPL figure stated). Subjective (Tier D, Erin): resonance near 600 Hz and 1 kHz noticeable on some notes; treble slightly laid back up close, better beyond a few feet; suggests boosting 400-500 Hz and above 4 kHz or a -1.5 dB wide cut near 1.2 kHz when used at a desk.

## Reliability notes
Single loaned sample; Erin states the manufacturer did not see the review before release. Computed F3 and Erin's text F3 differ by 10 Hz.
