# TONEAudio: Paradigm Monitor SE Atom

## Summary
Enthusiastic budget-speaker spotlight written around the $298/pair launch price (about 2018; date not in capture). No measurements.

## Key claims
- Reviewer described an immersive soundstage with wide imaging, while noting no deep bass or very deep soundstage at the price.
- Called basic linearity a strength; plays loud for its size and holds the image together at low level.
- Very wide dispersion makes placement easy; described as tube-friendly and easy to live with.
- Verdict: among the best budget compact speakers heard.

## Method notes
Used with vintage receivers, PS Audio Sprout, Dynaco Stereo 70 and high-end gear.

## Reliability notes
Short, anecdotal write-up; "linearity" is a listening claim, and ASR measured a bumpy on-axis response.
