# Hi-Fi News lab report: B&W 805 D4

## Summary
Paul Miller finds a "dished" response with treble about 6 dB above the midband at 10 kHz, so sensitivity is below the claimed 88 dB. Bass extension is good thanks to a 41 Hz port, impedance is not especially difficult (minimum 4.65 Ω), and distortion is low.

## Method notes
HFN quasi-anechoic freefield response with nearfield summing for the bass; impedance 20 Hz-20 kHz.
