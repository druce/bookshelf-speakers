# User reports: ATC SCM19

## Summary
Two older forum threads, 12 posters total, about 9 giving opinions (WebFetch paraphrase). Anecdotal, original-generation speaker.

## Themes
- Positive sound/bass dynamics: 3 reports.
- Wants a capable, high-voltage amp (low sensitivity): 4 reports; disputed as a myth by 2; 2 report no trouble with Audiolab / modest amps.
- QC/reliability: none; one owner's visible cone wires judged normal by a dealer (1 report).
