# Secrets: NHT C3 review (2018)

## Summary
Listening plus bench review of two C3 samples. Haider found a clear, neutral sound with a slight midrange forwardness, "superbly flat" outdoor on-axis response and a tight CEA-2034A listening window, about 120 degrees coverage to 7 kHz, and bass that needed subwoofers. Won a Secrets Best of 2018 award. Published date is approximate: page header shows 2026-10-07, but the image paths and tags are 2018.

## Method notes
Frequency response measured outdoors (ground-plane style, 1/12-octave smoothing), on-axis and 15/30/45 degrees; polar map; impedance measured. Not full anechoic or Klippel, so low-frequency values are less reliable (Tier B). Reviewer used 21 degrees toe-in for best treble balance.

## Reliability notes
Two samples, consistent impedance. No numeric deviation figures. NHT supplied the review samples.
