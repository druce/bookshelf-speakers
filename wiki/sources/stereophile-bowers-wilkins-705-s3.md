# Stereophile: 705 S3 measurements (2023-06-20)

## Summary
Quasi-anechoic MLSSA measurements by John Atkinson of two samples. Atkinson found a tweeter output that rises about 10 dB peaking at 10 kHz (identical in both samples), a crossover near 3 kHz with shallow slopes, wide horizontal dispersion with a top-octave fall-off beyond 20 degrees, and a vertical suckout at the crossover when off the tweeter axis. He calls the behaviour "enigmatic" and says the peak adds air rather than brightness as such but makes system matching tricky.

## Method notes
DRA Labs MLSSA with DPA 4006 microphone for farfield (tweeter axis at 50", 30-degree horizontal window average, grille off), Earthworks QTC-40 for nearfield below 300 Hz. Quasi-anechoic, so low-frequency data are nearfield-merged; confidence is lower below about 300 Hz. Impedance and EPDR from Stereophile's own rig.

## Reliability notes
Two samples measured with matching tweeter behaviour. Tier B; no CTA-2034 spinorama, so spinorama-derived fields stay null.
