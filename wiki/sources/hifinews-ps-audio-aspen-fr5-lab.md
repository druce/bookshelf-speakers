# Hi-Fi News lab report: PS Audio Aspen FR5

## Summary
Paul Miller's lab report finds 40 Hz (-6 dB re 200 Hz, diffraction-corrected) bass extension, an uneven forward response (dished presence region), sensitivity of 81.7 dB over 500 Hz-8 kHz, and an easy impedance with EPDR down to 2.5 Ω. Graphs and the data table in the raw capture did not survive scraping; values below come from the text.
