# Stereophile review: ATC SCM7 v.3 (John Marks, 2014)

Raw also includes page 2: raw/reviews-critic/stereophile-review-atc-scm7-p2.md.

## Summary
Marks calls it highly recommended and an "extremely competitive" entry among British shoebox monitors.

## Key claims
- Pinpoint imaging and excellent detail retrieval; no audible distortion, attributed to a robust woofer.
- Bass "respectable" rather than "convincing"; no extension below the midbass, a non-issue for choral music.
- Woofer very tough: huge excursions on a 5 Hz glide tone with no distress sounds.
- Treble blended with mid after break-in; tweeter judged more extended than the older SCM11's.
- Price then $1,499/pair; less transparent than a far costlier Vivid Oval V1.5.

## Reliability notes
Tier D, 2014; review pair matches the v.3 measured by John Atkinson ([[stereophile-atc-scm7]]).
