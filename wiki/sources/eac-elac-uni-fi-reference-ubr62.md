# Erin's Audio Corner: ELAC UBR62 (2021-06-21)

## Summary
Klippel NFS measurement of a three-way coaxial standmount. Listening window is good (+/-1.9 dB), dispersion is unusually wide for a coaxial, and sensitivity is low at about 83 dB. Erin liked the wide soundstage but heard slight upper-female-vocal resonance and notes an upper-midbass dip near 150 Hz.

## Method notes
Klippel NFS, CTA-2034. Subjective listening at 3-4 m, 80-95 dB, on Parasound integrated amps; 110 W integrated was not enough, the stronger HINT 6 was used. Erin suggests about 10 degrees toe-in or toe-out rather than on-axis. Long-term compression plots exist (not read).
