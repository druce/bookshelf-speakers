# Stereophile: PSB Alpha P5 (John Atkinson, 2019-09-27)

## Summary
Atkinson's MLSSA measurements find a flat, even response on the woofer axis (slight midrange dip and a narrow suck-out below 4 kHz), sensitivity of 85 dB, and minimum impedance of 3.85 Ω at 190 Hz. He concludes it measures excellently for the price; ASR disputes that reading of broadly similar data. Also lists a Stereophile product-of-the-year award for 2019 (per maker page).

## Key claims (Tier D, John Atkinson)
- Bass full to the 63 Hz band, with 32 Hz just audible via room mode; no port chuffing at realistic levels.
- Voices uncoloured; treble a little mellow; imaging less precise than KEF LS50 and Rogers LS3/5a.
- Clarity best below about 90 dBC at the listening seat; slam is "pretty good" for the size.
- Verdict: outclassed by dearer speakers but not by a wide margin; "scores super big-time" for the money. Extra raw: raw/reviews-critic/stereophile-review-psb-alpha-p5.md, -p2.md, -specs.md.

## Method notes
DRA Labs MLSSA, DPA 4006, farfield at 50", on woofer axis (tweeter is at the bottom), grille off, nearfield for LF.

## Reliability notes
Single review pair. Quasi-anechoic, Tier B below about 300 Hz.
