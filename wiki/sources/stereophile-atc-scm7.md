# Stereophile: ATC SCM7 v.3 measurements (John Atkinson, 2014)

## Summary
MLSSA quasi-anechoic farfield plus nearfield woofer measurements. Sensitivity 2 dB under spec, a sealed-box bass with only modest extension, a mild 500-700 Hz anomaly, and wide even horizontal dispersion. A mellow top octave suits close listening.

## Method notes
DRA Labs MLSSA with DPA 4006 mic farfield and Earthworks QTC-40 nearfield; no grille; serial 1002. Response on tweeter axis at 50 in, 30° horizontal window average.

## Reliability notes
Single sample; no spinorama so no numeric LW/f3. Graphs not downloaded; values from text.
