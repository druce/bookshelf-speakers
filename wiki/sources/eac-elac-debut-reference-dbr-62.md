# Erin's Audio Corner: ELAC DBR62 (2023-07-16)

## Summary
Klippel NFS measurement of a self-purchased, broken-in pair, grille off, reference at the tweeter. Listening-window flatness is excellent and the speaker is wide-dispersion; sensitivity is low at 83.9 dB. The written page is data-only; discussion is in a video not ingested.

## Method notes
