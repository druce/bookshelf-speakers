# Audioholics: Revel Performa M126Be review (2019-08-16)

## Summary
Outdoor, gated free-air measurements plus listening. Larson reports an exceptionally flat, wide off-axis response, usable bass below 50 Hz, a benign impedance load and 87.9 dB sensitivity. He rated performance and value 5 stars and called it one of the very best medium-sized bookshelf speakers. Revel also supplied its own spin for the same sample.

## Method notes
Free-air at 7.5 ft height, 2 m, gated at 9 ms: resolution lost below 250 Hz and accuracy lost below 110 Hz; 1/12-octave smoothing; groundplane for bass; port-plugged curve also measured. No distortion or compression measurements.

## Reliability notes
Gated outdoor measurement (Tier A per CLAUDE.md for Audioholics but with limited LF and no numeric spinorama values extracted). Sensitivity is 2 dB above the Klippel computation. Single review sample from the manufacturer.
