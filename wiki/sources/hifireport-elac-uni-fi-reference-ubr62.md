# HiFiReport: ELAC UBR62

## Summary
Positive listening review on an Arcam SA30 (and an Onkyo receiver), no measurements and no numeric score.

## Key claims
- Rich mid-bass texture and a linear, natural sense of extension; timpani rendered well.
- Warmer tonality suits vocals and jazz; wide, stable soundstage.
- Detail across the spectrum; credits the three-way design and 260 Hz / 1.8 kHz crossovers.
- Approx. $1,193 for a 3-way coaxial described as rare at the price.

## Method notes
Subjective only; bass-depth claims lean on the maker's 41 Hz figure.
