# TNT-Audio: XSA Labs Vanguard (M.L. Gneier, June 2023)

## Summary
Positive listening test positioning the Vanguard as a LS3/5a variant at 995 USD/pair (bamboo). Calls it fantastic.

## Key claims
- Reviewer described tonal balance as integrated, with no sense of separate tweeter and woofer.
- Top end extended but smooth, "polite" yet musical.
- Strong on acoustic music and voices; more guts than expected when loud, but strained at high levels.
- Best on 25-27 inch stands with ears near tweeter height.
- Build precision (joinery, binding posts) praised.

## Reliability notes
Subjective only; no measurements. Single reviewer, long-time LS3/5a enthusiast.
