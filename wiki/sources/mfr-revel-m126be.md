# Revel M126Be: maker specs and US availability

## Summary
Revel's US site (revelspeakers.com) still lists the M126Be under in-room loudspeakers; the product page I tried (revelspeakers.com/products/performa-m126be) returned HTTP 410 to scripted fetch, so specs are taken from Audio Advice's reproduction of Revel's data.
