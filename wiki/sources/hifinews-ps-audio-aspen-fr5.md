# Hi-Fi News: PS Audio Aspen FR5 (Mark Craven; first published 2024-10-01)

## Summary
Craven finds the FR5 seriously entertaining for the money: deep, controlled bass, clean treble and a polished presentation across genres. The main limit is soundstage scale and ultimate output. Sound Quality score 89%. Lab figures are on [hifinews-ps-audio-aspen-fr5-lab](hifinews-ps-audio-aspen-fr5-lab.md).

## Key claims
- Bass: tight, meaty and controlled for the size; deep electronic bass handled well (Craven).
- Treble: tweeter picks out hi-hats and cymbals without sibilance or harshness.
- Imaging and scale: soundstage is "CinemaScope rather than IMAX"; gives ground to larger speakers in envelopment.
- Dynamics: grip and slam on rock material; limits in sheer output and scale acknowledged.
- Verdict: lush-sounding, better than most at the price; Sound Quality 89%. UK price GBP 3,500 (GBP 4,000 with stands).
