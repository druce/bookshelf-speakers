# SoundStage!/NRC: Dynaudio Special Forty measurements (2018)

## Summary
Anechoic measurements at Canada's NRC for the 2018 SoundStage! review. Sensitivity 83.6 dB, a 6 Ω-rated load with a graph-read minimum near 4.7 Ω around 200 Hz, and THD+N at 90 dB that is highest (about 2%) near 100 Hz. The page publishes curves but no spinorama or CSV; the listening window is flat within roughly +/-2 dB above 200 Hz by eye.

## Extracted values
| Field | Value | Note |
|---|---|---|
| sensitivity_db_2v83 | 83.6 dB | stated, averaged 300 Hz-3 kHz, listening window, 2.83 V/1 m |
| min_impedance_ohm | about 4.7 Ω near 200 Hz | graph-read (approx.) |
| impedance peaks | about 35 Ω near 35 Hz; about 30 Ω near 85 Hz | graph-read (approx.) |
| lw_response | roughly 83-86 dB, 100 Hz-20 kHz; peak at about 550 Hz, dip near 1.3 kHz | graph-read (approx.); LW = 5-point average |
| low-frequency extension | LW about 76 dB at 45-50 Hz vs about 84 dB mid-band | graph-read (approx.); no f3 stated |
| THD+N @ 90 dB | about 2% (-34 dB) near 100 Hz; below plot floor (about -45 dB or lower) above ~250 Hz | graph-read (approx.); level 90 dB, not 86/96 |

## Method notes
NRC anechoic chamber, measured at 2 m and plotted at 1 m; microphone between tweeter and midrange-woofer; grille off. Listening window is a 5-point average (on-axis, +/-15 deg horizontal and vertical), not the CTA-2034 9-point window.

## Reliability notes
Single sample supplied by the manufacturer for review. Values are graph-read; no f3 or deviation figures stated by the source.
