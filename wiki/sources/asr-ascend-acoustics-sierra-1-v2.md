# ASR: Ascend Sierra-1 V2 review (2024-03-29)

## Summary
Klippel NFS measurement of a member-loaned, user-purchased Sierra-1 V2 pair, a second sample after Erin's. Amir finds a good on-axis response with minor port/driver resonances and a dip near 3 kHz, an unusually smooth early-reflection response and an excellent predicted in-room curve. The woofer is not graceful at high SPL given the low sensitivity; 102 dB is outside its envelope. Added to his recommended list; light EQ improved his listening impression (Tier D).

## Method notes
Klippel NFS, grille off. THD is a non-anechoic sweep (a forum member says it is taken near-field, about 1/3 m, and scaled to 1 m). Listening notes: pleasant but not exciting until EQ filled the 3 kHz dip and pulled down two port resonances (Tier D). Amir reports deep sub-bass distortion on "speaker killer" tracks only. He sent the data to the designer, who said it correlates with Ascend's own measurements.

## Reliability notes
Independent, user-purchased sample (loan from a member). ASR plots THD in percent with a 5 % ceiling, so high-level values are clipped. Graph-read values approximate.
