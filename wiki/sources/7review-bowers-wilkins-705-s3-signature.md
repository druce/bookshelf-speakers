# 7Review: B&W 705 S3 Signature

## Summary
English-language rendition of a German magazine test. Lothar Brandt hears an impeccably neutral, fresh, detailed speaker with punchy bass and finds the Signature even more coherent than the standard 705 S3, at a large price premium. Overall score 8/10 (161/200 points).

## Key claims
- Sound called neutral, detailed, well organised, punchy bass; more homogeneous and coherent than the standard 705 S3.
- Cons: significantly more expensive than the standard model; "not a deep-bass miracle".
- Scores: neutrality 15/20, resolution 18/20, imaging 16/20, dynamics 15/20, bass 16/20; lab analysis 37/50; price/performance "outstanding".
- Price quoted at $4,200 pair (US), EUR 4,000 list; retailer widget showed $4,500.

## Method notes
Listening with Luxman L-509Z and a Lehmannaudio/Accustic Arts combination. The lab numbers (frequency response, cutoff) were not in the captured page text, so no measurements are taken from this source.

## Reliability notes
Single sample; affiliate links on page; prices differ from current B&W US $5,200.
