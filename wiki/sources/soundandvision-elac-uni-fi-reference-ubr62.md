# Sound & Vision: Elac UBR62 system (2022-03-23)

## Summary
Review of a 5.0 system (four UBR62 plus a UCR52 center) with SVS subwoofers; a Top Pick. Page 2 is saved at raw/reviews-critic/soundandvision-elac-uni-fi-reference-ubr62-p2.md.

## Key claims
- Plus: clean dialogue, silky detail, big open presentation; minus: limited deep bass without a sub.
- Without subs: open, natural, detailed; male voices a bit warmer than he likes; little "wow factor" but easy, sweet sound.
- Lacked bass in his very large room; Audyssey below 300 Hz cleaned up the 100-200 Hz bump but did not deepen bass; with two subs the speakers sounded big and imposing.
- Aimed the fronts inward more sharply than usual for the concentric driver.
- Cabinets said to be vinyl-wrapped wood grain; crossovers 260 Hz / 1.8 kHz; priced $1,200 per pair.

## Method notes
Listening only (a separate in-room test bench page exists, not ingested). Subs and room EQ affect conclusions.

## Reliability notes
Sample supplied by Elac. Stated in-room measurements are not anechoic and not used.
