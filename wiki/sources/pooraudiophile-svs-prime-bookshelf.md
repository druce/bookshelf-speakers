# Poor Audiophile: SVS Prime Bookshelf review (2015-02-02)

## Summary
Independent enthusiast-blog review of the launch model with an SB-2000 sub, run for a month on a 50 W Emotiva mini amp and a month on a high-end rig. Positive overall; it drove easily from modest amps.

## Key claims
- Detailed, solid tonal character with strong imaging and off-axis behaviour for the price.
- Reserved in drum impact, cymbal top-end detail and bass below 48 Hz without a sub.
- Hint of boxiness and not the vocal purity of far dearer speakers (reviewer's own comparison).
- With sub and good electronics the speakers "disappeared"; soundstage somewhat small, as with most speakers this size.

## Method notes
Subjective only; no measurements. Original 2014-15 price $499-599 per pair.

## Reliability notes
Maker-supplied sample, hobbyist reviewer, over 10 years old and may not reflect current production.
