# TNT-Audio: Elac Uni-Fi 6.2 Reference (July 2024)

## Summary
Negative listening test of the UBR62 in direct comparison with Elac's cheaper Debut speakers. Approx. price EUR 800. Day of month not stated; July 2024 used.

## Key claims
- Standing alone: substantially correct, defined and extended mid-high, good but not deep or powerful bass.
- Compared with Debut 5.1 and Debut 6.2 Reference it lacked cohesion, communication, impact and imaging; bass described as a little late.
- Sounded best parallel to the rear wall in his room; concentric unit needs placement experiments.
- Concludes the price premium over the Debut 6.2 Reference is not justified.

## Method notes
Subjective comparison with two other listeners confirming. Outlier against the other critic reviews.

## Reliability notes
Sample supplied by an Italian distributor. Review is of the same model (UBR62).
