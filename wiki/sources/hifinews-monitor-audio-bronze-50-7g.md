# Hi-Fi News lab report: Monitor Audio Bronze 50 7G

## Summary
The PM-signed lab report measures two samples: flat forward response, sensitivity above spec, and a load tougher than the 8 Ω rating, with minimum impedance 4.18 Ω and EPDR down to 2.05 Ω. Publication date and issue were not captured (the review is from 2025).

## Method notes
Hi-Fi News lab: quasi-anechoic farfield plus nearfield summed driver/port response corrected to 1 m at 2.83 V. The saved page lost its "Measured Specifications" table (only prose and captions captured), so only text values are entered.

## Reliability notes
Two samples, good agreement. Quasi-anechoic, so less certain below about 300 Hz. No directivity data beyond a mention of a flatter response off-axis.
