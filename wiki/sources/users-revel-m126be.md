# User reports: Revel M126Be (clip)

## Summary
Only about 3 owner-type comments were found (anecdotal): one detailed ASR thread plus two Audiogon remarks known only through a search summary. No Reddit or AVS Forum owner threads surfaced.

## Themes (counts)
- Strong overall sound, smooth/dynamic: 2 of 3 reports.
- Wants a subwoofer / bass extension limited: 1 of 3 (Audiogon); the ASR owner also ran a sub.
- Large symphonic crescendos slightly strained or compressed: 1 of 3 (ASR owner).
- Could not perceive soundstage: 1 of 3 (ASR owner, suspects setup).
- QC/reliability: none reported.

## Reliability notes
Fewer than 5 reports; do not treat as a pattern.
