# StereoNET: RP-600M II review (2022)

## Summary
Listening review (Applause Award) with Naim and Leak tube amplifiers. Describes a big, lively, dynamic speaker that is not neutral, best with rock and pop, less suited to classical and delicate voices. Publication date approximate (2022 per site category).

## Key claims
- Pickford (Tier D): big, bold, immersive sound with strong dynamics and a broad sweet spot and deep soundstage.
- Far from neutral; adds warmth and body to thin recordings; bass generous but not slow.
- Horn tweeter adds bite; woofer and tweeter do not blend like a point-source, giving a "live PA" character.
- Raspy horn voicing and fatter double bass on a jazz recording; less ideal for classical, Bach.
- Easy to drive (94.5 dB claimed, 8 Ω); suits low-power tubes. Price quoted £729.

## Method notes
Listening only, ungrilled.

## Reliability notes
No measurements; sensitivity statement repeats the manufacturer claim.
