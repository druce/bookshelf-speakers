# Hi-Fi Choice: ELAC Debut B6.2 (2018-12-14)

## Summary
Warmly recommended; stunning stereo soundstaging for the money, with a slight forwardness in the midrange.

## Key claims
- Bass firm with excellent timing, not trying to dig too deep.
- Very strong central imaging; straight-ahead (no toe-in) placement widened the stage while keeping focus.
- Slight midrange forwardness; strident on some material, so needs careful amp/source matching.
- Treble sweeter and more open than the first-generation B6, composed at high volume.

## Reliability notes
Tier D; UK magazine; no rating figure captured.
