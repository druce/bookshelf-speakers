# SoundStage! Xperience: ATC SCM19 V2 (Wes Marshall, October 2016)

## Summary
Subjective review by a former ATC SCM50ASL owner; no measurements. Price then $4,299/pair.

## Key claims
- Very high clean output for the size; "effortless" dynamics and no audible crossover seam.
- Excellent decay of instruments in room ambience.
- Slightly opaque in the highest frequencies in one passage, which he thought most would not notice.
- Sealed cabinet gives slow bass rolloff; could excite his room at 41 Hz with a bass line.
- Recommended pairing with ATC P1 power amp.

## Reliability notes
Xperience is not the NRC/SoundStage measurement outlet, so no Tier A/B data. Tier D only.
