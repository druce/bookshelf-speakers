# SoundStage! Access: Kin One music system (2024-12-15)

## Summary
Review of the Kin One speakers with the Kin Amp ($1,199 system). Capture is a WebFetch paraphrase, not verbatim.

## Key claims
- Thom Moon found treble and upper mids a little bright at times, fine on acoustic, jazz and classical.
- Bass lacks the deepest octave; a subwoofer is needed in a large room.
- Broad soundstage with stable guitar placement; good rhythm for the size.
- Could not play loudly in a 22-ft-wide room without strain.
- Verdict: very listenable and well suited to dorm, den or office.

## Method notes
Reviewed as a system with the Kin Amp (100 W class D), so amplifier influence is mixed in.

## Reliability notes
Model-extracted capture. Single reviewer.
