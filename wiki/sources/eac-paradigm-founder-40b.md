# Erin's Audio Corner: Paradigm Founder 40B review (2025-01-29)

## Summary
Klippel NFS measurements of a loaned pair. Erin found the on-axis response tilts upward at high frequencies and, because of constant directivity, still needs EQ in a room even when aimed off-axis; bass extension is limited so a sub suits home theatre use. Distortion and compression are low.

## Method notes
Klippel NFS; reference point at tweeter; no grille; broken-in pair. Data mixes 0° and 30° horizontal (the 30° axis is Paradigm's recommended listening axis; spinorama.org lists the 30° set). Impedance at 0.10 V RMS.

## Reliability notes
Single loaned sample. spinorama.org categorises the entry as "passive center", which is a labelling error for this standmount. No raw spinorama export was in the packet, so metrics here are from the review text, graphs and the spinorama.org page, not scripts/cta2034.py. The summaries differ slightly on preference score (4.89 vs 4.99 reported elsewhere); 4.89/7.11 taken from the page fetched directly.
