# Stereophile review: SVS Ultra Evolution Bookshelf + SB-3000 (Tom Fine, 2026-08-21)

## Summary
Reviewed as a 2.1 system with the SB-3000 subwoofer. Alone the speakers gave good extension for their size but could not fully excite the room at low levels; with the sub they sounded full-sized.

## Key claims
- Compared with his KEF LS50 Meta, Fine heard better low-frequency extension and a brighter, less polite top end, a more "American" sound with meaty but not flabby bass.
- Imaging was wide and tall; he found the sound smoother after a few dozen hours of break-in.
- Alone, bass could not fully load the room (e.g. electric bass on a soul track), particularly at low volume; he suggests a sub for SPL-constrained listening.
- They stayed clean and stable as loud as he cared to play.
- Partnered with an Onkyo Icon A-50 integrated; no explicit star rating captured.

## Reliability notes
System review: judgements partly reflect the subwoofer. Sample supplied by SVS.
