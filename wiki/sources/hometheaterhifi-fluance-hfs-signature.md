# HomeTheaterHiFi: Fluance Signature HFS (Taps Das)

## Summary
Sponsor-supplied sample reviewed as home-theatre rears and as a stereo pair replacing B&W 685 S1 speakers. Reviewer was impressed for the price and recommends it for budget or first systems, with a sub for bass.

## Key claims
- Dialogue clear and lifelike; vocals ample weight at low and moderate volume.
- Played loud without obvious strain on several tracks; female vocal slightly shrill on one track at very loud levels.
- Bass lean on bass-heavy tracks; recommends a subwoofer. Some flatness/lack of dynamics noted on one track.
- Verdict: great value, "quite impressed"; no listed dislikes.

## Method notes
Listening only, no measurements. Cambridge CXA60 amp.

## Reliability notes
Manufacturer-supplied sample, retailer-friendly outlet. Contrasts with ASR measurements and listening.
