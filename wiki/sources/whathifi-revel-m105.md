# What Hi-Fi?: Revel Performa3 M105 review (2021-07-20)

## Summary
Review originally from Australian Hi-Fi magazine, republished on What Hi-Fi?. It calls the M105 one of the most accurate, revealing loudspeakers the reviewers had heard, suitable for studio monitoring or home listening. No star rating is shown in the captured text; cons listed are low-ish sensitivity and stands not included.

## Key claims
- Bass "outstandingly neutral", clean at higher levels, but the lowest octave sits a little lower in level than on an M106 or F206 because the curved cabinet limits volume.
- Maximum output requires leaving the port plugs out and positioning near a rear wall (their audition position).
- Midrange judged flawless in balance on voice and acoustic guitar; highs silky and smooth.
- Verdict: monitor-grade accuracy, stunning sound, superb finish; stands (Performa3 M stands) sold separately.
- Priced AU$2,799 / £1,599 / US$1,500 in the headline.

## Method notes
Listening only; no measurements in the captured text.
