# ASR: NHT SuperZero 2.1 review (2020-12-21)

## Summary
Klippel NFS review of a unit bought for $70 (single). Amir found almost no bass (tiny sealed box), very low sensitivity, a directivity mismatch at the 4-5 kHz woofer/tweeter handover and a dip in the 2-3 kHz region. Distortion is high at low frequencies. He disliked it in listening and gave no recommendation.

## Method notes
Klippel NFS, tweeter-center reference, 60 F, over 800 measurements. Listening notes (Tier D): no bass, quiet, 2-3 kHz weak; 3 dB boost there helped a little; Amir switched it off.

## Reliability notes
Single sample. Reviewer judged it without a subwoofer although the maker says one is required; forum members objected (see [[users-nht-super-zero-2-1]]).
