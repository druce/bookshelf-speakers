# HiFiReport: Neumi Silk 4 review

## Summary
A Taiwan-based review (price quoted as NTD 10,900, about $363) that finds the Silk 4 smooth and slightly warm, with a wide soundstage and clean, not deep, bass. Recommended for small rooms and desktops. Publication date not stated (images uploaded 2025-04).

## Key claims
- Described the tonal balance as smooth, natural and slightly warm; details not pushed forward.
- Found dispersion commendable and the "disappearing speaker" effect attainable with positioning.
- Said bass is clean and honest without bloat, though not deep; a powered sub is suggested for living rooms.
- Singled out vocals as the strongest area; judged movie playback adequate in small rooms.
- Cautioned that pushing the volume brings resonance and constrained dynamics.
- Drove them successfully with a 13 W desktop amp.

## Method notes
Subjective only, no measurements. Magnetic grilles and woven-veneer MDF cabinet noted.
