# Hi-Fi News review: Monitor Audio Bronze 50 7G

## Summary
Reviewer (name not captured) auditioned on 24" stands with WiiM Amp and Primare A35.2. Verdict: more accomplished than some standmounts at twice the price, a must-audition; Sound Quality 88%, awarded a "gold star".

## Key claims (attributed)
- Bass notably robust and "true", deep but controlled, with snap and no booming; larger woofer than the 6G model.
- Balanced mids and treble; treble described as straight and true with excellent off-axis uniformity, tolerant of placement.
- Soundstage widescreen rather than pin-sharp; not an analytical tool.
- Performance fine with a WiiM Amp; more authoritative bass with the bigger amp.
- Recommends removing grilles (see [[hifinews-monitor-audio-bronze-50-7g]]).
