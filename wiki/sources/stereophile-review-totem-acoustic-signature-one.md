# Stereophile: Totem Signature One review (Herb Reichert, 2017-12-28)

## Summary
Reichert, an owner of the earlier Model 1 Signature, finds the new 8 Ω Signature One purer and clearer than its predecessor.

## Key claims
- Sounded pure, clear, quiet; delicate textures rather than sturdy, upfront ones.
- Response sounded flat and undistorted from about 60 Hz to 6 kHz, except for a narrow midrange band of noise or confusion near 500-600 Hz (matches measured resonances).
- Organ recordings sounded full and forceful for a small speaker; bass taut and tonally accurate.
- Intimate, small scale compared with horns; placed 28 in to 36 in from wall.
- Specs quoted: 8 Ω nominal, 6.5 in cellulose-acrylate cone, 1 in SEAS Al-Ti dome, 2.7 kHz crossover, 19 lb.

## Reliability notes
Single listener, extended break-in noted. No rating.
