# SoundStage!: Dynaudio Special Forty review (Doug Schneider, 2018-01-15)

## Summary
A Reviewers' Choice review in a large (about 36 x 18 ft) room. Schneider initially found bass thuddy and treble slightly tipped up, which improved after about 50 hours of run-in; he then described a very uncolored, detailed sound with a wide soundstage, but no bottom octave. Measurements for this review are in [[soundstage-nrc-dynaudio-special-forty]].

## Key claims
- After run-in, balance seemed neutral from about 40 Hz up; bass was tight and very clean, with no audible port noise at high levels.
- Missing 20-40 Hz octave, and the speaker cannot play as loudly as larger floorstanders; it still played normal and somewhat above-normal levels without strain in his big room.
- Described as free of the colorations he found in other Dynaudio two-ways; soft-dome tweeter did not sound reticent.
- Grilles on subtly reduced highs and added midrange splashiness (he listened with them off).
- Stage width and depth rated on par with any speaker heard at any price; verdict: neutrality and resolution at a reasonable price, $2,999/pair at launch.

## Method notes
Reference system with Constellation electronics, 24" stands, about 10 deg toe-in, 8 ft spacing, grilles off.

## Reliability notes
Single reviewer, loaned sample, 2018 launch-era review. Review text does not discuss the cabinet-resonance issue Stereophile reported.
