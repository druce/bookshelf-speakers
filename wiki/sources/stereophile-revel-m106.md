# Stereophile: Revel Performa3 M106 (2014)

Raw files: measurements sidebar `raw/measurements/stereophile-revel-m106.md`; listening page 2 `raw/reviews-critic/stereophile-revel-m106.md` (URL https://www.stereophile.com/content/revel-performa3-m106-loudspeaker-page-2).

## Summary
John Atkinson's MLSSA measurements find the M106 "superbly flat" to nearly 30 kHz, sensitivity 86.4 dB (vs 87 claimed), integrated crossover and clean spectral decay. A deep vertical suckout appears in the crossover region more than 5° above and 15° below the tweeter axis, suggesting high stands. The listening reviewer (initials BJR) called it extraordinary, with unsurpassed upper-bass clarity and transient articulation, and rated its neutrality and bass impressive for size.

## Method notes
DRA Labs MLSSA with DPA 4006 mic (farfield) and Earthworks QTC-40 (nearfield), tweeter-axis response averaged over a 30° horizontal window, grille and port plug off. Quasi-anechoic gated response: low-frequency data are nearfield-spliced below about 300 Hz.

## Reliability notes
Single sample. No numeric listening-window deviation or f3 stated. Listening reviewer's author name is not captured in the raw file (initials only).

## Key claims (Tier D, listening)
- Upper-bass (90-120 Hz) clarity and detail beyond any bookshelf speaker he had heard.
- Transients "unsurpassed" in articulation, clean yet relaxed.
- Beat Epos Elan 10 and Dynaudio Excite X12 in every parameter; bottom end impressive for size.
- Summing up: no reservations, "extraordinary".
