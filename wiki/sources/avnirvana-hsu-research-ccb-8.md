# AV Nirvana: Hsu CCB-8 review (2017-07-27)

## Summary
Part one of a two-part review, listening to the CCB-8 as a stereo speaker. Anderson called it highly detailed and full-range with excellent build quality and wide, even dispersion for placement in any orientation.

## Key claims
- Detail was the standout trait; he reported hearing new details in familiar recordings.
- Bass depth was good for the size.
- Summary praises build quality and manufacturer support; MSRP then $369 each.
- Describes the coaxial design as giving an evenly dispersed field (design claim, not measured).

## Method notes
Subjective listening, no measurements. Reviewer had a prior favourable view of Hsu's HB-1 MK2.

## Reliability notes
Single reviewer; 2017 sample; contradicted on dispersion by [asr-hsu-research-ccb-8](asr-hsu-research-ccb-8.md) and partly by [audioholics-hsu-research-ccb-8](audioholics-hsu-research-ccb-8.md).
