# ASR: Wilson Audio TuneTot review (2021-12-21)

## Summary
Klippel NFS review of a member-loaned pair (~$10,000). Port is tuned too high, giving a large peak near 115 Hz; directivity is irregular and lost around 3 kHz; the early-reflection and predicted in-room responses look better than on-axis. Distortion is very low at 86 dB, with a 2.6 kHz resonance audible at 96 dB. Amir concluded the measurements are poor but he liked the sound, recommending it with EQ.

## Method notes
Klippel NFS, reference at tweeter, no grille, 14 °C room. Port plug tested; measurements with port open. Listening (Tier D): bass overwhelming with port open on bass-heavy music, little bass with plug; EQ improved it; preferred TuneTot to Revel M106 (deeper, cleaner bass, more "audiophile" treble delineation but a "showroom" brightness); Revel Salon 2 more balanced.

## Reliability notes
Single loaned sample. Recommendation is "with equalization (cost not considered)", despite poor measurements.
