# Future Audiophile: B&W 705 S3 Signature

## Summary
Bob Barrett praises superb midrange and treble resolution and bass beyond what the size suggests, and says it delivers a good share of the 805 D4 Signature's performance for much less money.

## Key claims
- Midrange lush, full and timbrally accurate; female vocals detailed and precisely centred in a wide soundstage.
- Bass satisfying and extended on all but the most bass-heavy tracks; found the 50 Hz (±3 dB) rating possibly conservative.
- Placement note: closer than about 18 inches to the front wall can over-emphasise bass; the foam bung may over-limit it.
- Compared with Studio Electric M4 (sealed) and Alta Audio Alyssa (deeper bass).

## Method notes
Listening only, on FS-700 S3 stands, about 4 ft from the front wall, slight toe-in. No measurements.

## Reliability notes
Single reviewer; publication date not captured; headline price $5,200 matches current B&W US price.
