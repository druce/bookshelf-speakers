# Audioholics: Hsu CCB-8 review (2017-08-07)

## Summary
Review with listening and measurements: 4.5/5 performance, 5/5 value. Audioholics praised imaging, dynamic range and the stable centre image off-axis, and measured a response within about ±3 dB to 10 kHz on axis, with a bright, spiky top end that is best avoided by listening 15-25° off axis. Measured impedance min 5 Ω, sensitivity 93.8 dB half-space.

## Method notes
Outdoor free-air at about 9 ft height, 1 m, gated at 14 ms: resolution lost below 140 Hz and invalid below 70 Hz; unsmoothed. This is gated quasi-anechoic (closer to Tier B in method); tier A is the packet classification. No full CTA-2034 derived numbers used from this source.

## Reliability notes
Single sample from the maker. Internal inconsistency on nominal impedance (8 Ω in spec box, 6 Ω in text). 2017 measurement; production may differ from the 2025 ASR sample.

## Key claims (Tier D)
- Outstanding imaging; rich, full soundstage; sounds the same at any distance.
- Above-average dynamic range; no distortion heard at high levels in movies.
- Direct-axis treble above 10 kHz is hot and could make sibilance if aimed straight at the listener.
- Cons listed: odd appearance, placement needs care; no cabinet bracing.
