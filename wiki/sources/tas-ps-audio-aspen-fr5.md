# The Absolute Sound: PS Audio Aspen FR5 (2025-11-04)

## Summary
A video-style write-up from TAS staff testing the FR5 in a large reference room and a small room. Reviewer praises the bass for the size, balanced presentation and imaging; the only limitation heard was the bottom octave. Recommends a decent-power amplifier given the low sensitivity.

## Key claims
- Bass: standout quality; reviewer reports "legitimately" getting 30 Hz bass in room (room gain, not anechoic).
- Tone: well balanced; clean, detailed highs that do not call attention; midrange clean and slightly warm.
- Imaging: excellent placement; stage large in depth, not much beyond speaker edges laterally.
- Dynamics: more dynamic than expected from a small box; can fill a large room without apparent strain.
- Price $3,500/pair; stands $500 extra; spec sheet echoed: 6 Ω nominal, 83.5 dB sensitivity, 35 Hz-20 kHz (-6 dB).

## Method notes
Two rooms; reference system plus roughly $12,000 smaller system. No measurements.

## Reliability notes
Subjective Tier D; unsigned staff byline.
