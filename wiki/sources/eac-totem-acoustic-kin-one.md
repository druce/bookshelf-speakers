# Erin's Audio Corner: Totem Kin One review (2024-12-12)

## Summary
Klippel NFS measurement of a loaned, broken-in pair, no grille. The speaker shows a mid-range dip and high-frequency roll-off per Erin's video summary, limited bass (f3 61 Hz), and an impedance that dips to 4.4 Ω above 80 Hz. Distortion rises to a few percent in the 2-3 kHz region at 96 dB.
