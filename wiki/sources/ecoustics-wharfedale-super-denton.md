# eCoustics: Wharfedale Super Denton (updated 2026-07-31)

## Summary
Strongly positive listening review (Editors' Choice, 10/10 on all scales) praising tonal richness, vocals and value; notes bass is not full range and the speaker likes more power.

## Key claims
- Rich, substantial, spacious sound with smooth top end; vocals natural and textured.
- Bass substantial for size but not full range; subwoofer helps with electronic music and larger rooms.
- Imaging spacious with good depth; character varies with amplifier.
- Scales with better amplification; reviewer prefers 100-200 W class A/B over 60 W.
- Cons: needs room from wall (rear ports), not the final word in speed or treble openness, stands needed.

## Method notes
Listening only; specs quoted from the maker (crossover 940 Hz / 4.6 kHz).
