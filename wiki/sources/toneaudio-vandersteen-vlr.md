# TONE Audio: Vandersteen VLR Wood (2015-11)

## Summary
Glowing subjective review, read from a reprint hosted by the manufacturer. Priced $1,275 per pair at the time. Contains no measurements.

## Key claims
- Jeff Dorgay described natural tonal balance, wide soundstage and strong driver integration, better than the Vandersteen 1Ci.
- Said it is easy to drive despite the 86 dB spec and compared favourably with the KEF LS50 for treble extension and coherence.
- Said it needs placement within about a foot of the rear wall for full bass; add a subwoofer if used well out into the room.
- Verdict: no small speaker more deserving; Product of the Year, small speakers.

## Method notes
Subjective, multiple amplifiers, mixed with comparison to the LS50.

## Reliability notes
Tier D. Hosted by the manufacturer (selection bias). Review is seven years older than the ASR measurement and says nothing about the 2.3 Ω impedance dip measured later; its "easy to drive" claim conflicts with [asr-vandersteen-vlr](asr-vandersteen-vlr.md).
