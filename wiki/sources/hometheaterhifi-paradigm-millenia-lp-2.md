# HomeTheaterHifi: Millenia LP XL and LP 2 (2015-03-02)

## Summary
Subjective, system-level review: LP 2 pair on the back wall with a Seismic 110 sub. No measurements of the speakers.

## Key claims
- Milton described dialog as natural and clear and the soundstage as well focused for a 2 in deep speaker, with a sub integrated (reviewer's claim).
- He relays that Paradigm designed the LP 2 and LP XL to work best directly on a wall and to avoid dramatic boundary gain (reviewer's paraphrase of maker; not on the maker page).
- He heard the speakers as better pointed straight ahead than at the listener when wall-mounted.
- Overall he would recommend them, and called them good for flat-panel setups without towers.
- Launch MSRP then: $499 each (LP 2).

## Method notes
Listening only, 2015, early production; sub and room-correction kit used throughout.

## Reliability notes
Does not mention the elevated treble and thin bass found by [asr-paradigm-millenia-lp-2](asr-paradigm-millenia-lp-2.md); sub-assisted, so not comparable on bass.
