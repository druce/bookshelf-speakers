# HomeTheaterHifi: Revel Performa 3 M105 (2013)

## Summary
Jim Clements ran in-room bench tests (1 m response, distortion) and listened. He found a flat response with good dispersion, very low distortion kept within limits (especially in the treble), a smooth natural balance, and bass that rolls off below about 60 Hz, so he recommends a subwoofer for large-orchestra material. Publication date inferred from image upload paths (September 2013); the page header date reflects a later site update.

## Key claims
- "Incredibly communicative" with smooth, natural frequency balance; non-fatiguing; handled the crossover region well.
- Bass rolls off below 60 Hz, limiting hall-size cues; recommend a sub for large orchestral works.
- Would put it on a short list even with a $3,000 budget.
