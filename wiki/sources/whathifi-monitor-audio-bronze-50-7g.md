# What Hi-Fi? review: Monitor Audio Bronze 50 7G (2025-09-25)

## Summary
Five stars on sound, build and compatibility (5/5/5). Tested at £500 / $849 with Naim Nait XS 3 and Arcam A5.

## Key claims (attributed)
- Unusually precise, controlled, clear, fast and detailed; front-footed, vibrant presentation.
- Large-scale, broad, spacious sound; deep, fast, grippy bass; clearer and more rhythmic than the B&W 607 S3.
- Plays happily at higher volumes; no amplifier compatibility issues.
- Slightly relentless, assertive character, occasionally "a little uptight"; Acoustic Energy AE300 Mk2 is more relaxed and fluid.
- Best placed on stands about 30 cm from the rear wall with ports unobstructed.

## Reliability notes
Single reviewer, medium room; the author name comes from the page's editor byline.
