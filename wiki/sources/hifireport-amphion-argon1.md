# HiFiReport: Amphion Argon 1 review

## Summary
Positive Taiwan listening review (images dated 2025-02; Taiwan price about US$2,227). Finds a fresh, clear, bright-but-not-harsh sound, wide soundstage in near-field equilateral placement, and easy drive from 35-100 W amplifiers.

## Key claims
- Clear, transparent foundation; bass not heavy; remains pleasant at high volume.
- Bright and open mid/highs with rich detail, not sharp.
- Foam port plugs useful near walls; should be kept off the wall for staging.
- 35 W amp was enough for most music but pushed too hard, the woofer bottoms out and the amplifier clips.

## Method notes
Listening only; specs quoted from the maker (45 Hz, 86 dB, 1.6 kHz crossover).

## Reliability notes
Single reviewer; date unknown; price is non-US.
