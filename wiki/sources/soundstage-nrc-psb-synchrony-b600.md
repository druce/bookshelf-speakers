# SoundStage!/NRC: PSB Synchrony B600 measurements (2021-11-15)

## Summary
Anechoic NRC measurements accompanying the SoundStage! review. Sensitivity 85.7 dB; impedance dips to roughly 3.3 Ω near 190 Hz (graph-read), well below the maker's "4 Ω minimum"; THD+N about 1.6-2.4% at 90 dB/2 m. No full spinorama published.

## Extracted values
| Field | Value | Note |
|---|---|---|
| sensitivity_db_2v83 | 85.7 dB | stated: LW average 300 Hz-3 kHz, 2.83 V/1 m |
| Listening window (5-point) | roughly 84-88 dB from 100 Hz to 20 kHz; port bump near 40 Hz at about 81 dB, dip to about 78 dB near 50-55 Hz | graph-read (approx.); no deviation figure stated |
| min impedance | about 3.3 Ω at about 190 Hz | graph-read (approx.); second dip about 4 Ω near 45 Hz |
| Impedance peaks | about 44 Ω near 31 Hz, above 50 Ω near 75 Hz, about 14 Ω near 800 Hz | graph-read (approx.) |
| THD+N @ 90 dB SPL, 2 m (about 96 dB at 1 m) | about 2.4% near 100 Hz; about 1.6% near 1.4 kHz; rises to about 10% at 50 Hz | graph-read (approx.); not exactly the 96 dB/1 m convention |

## Method notes
Microphone 12 in. up from cabinet bottom (PSB's listening axis), grille off, measured at 2 m and plotted at 1 m. LW is a 5-point average. 90 dB test at 9.0 V input.

## Reliability notes
Independent lab, single sample. Impedance and distortion values are read from small graphs.
