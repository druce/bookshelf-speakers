# What Hi-Fi? (Sound+Image): Polk R200 (2021-09-10)

## Summary
Australian Sound+Image review republished by What Hi-Fi?. Positive: powerful performance for the price; only con is unforgiving of edgy recordings. No star rating captured.

## Key claims
- Clarity and command, impressive bass punch, "incredible value".
- Reviewer preferred grilles off; magnetic grille audibly tempered the treble.
- Vocals strongly projected; midrange from X-Port unencumbered by boom; even response; noticed no distortion when turned up.
- Wide sweet spot but best imaging with the listener centred; works better with quality power.
- Claims bass output holds to ~150 Hz then 6 dB down near 70 Hz (maker-supplied/lab statement, not our measurement).

## Reliability notes
Sound United-adjacent distributor context (reviewer used Sound United amplifiers). Tier D.
