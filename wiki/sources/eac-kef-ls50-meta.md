# EAC: KEF LS50 Meta review (2023-12-21)

## Summary
Klippel NFS measurement of a loaned pair (Audio Advice). Erin found precise imaging and best balance about 15° off-axis, but limited low bass, minor upper-midrange issues, and audible distortion at 96 dB around 1-2 kHz.

## Method notes
Klippel NFS, reference point at tweeter (0°) plus 15° off-axis set. Multitone distortion also shown (increase at 96 dB, audible near 1-2 kHz per Erin). Pair-matching plot included (not read numerically).

## Reliability notes
Loaner, no compensation per Erin (affiliate link disclosed). Single pair.
