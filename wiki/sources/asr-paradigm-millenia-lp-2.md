# ASR forum: Paradigm Millenia LP 2 hobbyist measurements (2021-05-30)

## Summary
A forum member measured the LP 2 quasi-anechoically (6 ms gate, 1 m, UMIK-1/REW) for use as a center channel and found elevated output above the 2.6 kHz crossover, a bass-light response and poor horizontal directivity in the MTM orientation. His verdict: bright, needs EQ and a subwoofer.

## Method notes
Measured at 1 m with a roughly 6 ms gate (resolution to about 176 Hz); lower frequencies spliced from "hyper near-field" measurements with baffle-step adjustment. Speaker was not on a wall. Horizontal directivity was taken in the center-channel (horizontal MTM) orientation; vertical directivity was described as significantly better. A 5 dB-scale CTA-2034 plot is attached.

## Reliability notes
Single hobbyist sample, Tier B. Free-field measurement of an on-wall design: the LF and any boundary-related behavior do not represent wall placement. A later forum post noted the LP 2 was likely designed for wall mounting and asked whether free-standing data misrepresents it.
