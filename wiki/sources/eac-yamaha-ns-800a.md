# Erin's Audio Corner: Yamaha NS-800A (page dated Dec 9 2024; dataset 2025-01-14)

## Summary
Klippel NFS measurement of a loaner from Yamaha. Response is reasonably even (listening window ±2.2 dB) with wide, smooth directivity and low distortion; bass is limited (f3 57 Hz) and Erin heard bright treble that benefits from EQ. Impedance minimum 4.3 Ω, EPDR 2.1 Ω.

## Method notes
Klippel NFS, reference point at tweeter, no grille, broken-in sample. Also ran multitone distortion (images only, not read). Erin's subjective notes (Tier D): bright treble, weak bass unless near a wall, needs EQ to enhance mids and tame highs; good directivity makes EQ effective.

## Reliability notes
Single loaner sample. Measured price context: Erin quotes about 4,800 USD/pair MSRP. Distortion and compression values are graph-read.
