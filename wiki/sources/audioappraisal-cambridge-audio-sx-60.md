# Audio Appraisal: Cambridge Audio SX-60 (2022-02-14)

## Summary
UK independent site, loaner pair driven by a Cambridge AXA35 amp. Finds them confident, engaging, fun and well controlled, but not refined: edgy on bright recordings and some cabinet resonance.

## Key claims (attributed, paraphrased)
- Used with ~15 degrees toe-in, tweeters just below ear height, because treble can get edgy.
- Woofer nimble and well controlled; more bass with a stronger amp; slightly stiff woofers.
- Fatiguing with bright recordings; boxes resonate on cello and double bass notes.
- Better for rock, pop, hip-hop, electronic than classical; suggests a warmer amp.
- Quotes maker specs: 89 dB, 8 Ω nominal, 20-100 W, 41 Hz-22 kHz (claimed). UK price GBP 229/pair at review time.

## Method notes
Subjective; no measurements. Single partnering amp.

## Reliability notes
Independent hobbyist-style site; loaner from manufacturer.
