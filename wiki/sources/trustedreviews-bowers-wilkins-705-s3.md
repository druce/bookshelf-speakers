# Trusted Reviews: 705 S3 (2023-02-27)

## Key claims
- Verdict: tremendously capable, clean, expansive and detail-heavy; good build.
- Bass shaped impeccably and detailed; treble "pure in the manner of a bell"; mids very transparent.
- Slightly undemonstrative at low volume; wants gregarious amplification and is fussy about cool or analytical electronics.
- Stands (FS-700 S3) very expensive. Numerical score not captured in the raw text.
