# Bound for Sound: Role Audio Skiff

## Summary
Yun describes a surprisingly neutral, sprightly speaker with a strong midrange, excellent coherence, wide placement tolerance, and a smooth low-frequency roll-off suited to subwoofer pairing. Published issue 154 (date not given). Reproduced on the manufacturer's site, so selection may be favourable.

## Key claims (attributed)
- Midrange to lower treble "truly shines": crisp but silky, excellent articulation and resolution.
- Woodwinds and strings tonally very good; treble clean with shimmer; imaging similar to Role Windjammer and a little short of Merlin TSM.
- Dynamics somewhat compressed in absolute terms but contrasts preserved; handled orchestral material without breaking up.
- Limited bass; benign load; sub can be added with only a low-pass.
- Beams in the top octaves, so listen on-axis.

## Method notes
Listening only. Maker figures quoted: 8 Ω nominal (min 6.5 Ω), 88 dB/W/m, 125 Hz-20 kHz (-6 dB).
