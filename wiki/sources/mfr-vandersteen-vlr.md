# Manufacturer: Vandersteen VLR Wood

## Summary
Vandersteen's US page lists the VLR Wood as a current product: a compact coaxial (6.5" woofer with 1" dome tweeter) sealed monitor starting at $2,060 per pair (excluding duty, taxes, freight). The ASR-reviewed "VLR" is taken to be this wood-finish model (the VLR CT is a separate, higher-priced variant); this is an assumption from the ASR photos and finish.
